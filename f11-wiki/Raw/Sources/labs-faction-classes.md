# Labs Website — Faction Classes

An earlier website the author built for this game, holding older lore. Captured verbatim.

**Scope note from the author:** *"The attribute information doesn't matter to this game at
all, I'm only interested in the lore, what factions had what classes etc."* Attribute,
affinity, stat-level and ID data on these pages is therefore **deliberately not captured**.

**Provenance warning.** This is legacy material from an earlier iteration of the game. Treat
every claim below as the last written state, **not** as confirmed current design, exactly as
with the factions-retro prototype notes.

## Terminology (author, 2026-08-16)

> a class is a role of a type of character in a faction - for instance medic. A factionclass is
> the kind of charactor that fulfills a class for a faction. For example the autofix is the medic
> of the robot faction. The Minatour is the Heavy Weapons class of the Green skins.

## Character Classes (`/labs/characterclasses`)

Six, listed with no description beyond their own names:

- Commander
- Front Liner
- Heavy Weapons
- Infiltrator
- Medic
- Specialist

## Faction Classes (`/labs/factionclasses`)

Ten factions, each with six faction classes — one per character class.


### Institute of Eight

> Highly trained specialist operatives.

**Samurai** — *Commander*

> Followers of Bushido, the Way of the Warrior.
>
> Originally Japanese warriors who were a member of the feudal military aristocracy. Samurai are professional students of war and the martial skills.

**Mercenary** — *Front Liner*

> Highly trained military forces for hire.

**Sniper** — *Heavy Weapons*

> Pitch a tent and set up camp.
>
> This class is perfect for you if you are the type of scumbag that likes to camp 3000km away with a massive rifle.

**Ninja** — *Infiltrator*

> Sabotage, espionage, scouting, assassination.

**Kami** — *Medic*

> Animal spirits.
>
> The Kami are named after the Shinto gods of Japan who have spirit messengers often in the form of an animal. Also called Youkai or half-spirits, Kami can transform into an animal and have powers of healing and cleansing. Common forms are Fox, Snake, Cat, Badger, Dog and Spider.

**Secret Agent** — *Specialist*

> The suave, sophisticated, gadgets and guns kind.


### Robots

> Self replicating artificial life.

**Tactical Cortex** — *Commander*

> Specialist small unit tactics and strategy entity.
>
> Armed with liquid logic algorithms and pre-loaded decision trees, the Tactical Cortex provides strategy planning and decision making. Specifically design for small unit tactics.
> As the tactical cortex is often relied upon to perform negotiation duties, unlike much of the Robot faction they have been loaded with algorithms that represent ethical considerations observed in the behavior of other faction members. This has given the tactical cortex units uncommon awareness of right and wrong.

**Battlebot** — *Front Liner*

> All purpose killing machine.
>
> Battlebots double as scouts in the Robot Ranks. They have special curiosity routines for this purpose. Battlebots also have a modified logistics chip that will give weight to the feasibility of their survival when following the commands of their superiors, providing the bot with unwarranted optimism.

**MWP Killbot** — *Heavy Weapons*

> Basically a walking Bazooka
>
> Mobile Weapons Platform - designed with genocide in mind.

**Android** — *Infiltrator*

> Artifical imitation of life.
>
> Androids have special active Chameleon polymers used in the construction of their ectodermal layer. This allows the android to change thier physical appearance for camoflague and infiltration purposes. Androids are designed to observe and assimilate into the target enviornment, imitating the behaivor of the group they attach themselves to.

**Autofix** — *Medic*

> Nanotech enabled service droids.
>
> Autofix robots are equiped with nanite factories known as hives that generate short-lived nanopaste to repair damaged units. Autofixes can also produce energy generating nanites to re-power the cores of of over-exerted comrades.
> Autofix bots suppliment tactical cortex units, providing localized analysis as they collate reports from combatant units. Autofix bots convey and in some cases make tactical decisions which they in-turn convey to repaired units for relay to active locations.

**Raptorbot** — *Specialist*

> Flying death machines.
>
> All raptorbots are installed with extensive skill matrices and decision processing. Given appropriate preconditions these capabilities allow raptorbots to take matters into their own hands.
> Raptorbots make use of poorly understood quantum probability module to monitor local possible events of interest. This gives the raptorbot insight into critical spacetime junctures, providing the raptorbot an opportunity to be in the right place at the right time.


### Celestials

> Righteous warriors on a militant pilgrim.

**Templar** — *Commander*

> Knights of a religious military order.
>
> A knight of a religious military order established in 1118 to protect pilgrims and the Holy Sepulcher.

**Knight** — *Front Liner*

> Protect the weak, defenseless, helpless, and fight for the general welfare of all.
>
> Originating from the middle ages, a Knight is a social position. Knighthood is a non-inheritable form of gentry. Knights live to a chivalric code. Depending on the Order the Knight belongs to this code is a set of ideals and beliefs the Knight should abide by and promote.

**Monk** — *Heavy Weapons*

> Peace on the inside, chaos on the outside.
>
> Monks of the Crusader faction pursue enlightenment by mastering their physical and mental state.

**Reaper** — *Infiltrator*

> Death Angels
>
> Reapers are Angels tasked with the responsibility of herding the recently dead to their ultimate destination.

**Angel** — *Medic*

> Winged Deliverers of Mercy.

**Exorcist** — *Specialist*

> Bane of Demons.

### Pirates

> Rum loving scags of the sea.

**Captain** — *Commander*

> Master of a crew. Arg.
>
> Shiver me Timbers! Pirate captains are a mix of leadership and strategy. The title of captain is a heavily contested station that a pirate crew allocate based on merit. Pirates think on their feet and act quickly on their decisions. In fact they act on decisions a little before they make them!

**Swashbuckler** — *Front Liner*

> Staple of the fleets.

**Cannoneer** — *Heavy Weapons*

> They like things that go BOOM!

**Quatermaster** — *Infiltrator*

> Boarding party leaders.
>
> It was often the quartermaster's responsibility to lead the pirate boarding party when coming aboard another ship. This was usually done from the quarter deck which was the place where two ships touched during the boarding attack.

**Jester** — *Medic*

> A saw in one hand, a sword in the other.
>
> A Jester is a rank unique to the Pirate faction. After the pirates were unified by Boris of South Shore, a few of the ships medics began to meet and trade knowledge. This group grew to become the Jesters, a loose collection of apothecary, chirugen and barber surgeons. In combat, Jesters specialise in poisons and precision hits.

**First Mate** — *Specialist*

> Second in command to the Captain.

**Zombie** — *Front Liner*

### Green Skins

> The old tribes of the earth.

**Orc** — *Commander*

> Masters of the tribes.
>
> Orcs are a warlike humanoid race whose above-average intelligence lends the orcs to a leadership role. This is somewhat due to the reluctance of other members of the Green Skins factions to make decisions. Before the Orcs the Green Skin tribes were subjugated by the other factions, used as manual labor and cannonfodder.
> The Orcs bring purpose and co-ordination to the Green Skins. Without them the faction would splinter.

**Giant** — *Front Liner*

> Regal mountains.

**Minotaur** — *Heavy Weapons*

> Part man, part bull, all beef.
>
> In greek mythology the offspring from the coupling of a bull and a human woman. The original Minotaur dwelt at the center of the Labyrinth built for King Minos of Crete and designed by the architect Daedalus and his son Icarus who were ordered to build it to hold the Minotaur. The historical site of Knossos is usually identified as the site of the labyrinth. The Minotaur was eventually killed by Theseus.

**Gargoyle** — *Infiltrator*

> Bat-winged and Stone Skinned.
>
> Gargoyles can move through stone and fly. When their wings are folded away they can make themselves appear to be flesh and bone.

**Shaman** — *Medic*

> If they can't fix you, they've got your afterlife covered.

**Troll** — *Specialist*

> Do not feed the trolls.
>
> Immensly amused by sockpuppets.


### Aliens

> Unknowable intelligence, unfathomable motives.

**Grey** — *Commander*

> Ruling species of the Alien Alliance
>
> The greys are an old race who left physical pursuits behind long ago for more esoteric goals. A rare trait for Alien faction members, the Greys are able to evaluate the motivations of the unpredictable terrans.

**Thunder Lizard** — *Front Liner*

> Reptilian dinosaur-like humanoids.
>
> Thunder Lizards are from a planet where the reptiles evolved to be the dominant species. There are many species of Thunder Lizard.

**Tripod** — *Heavy Weapons*

> Installed with a death ray.

**Martian** — *Infiltrator*

> Little Green Men from the Red Planet.
>
> Martians are native to Mars but have been living in secret among Earthlings for centuries. Minor species from the lesser-knwon inhabited planets of Sol work under the Martians, including the Venusians, Jovians, and Borts from the Oort cloud and Kuiper belt.

**Squidling** — *Medic*

> Cute Squiddish Aliens.
>
> They have tentacles that can both heal and hurt... anatomical experts due to their experimentation on other species, if you were ever probed by an alien, one of these guys did it.

**Feline** — *Specialist*

> A race of alien humanoid cats.
