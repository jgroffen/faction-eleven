# Chapter 2 — The Signals

Where the premise starts to pay. Three protagonists converge on **one object** —
[[the-monastery-autofix-bot]] — and none of them knows the others are looking. [[templar]] holds
it, [[autofix]] hears it, and [[ninja]] and [[gargoyle]] hunt it.

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** _not stated — see Open_

## Story

1. **[[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]** — [[templar]] loses to the war machine a second time and
   retreats to [[templar-monastery]], chosen because they believe the Celestials need **ancient
   allies**. Exploring its ancient parts between battles, they find and use [[the-wand]] and
   [[the-monastery-autofix-bot]]. The [[mystics]] and the [[robots]] hear.
2. **[[the-second-signal]]** — the awakened shrine bot detects a *second* mini-fix signal, unlike
   the one through the cracked gate. It leads [[ninja]] to [[the-ancient-ruin]] and its abandoned,
   already-cracked gate into [[realm-09]] — and to [[gargoyle]], who is **very ancient** and wants
   to stop his own faction's war on the Celestials. They set out together to find the bot.

## Content Load

Substantially heavier than chapter one, on every axis:

- **Five factions, against chapter one's three.**
- **Two entirely new game styles** — [[mystics]] and [[green-skins]] — neither of which is
  designed. See [[faction-design-status]].
- **Two new realms** at meaningful scope: [[realm-04]] as a *major setting*, and [[realm-09]].
- New locations: [[templar-monastery]] with its ancient parts, [[the-ancient-ruin]].
- The first cross-realm **travel** by a protagonist, rather than signalling.

**This is the balance problem the chapter notes exist to make visible.** Chapter one is three
factions with three settled styles; chapter two is five factions with three settled and two
unwritten, plus a major new realm. Unless chapter two is rescoped or the two styles are designed
early, it is a much bigger build than the chapter that has to fund it.

## Continuity

**Depends on chapter one:**

- [[the-cracked-gate]] and the awakened shrine bot — without them there is no second signal to
  detect.
- [[autofix]]'s remote communication — without it the monastery bot calls and nobody hears.
- The Templar's retreat to [[templar-monastery]].

**Sets up:** the Ninja and Gargoyle's search for the bot, unresolved at chapter end; the
[[mystics]]' response to [[the-wand]]; the Celestials' war machine problem, still unsolved.

## Open

- **No lead faction is stated.** Chapter one's structure was "one faction carries the plot, others
  are reduced" ([[chapter-based-release]]). Chapter two has two major factions ([[celestials]],
  [[institute-of-eight]]) and a major setting belonging to a third. Whether the lead/reduced model
  still applies, or chapter two is deliberately an ensemble, is undecided — and it changes the
  build enormously.
- Do the Ninja and Gargoyle **reach** the Templar in this chapter, or is the convergence chapter
  three's payoff?
- The route from [[realm-09]] to [[realm-03]] is unexplained — see [[the-second-signal]].
- The [[mystics]] "receive" the wand's call but what they *do* is unwritten, despite their realm
  being a major setting.
