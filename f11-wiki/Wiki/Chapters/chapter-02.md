# Chapter 2 — The Signals

Where the premise starts to pay. Three protagonists move toward **one machine** — [[odie]] — and
none of them knows the others are looking. [[templar]] holds him, [[autofix]] detects him, and
[[ninja]] and [[gargoyle]] set out after him.

**Restructured 2026-08-12 (provisional):** the chapter now **focuses on [[gargoyle]]'s gameplay**.
The [[mystics]] and the four-way arrival at the Templar moved out to [[chapter-03]].

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** [[green-skins]] — the author's phrasing was "make Chapter 2
focus on the Gargoyle gameplay"

## Story

1. **[[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]** — [[templar]] loses to the war machine a second time and
   retreats to [[templar-monastery]], chosen because they believe the Celestials need **ancient
   allies**. Exploring its ancient parts between battles, they find and use [[the-wand]] and
   [[odie]]. The [[mystics]] and the [[robots]] hear.
2. **[[the-second-signal]]** — [[mifix]] detects a *second* mini-fix signal, unlike the one through
   the guardian's cracked gate. It has routed from [[realm-03]] through the open
   [[celestials]]/[[green-skins]] gate and the cracked one in [[the-ancient-ruin]]. He *detects* it — too far to
   communicate with — only once beat 1 has happened **and** chapter one's two conditions are met — the crack, and
   [[autofix]]'s Quantum Comms. Mifix and the Ninja pursue it as **an alternative way to reach the
   Robot Realm**, the guarded gate being cracked but still impassable. It leads [[ninja]] to [[the-ancient-ruin]] and its abandoned,
   already-cracked gate into [[realm-09]] — and to [[gargoyle]], who is **very ancient** and wants
   to stop his own faction's war on the Celestials. They set out together to find the bot.

## Content Load

Substantially heavier than chapter one, on every axis:

- **Five factions, against chapter one's three.**
- **Two entirely new game styles** — [[mystics]] and [[green-skins]] — neither of which is
  designed. See [[faction-design-status]].
- **Two new realms** at meaningful scope: [[realm-04]] as a *major setting*, and [[realm-09]].
- New locations: [[templar-monastery]] with its ancient parts, [[the-ancient-ruin]].
- The first cross-realm **travel** by a protagonist, rather than signalling.

**The restructure fixes most of the balance problem** the chapter notes were written to expose.
Moving the Mystics to [[chapter-03]] takes chapter two from five factions and two undesigned styles
down to four factions and one — and that one, the [[green-skins]], is now the chapter's *lead*
rather than a walk-on.

**The remaining risk is concentrated rather than removed:** chapter two's headline faction has **no
game style at all**. Designing [[gargoyle]]'s gameplay is now the most urgent open item in the wiki
after the story outline. See [[faction-design-status]].

## Continuity

**Depends on chapter one:**

- [[the-cracked-gate]] — without it [[mifix]] hears nothing at all, by either route.
- [[autofix]]'s **Quantum Comms** — without it the monastery bot calls and nobody hears.
- The Templar's retreat to [[templar-monastery]].

Beat 2 is gated on **three protagonists across two chapters** — the crack (Ninja, ch1), Quantum
Comms (Autofix, ch1) and the Templar activating his signals (ch2). It is the most demanding gating
chain in the game so far, and the clearest argument for tracking beats per chapter.

**Sets up:** [[chapter-03]] — the Ninja and Gargoyle's search unresolved at chapter end, the
[[mystics]]' response to [[the-wand]] still to come, and the Celestials' war machine problem
unsolved.
