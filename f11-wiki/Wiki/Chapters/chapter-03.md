# Chapter 3 — The Trial

Two protagonists who have never met walk toward the same scene from opposite directions.
[[gargoyle-granite]] and [[ninja-kazuma]] hunt the monastery bot together; [[wraith-delahaye]]
searches a frozen kingdom for allies a thousand years dead and follows the only lead she finds —
[[val]] — out of her realm. The chapter ends on **one scene watched twice**: Val's trial of the
Gargoyle, played in the room as Granite and, unseen, from behind Val's shoulder as Delahaye.

**Status:** outline · **Lead factions:** [[green-skins]] and [[the-damned]], jointly — **the
Gargoyle by gameplay, the Wraith by story**

## Story

**The Gargoyle and the Ninja.**

1. **The arrival.** [[ninja-kazuma]] and [[mifix]] come through the abandoned, already-cracked gate
   in [[the-ancient-ruin]] into [[realm-07]] — the second half of [[the-second-signal]] — and meet
   [[gargoyle-granite]], who is very ancient and wants to stop his own faction's war on the
   Celestials. They set out together to find the bot.
2. **The search.** Ninja and Gargoyle work [[realm-07]] together toward the faint signal — the
   Gargoyle's Metroidvania played twice, by its own protagonist and by a visitor under
   [[realm-governs-game-style]]. The Gargoyle recovers the shape of the gate network from a
   [[gargoyle-fragments|fragment]] along the way.

**The Wraith.**

3. **[[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]** — Delahaye searches the frozen world map for her allies and
   learns, object by object, that much time has passed; rescue becomes revenge. Investigation and
   infiltration reveal [[the-guardians-of-night]] working to reopen gates, and lead her to
   [[val-s-castle]] — just as Val arrives, and leaves for the green-skins' realm. Delahaye follows
   her through the gate Val herself opened.

**The trial.**

4. **[[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]]** — the chapter's ending. Val, now aware a Gargoyle
   survived, hunts him down herself, fails to talk him into believing he's malfunctioning, and sets
   the Minotaur on him. Kazuma and Mifix are with him. He survives on defence alone and cannot let
   the doubt show. **Delahaye watches all of it, and nobody knows she is there.** She picks the
   Ninja out of the room as her road home ([[the-shadowing-of-the-ninja]]).
5. **[[the-unfreezing-of-the-kingdom]]** — Val returns to her castle, notices the realm is frozen,
   and [[the-retcon-engine]] rewrites her noticing and unfreezes the game. The player's kingdom
   resumes. The Damned's thread closes with the RTS handed back.

## Content Load

Lighter than chapter two, on purpose — the chapter spends what chapter two built:

- **No new game style.** The frozen map is played with the wraith puzzle's verbs; the RTS does not
  run until the last scene.
- **No new realm.** [[realm-07]] is played twice; [[realm-08]] is the map the player already owns,
  now walked on foot.
- New locations: [[val-s-castle]] (exterior, a signpost); wherever the trial is staged.
- **The first cross-realm travel by two protagonists**: the Ninja into realm-07 by the ruin gate,
  Delahaye into realm-07 by the gate Val opened ([[sealed-interplane-gates]]).
- **One scene built for two viewpoints.** The trial as a Metroidvania boss encounter for Granite
  and as an observed cutscene for Delahaye.

## Continuity

Tracked across all chapters in [[story-continuity-timeline]].

**Depends on chapter two:** the barrier known to be broken from inside; the Ninja's departure; the
freeze.

**Sets up [[chapter-04]]:** the Gargoyle's doubt ([[the-diagnosis-of-the-last-gargoyle]]); Val's
assassins ([[the-ambush-on-the-road]]); Delahaye on the Ninja's trail; and a kingdom back in play
with its would-be wraith gone.

**Glitches this chapter:** the unfreeze — [[the-retcon-engine]] editing a named antagonist's memory
on screen ([[continuity-glitches]]).

## Open

- **The chapter's shape is provisional.**
- **Whether the Gargoyle's thread has enough of its own** between the arrival and the trial, or
  whether the search needs a beat of its own — a quest note for the Ninja–Gargoyle search does not
  exist.
- How Delahaye learns Val's destination at the castle, and whether she enters it.
- Whether the player controls the kingdom at all before the unfreeze, or only Delahaye.
- Whether the freeze is felt as a loss by the player (a genre they enjoyed gone for a chapter) or
  as a mystery — and whether the kingdom's map should show anything changing while frozen.
- Where in [[realm-07]] the trial is staged, and whether Delahaye's vantage is a place the player
  chooses.

## What Ends The Chapter

**Settled:** [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]], seen twice. It is the chapter-scale revelation with
a named antagonist — and the first time two of the player's protagonists are in one scene without
either knowing it. The coda is [[the-unfreezing-of-the-kingdom]]: Val goes home, the engine tidies
up after Delahaye, and the player gets their kingdom back not knowing what it cost.
