# Chapter 5 — The Machine

The war machine reaches [[templar-monastery]] and six protagonists from six realms fight it
together. Then, still standing in the wreckage, they hold council: [[templar-will]] names them
**[[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]]** and they decide where to go next
([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]).

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** [[celestials]]

## Why This Chapter Exists

Chapter four spent itself on arrivals — a whole new faction, a whole new realm, and four travellers
crossing into a fifth. The machine cresting the hill is where that chapter stops, which leaves the
battle and the council to be the spine of this one. **The council is better here than there**: the
Templar names the room having just watched it win, rather than on the strength of a handshake.

## Story

1. **The battle.** The machine the [[celestials]] have lost to twice arrives at a monastery that
   now holds [[conjurer-voisin]], [[ninja-kazuma]], [[mifix]], [[gargoyle-granite]] and
   [[wraith-delahaye]] alongside [[templar-will]] and [[knight-ermengarda]]. Played as
   [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]] under [[realm-governs-game-style]], with the visitors as
   **hero units** — which is what that mechanic was built to do.
2. **[[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]** — the council. The Templar names the room; each
   party says what it brings; nobody knows where the gate to the [[robots]] is; they settle on
   finding a [[werebeasts|Werebeast]] gargoyle, for four reasons, one per party. Delahaye asks why
   she should go the wrong way round the wheel and the Gargoyle answers her.
3. **The road out.** The party leaves for [[realm-09]]. **Not designed** — see Open.

## Content Load

- **The war machine as a boss**, which the tower defence has promised since chapter one and never
  delivered.
- **Six hero units in one defence**, five of them imported from other genres. This is the
  presentation problem chapter four used to carry, moved here and made concrete: it is now a
  *balance* problem in a known system rather than an open question about scene direction.
- The council as a scene with six speaking parts.
- Whatever the journey to [[realm-09]] turns out to be.

  Templar eventually taking one, upgrading it and turning it on its owners, and

  This is the battle where that becomes available.
- Whether the council is one scene or several, and whether the player plays any of it.
- How the party travels from the monastery to [[realm-09]] — the Celestials' gate to the Werebeasts
  stands open ([[celestials]]), a road the enemy is using in the other direction.
