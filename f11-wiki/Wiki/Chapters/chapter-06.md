# Chapter 6 — The Parting

The [[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] arrive in [[realm-09]], meet the [[werebeasts]], find out
what was done to realm-09's gargoyles, and cannot agree what to do about it. The chapter ends with the
alliance **splitting in two** — and the player plays both halves from here on.

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** [[werebeasts]]

## Why This Chapter Exists

Chapter five got the champions into [[realm-09]] with one shared goal: find an intact gargoyle. This is
the chapter where that goal **fails on its own terms** — the gargoyles are there and they cannot be
woken — and the party discovers it wants four different things. The naming at the monastery made them an
alliance; this is where being an alliance costs something.

## Story

1. **They meet [[werewolf-gill]].** The [[werebeasts]]' protagonist, and the sixth to join the
   champions. **Undesigned** — the Werebeasts have no story and no confirmed game style.
2. **The gargoyles are in stasis.** Realm-09's guardians are the only intact set in the game and they
   will not wake ([[the-realm-barriers]]). Somebody walked past their barrier without breaking it and
   put them under — [[the-graceful-servants]], to clear the way for the harvest
   ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). The champions came for a gargoyle who could answer questions
   and found a room full of sleepers.
3. **The council argues.** Four goals, one party:
   - Some want to move against [[val]] and [[the-guardians-of-night]].
   - Some want to **investigate the Servants** — what the stockpile is for, and whether the stasis can be
     reversed.
   - [[ninja-kazuma]] and [[mifix]] still want the [[robots]].
   - [[wraith-delahaye]] still wants the [[pirates]].
4. **They agree that Val waits**, and split.
5. **The Ninja's party turns toward the [[aliens]].** Kazuma carries [[the-clay-jar]] — the alien egg
   from his family shrine — which calls the [[aliens]] ([[allied-faction-signals]]). If they can find the
   gate, **he can open it** ([[the-red-power]]). Delahaye agrees to this road because **the Alien realm
   has a path to the Pirate realm** ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]). Where that goes is
   [[chapter-07]].
6. **The Gargoyle's party stays on the gargoyles.** [[gargoyle-granite]] leaves with
   [[conjurer-voisin]] and [[werewolf-gill]] to work towards **freeing realm-09's guardians** — and once
   they are free, Granite means to put the question to them: is working against
   [[the-guardians-of-night]] what gargoyles should do?

**The chapter ends on the parting.** The [[aliens]]' realm and the Glitch Liches are
**[[chapter-07]]**.

## Content Load

- **[[realm-09]] and the [[werebeasts]]' game style**, which does not exist. One realm, as
  [[chapter-04]] carried one.
- **A protagonist recruitment scene** for [[werewolf-gill]], who has no character written.
- **The party split**, which doubles the number of active threads the player is switching between. **The
  player does not choose it and plays both parties** — so this is where
  [[protagonist-swapping-and-story-gating]] stops being optional.

## Continuity

Tracked across all chapters in [[story-continuity-timeline]].

**Depends on [[chapter-05]]:** the champions in [[realm-09]], the Servants identified, and
[[templar-will]] absent with the war machine.

**Sets up [[chapter-07]]:** the Ninja's party opening a gate into [[realm-10]] and finding the Glitch
Liches. Further out: the freeing of realm-09's gargoyles and Granite's question to them; the
investigation of the Servants' stockpile; and Delahaye's arrival in the Pirate realm, where
[[captain-d-artigue]] is.

## Open

- **Everything about [[realm-09]]** — story, style, content. The [[werebeasts]] need a session of their
  own before this chapter can firm up, and so does [[werewolf-gill]], who has a recruitment scene and no
  character.
- Whether anything in this chapter reaches [[templar-will]], who is a realm away driving a captured
  siege engine.
- Whether the stasis can be reversed at all, and who knows how.
- How the player moves between the two parties once they exist.
