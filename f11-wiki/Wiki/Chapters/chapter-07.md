# Chapter 7 — Outside The World

[[ninja-kazuma]]'s party finds the gate to the [[aliens]], opens it, and meets the only inhabitants of
any realm who have stood **outside their own world and come back**: the liches of [[the-damned]], out in
[[the-void]].

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** [[aliens]]

## Why This Chapter Exists

The split at the end of [[chapter-06]] gave the player two parties. This chapter is the Ninja's, and it
exists because of a decision made in chapter one: a family kept two ancient devices in a shrine, and the
Ninja walked out with both ([[the-clay-jar]], [[the-second-signal]]). The second one finally does
something.

It is also where the player's understanding of what the game *is* takes its largest single step — not
because anything is explained, but because they meet characters who explain it wrongly and
convincingly.

## Story

1. **Find the gate.** Kazuma carries the alien egg, which **calls the [[aliens]]**
   ([[allied-faction-signals]]). How the jar leads them to a gate — whether it must be activated or only
   carried — is not designed.
2. **Open it.** The Ninja can breach a sealed interplane gate ([[the-red-power]]); it is why
   [[wraith-delahaye]] stayed with him ([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]).
3. **[[realm-10]] and its gameplay** — the [[aliens]]' realm, played for the first time.
   **Undesigned.**
4. **The Glitch Liches.** They see the machinery the world runs on, understand it, and present it as
   **ancient eldritch lore** — a costume rather than a lie, which makes them the game's most dangerous
   exposition ([[the-void]], [[continuity-glitches]]). *Liches cause glitches.*

## Content Load

- **[[realm-10]] and the [[aliens]]' game style**, which does not exist.
- **The Glitch Liches as characters** — the first time anyone in the fiction describes the game's real
  machinery out loud.
- **Two parties running in parallel** for the first time, with the player playing both.
