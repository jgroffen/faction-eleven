# Chapter 8 — The Fey Gate

[[gargoyle-granite]]'s party follows a hunter through a gate she should never have been able to open,
into the realm of the [[fey-folk]], and arrives in time to save a Phoenix.

**Status:** outline · **Lead faction:** [[fey-folk]]

## Why This Chapter Exists

The split at the end of [[chapter-06]] gave the player two parties. [[chapter-07]] was the Ninja's.
This one is the Gargoyle's, and it is the one where the old war could start again: a werebeast is
hunting in the Fey realm, and [[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] began with exactly that.

## Story

1. **The Fey Folk's own game, first.** [[phoenix-ember]] and the Fey Beasts, the empty lair, the
   ambush, and the Phoenix surviving and driving [[weresnake-taz]]'s pack back through the 09↔05 gate.
   He stays to guard it ([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]], [[fey-ley-cleansing]]).
2. **In [[realm-09]], the Gargoyle's party hears of Taz** and learns she has [[the-red-power]].
3. **Gill and Voisin argue.** [[werewolf-gill]] knows Taz will hunt Fey Beasts if she can get through
   the gate, and he would too if he could. [[conjurer-voisin]] says werebeasts hunting Fey Beasts is as
   bad as the Mystics hunting werebeasts.
4. **Taz plans how to beat the Phoenix** and goes back with a new group: high damage output, and a fast
   member to grab his ashes before he can be reborn.
5. **The party decides to stop her.** They will capture Taz, or otherwise stop her using the Red Power.
   [[gargoyle-granite]] and Voisin want to make sure the old war isn't restarted, and the best way to
   do that is to **close the gates**.
6. **They follow close behind Taz's new group through 09↔05** and join the battle to **rescue the
   Phoenix**. Taz gets away. She sees the rescue as the theft of a trophy she won fairly, and once
   she regroups she hunts the champions.
7. **Ember joins [[the-realm-champions]]** because they saved him. Without that he would never trust a
   group that has a werebeast in it. He wants the gate closed and Taz stopped, and he and
   [[werewolf-gill]] start out uneasy.
8. **Closing the gate is a question for [[realm-09]]'s gargoyles.** The barrier lets werebeasts pass,
   so the gate can only be shut if the gargoyles agree. Once they are freed, Granite will ask them
   whether they should still let a werebeast through when she is restarting the old war.

## How It Plays

**The player switches between parties, and their games, as in any chapter.** The Fey game is one
of them. **The Gargoyle's party is blocked in realm-09** until Taz has failed to beat the Phoenix and
he is guarding the gate, so that thread waits on the Fey game.

**The Fey game's zones 1 and 2 are already open from [[chapter-06]].** Zone 3 onward, with the empty
lair and the ambush, opens here.

## Content Load

- **[[realm-05]] and [[fey-ley-cleansing]]**: six zones, the Fey Beasts' stats and skills, and the
  zone 3–4 turn. Realm-05 is mostly a place to pass through and a stage for story events, not a
  second home for the champions' gameplay.
- **Taz as an on-screen antagonist**, met for the first time.
- **A rescue battle** in a realm with no combat mechanic.
