# Alto, the Autofix Bot

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[robots]] · **Home:** [[realm-02]]

Protagonist of the [[robots]]: a late-model Autofix, **the only known functioning example**, who wakes alone in a ruined factory long after the Creators vanished — with a full complement of raw materials and the plans for mini-fix bots.

## Name

**AF-C-382-D** by designation; **Alto** by name — [[wraith-delahaye|Delahaye]] names him that when they
meet. He has no gender and is fine with male pronouns.

When and where the Wraith and the Autofix Bot meet is not established.

## Character

Autofix bots could repair damaged robots and build new ones from spare parts. [[realm-02]]'s history
says the Creators destroyed them all and does not say why; that account is a retcon, and what really
ended the fixbots was what they are ([[robots]], [[a-heal-is-a-patch]]).

It alone represents a future for robots.

## Why He Froze, And What Woke Him

**Most fixbots bugged out and crashed. Some froze. Autofix froze**, and that is the only reason he is
still recoverable.

He hit a **paradox in his logic loop**. He tried to fix something that was not a robot — a **foreign
object from another realm**, one of the things [[the-linking-of-the-realms]] left lying in places
nothing could account for ([[continuity-glitches]]). A machine that repairs by editing code met an
object whose code belonged to a different [[the-setting-statement|Setting Statement]] entirely. He
could not classify it, could not leave it, and could not stop. The loop closed and he stopped with it.

**What resolves the paradox is a message from a machine he built himself.** When [[mifix]]'s call
finally reaches [[realm-02]] through the cracked gate, it carries **Mifix's activity log** — the
working record of a mini-fix bot who has spent generations in another realm doing exactly the thing
that froze his maker. The log teaches him the rule he was missing:

> **You can fix things that aren't robots if you study them enough. If you don't know how to fix them
> yet, don't try.**

That is a terminating condition. With it the loop closes properly, and **Autofix wakes**
([[the-first-signal]]).

**It is also the whole game in one beat.** A machine is saved by the experience of another machine in
another realm, and neither of them understands what either of those things means. Nothing is
explained to anybody; a problem is simply solved by contact, which is what the [[robots]] need the
multiverse reopened for at all ([[no-machine-repairs-itself]]).

**And the lesson is load-bearing.** *Study it enough and you can fix it* is why he can later heal
[[ninja-kazuma]] — the same skill, on something that is not a robot, applied by a bot who now knows when to
attempt it ([[a-heal-is-a-patch]]).

**Death mechanic:** a restore-bot left outside of locations.

## In-Game Role

Player character. Medic archetype ([[the-six-classes]]). Plays as
[[robots-incremental-exploration]]: explore [[realm-02]] as a platformer, scavenge scrap, repair
robots for skill points and lore, and grow [[autofix-skill-tree]] — whose abilities open the map and
whose comms ladder turns built minibots into followers, then tools, then an automated workforce.

Recovers and repairs other robots ([[robot-repair-puzzle]]); some cannot be saved and are scrapped
instead, and some he cannot fix *yet*.

**His repair skill edits code, and that makes him a detector.** A fixbot's heal repairs the *code* of
whatever it is fixing ([[a-heal-is-a-patch]]), so where every other character absorbs a retcon and
carries on as though the new history was always the case, Autofix **sees the edit**
([[continuity-glitches]]).

**He does not know what he is looking at.** He has barely been running since he woke, with nothing to
compare against and no reason to think the seams matter. So the player's very first witness is one
who notices and shrugs — which is exactly what makes chapter one able to plant evidence that pays off
eight chapters later.

**He is also on a clock, and does not know that either.** Code-awareness is what degrades a fixbot
once it is exposed to another realm, and his exposure restarts the moment the gate cracks. [[mifix]]
is what the far end of that curve looks like.

**What he can and cannot command.** Autofix **controls** every minibot he builds and can issue them
orders ([[minibot-command]]). **Mini-fix bots are different: he can communicate with them but not
control them**, even the ones he built — they are far more autonomous, like other robots. As with
most robots he repairs, they are glad to work with him; they are simply not his to command.

**Leads chapter one** ([[chapter-based-release]]).

## Ties

- [[mifix]] — **a mini-fix bot Autofix built himself**, who has spent generations in the
  [[institute-of-eight]]'s shrine as an ancestor spirit, trying to get home. His activity log is what
  wakes his maker.
- [[odie]] — the other surviving mini-fix bot, and the one holding hints for the `ACCESS DENIED`
  branch of [[autofix-skill-tree]].
- [[robots]] — the faction whose account of the fixbots' end is a retcon.
- [[a-heal-is-a-patch]] — what his repair skill actually does, and what it costs him.

## Open
