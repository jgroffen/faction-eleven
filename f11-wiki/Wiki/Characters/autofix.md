# Autofix

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[robots]] · **Home:** [[realm-02]]

Protagonist of the [[robots]]: a late-model Autofix, **the only known functioning example**, who wakes alone in a ruined factory decades after the creators vanished — with a full complement of raw materials and the plans for mini-fix bots.

## Character

Autofix bots could repair damaged robots and build new ones from spare parts. The creators, fearful of losing control, **destroyed them all**. That Autofix exists at all is the story's central mystery: how did it survive the purge, why was it shut down, and how did it awaken?

It alone represents a future for robots.

**Death mechanic:** a restore-bot left outside of locations.

## In-Game Role

Player character. Medic archetype ([[the-six-classes]]). Plays as
[[robots-incremental-exploration]]: explore [[realm-02]] as a platformer, collect Parts, repair
robots, and unlock an incremental skill tree whose abilities — wall-crawling and the like — are
what open the map.

Recovers and repairs other robots; some will not join, but give information or materials instead.

**Leads chapter one** ([[chapter-based-release]]).
