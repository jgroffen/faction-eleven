# Captain Anne

**Captain of [[the-drunken-mermaid]]**, whose First Mate was [[wraith-delahaye]] — and **Delahaye's
romantic partner.** Her ship and crew **controlled [[tortuga]]**, the island Delahaye calls home.
Female.

**Role:** npc · **Faction:** [[pirates]] · **Home:** [[tortuga]]

## Character

Not yet described beyond her place in Delahaye's life: her captain, her partner, and the holder of
her home. She is one of the memories the [[wraith-memory-puzzle]] returns, and when Delahaye breaks
out of the Damned's game meaning to rescue her allies, Anne is the ally the word means first.

**Her fate is not established.** She was in the armada [[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]] betrayed
([[the-pirate-institute-war]]); whether she died in it, survived it, or held Tortuga after it is not
written — and a thousand years have passed since either way. What Delahaye finds of her is one of
the things Delahaye is going to realm-06 to learn.

## In-Game Role

**A memory, and a question.** She is met in Delahaye's recovered past; whether she is ever met in
the present — as a grave, a legend, a line of descendants holding Tortuga — is the second half of
Delahaye's goal in [[realm-06]] ([[wraith-delahaye]]).
