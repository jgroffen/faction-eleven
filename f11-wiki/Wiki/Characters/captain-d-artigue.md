# d'Artigue, the Betrayer

**Captain of [[the-holy-socks]]**, a Pirate — and the one who **sold out the pirate armada** by
revealing its plans to the [[institute-of-eight]]. The betrayal got [[wraith-delahaye]] killed,
along with many of her allies, in [[the-pirate-institute-war]]'s last engagement before the gates
were sealed. **Why he did it is unknown — probably selfish gain.** Male.

**Role:** antagonist · **Faction:** [[pirates]] · **Home:** [[realm-06]]

## Character

A captain among captains — the armada was a fleet of independent ships, and his was one of them
until it wasn't. **His ship did not join the fleet**, and that absence is how the armada knew: they
realised d'Artigue **must have** betrayed them. Delahaye's own crew, [[the-drunken-mermaid]]'s,
**already knew him to be untrustworthy.** What he got for the armada's plans, and what he wanted,
is not known — to the design or to Delahaye. Selfish gain is the likeliest reading and the only one
on record.

## In-Game Role

**The object of Delahaye's revenge.** She breaks out of the Damned's game to rescue her allies; as
the frozen map teaches her how much time has passed, that becomes revenge on the one who sold them
([[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]). She knows who — the captain whose ship never came — but not why,
and not where he went. The Pirate realm's islands are where she starts looking.

Whether d'Artigue is ever met, fought, or found is not written. As an antagonist he is so far a
destination rather than a presence.

## Open

- A class ([[the-six-classes]]) and a face.
- **Why.** Selfish gain is probable, not established; what he was given, if anything, is unknown.
- **Where.** Whether he is alive after a thousand years, whether [[the-holy-socks]] still sails under
  anyone, or whether Delahaye finds a house, a descendant or a grave.
- **Whether the armada was right.** The in-world case is circumstantial — an absent ship and a bad
  reputation. That he did it is established; that anyone at the time could *prove* it is not.
