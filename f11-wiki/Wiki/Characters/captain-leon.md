# Leon, the Captain

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[pirates]] · **Home:** [[realm-06]]

Protagonist of the [[pirates]]. A commander rather than a lone adventurer — the only protagonist whose archetype implies playing at fleet scale.

## Name

**Leon.** Male. Named after **Leon Treich**, a French fiction writer of the 1940s who — along with
oral storytelling — contributed to the stories of Jacquotte Delahaye, the pirate the [[wraith-delahaye]] is
based on. The two Pirate protagonists, one alive and one dead, are drawn from the same legend;
whether that means anything in-fiction is not established.

## Character

Not yet established.

**Death mechanic:** pay the ferryman, losing booty.

## In-Game Role

Player character. Commander archetype. Currency: Booty.
