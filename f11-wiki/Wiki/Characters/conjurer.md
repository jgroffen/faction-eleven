# Conjurer

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[mystics]] · **Home:** [[realm-04]]

Protagonist of the [[mystics]]: a magician working out of a home cottage, descending into dungeons and the underworld below a friendly above-world.

## Character

Not yet established as a person. The loop that defines them is domestic as much as heroic — go down, come back, refine what you found.

**Death mechanic:** **homunculi** from the cottage retrieve the Conjurer's gear and body and revive them.

## In-Game Role

Player character. Specialist archetype. Currency: Scrolls. Gear comes out of dungeons and is refined or enhanced at the cottage.
