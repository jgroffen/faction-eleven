# Coruscate

An **ancient Summoner** of the [[mystics]] and a member of [[the-graceful-servants]]. She is the
**third carrier of [[the-red-power]]**, and she has used it twice — recently — to unseal two gates
out of [[realm-04]]: one to the [[werebeasts]], so her society could harvest them, and one to the
[[celestials]].

**Role:** npc · **Faction:** [[the-graceful-servants]] · **Home:** [[realm-04]]

## Name

**Coruscate** — a flash of lightning. Female. She glows red when the power is in use, as all three
carriers do.

## Character

Not yet established as a person. What is established is her position: **ancient**, which in a realm
of dispersed cottage-magicians makes her an institution rather than a neighbour, and a **Summoner** —
the Mystics' Front Liner class, "dominators of lesser creatures" ([[the-six-classes]]). That is the
same craft the homunculi come from, one rank up: [[conjurer-voisin]] lives with the small version of
what Coruscate does.

## In-Game Role

**An obstacle before she is a character.** The player meets [[celestial-tower]] as a place to sneak
through, not as a faction with a name. Whether Coruscate is seen, avoided or fought there is not
designed.

## What She Has Opened

Both gates were unsealed **recently**. This is current business, not old history.

| Gate | Why | What it took |
|------|-----|--------------|
| **04↔09**, to the [[werebeasts]] | so [[the-graceful-servants]] could harvest werebeasts for Reagents, which pay out an inordinate amount ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]) | her Red Power for the gate — then **other** high-level Servants to sneak past the gargoyle barrier behind it and put realm-09's guardians in **stasis** |
| **04↔03**, to the [[celestials]] | unstated | her Red Power alone; the gate sits inside [[celestial-tower]] under guard |

**She manifests the gift as *dispelling*** — a seal treated as a working that can be undone, rather
than a wall to be forced ([[ninja-kazuma]]) or a lock to be turned ([[val]]). Of the three carriers
she is the only one who has used it more than once, and the only one who appears to understand it as a
technique.

**Her power alone was not enough.** [[the-red-power]] opens gates and has no purchase on a
[[the-realm-barriers|barrier]]. Getting into realm-09 took a team: Coruscate for the gate, and
Servants with unique powerful gear and particular faction-class skills to pass the barrier **without
breaking it** — which is why nothing woke — and to put the gargoyles under.

## Ties

- [[the-graceful-servants]] — her society, and who acts on what she opens.
- [[the-red-power]] — the gift, and the other two who carry it.
- [[xipe-totec-the-red]] — its source, who gave it so that "compatible neighbours could reach each
  other again." She has used it for both things at once: **04↔03 is an allied gate**, exactly what the
  gift was for, and **04↔09 is the ring-opposite enemy gate**, exactly what it was not.
- [[conjurer-voisin]] — the Mystic protagonist, who crosses through her gate. Voisin has never heard
  of her.

## Open

- **Whether she knows what the power is or where it came from.** Neither [[ninja-kazuma]] nor [[val]]
  does. She has used it twice, deliberately, which suggests she knows more than either — but
  understanding a technique is not the same as knowing a god handed it to you.
- Whether she and Voisin ever meet.
- Why she opened the Celestial gate at all. The Werebeast gate has a motive; this one has none on
  record.
- What "ancient" means for a Mystic, and whether she was alive for [[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] —
  which would make the harvest a continuation rather than a revival.
- What the Servants are stockpiling Reagent *for*, and whether she is the one who wants it.
