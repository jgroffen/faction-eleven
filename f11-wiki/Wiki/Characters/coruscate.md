# Coruscate

An **ancient Summoner** of the [[mystics]] and a member of [[the-graceful-servants]]. He is the
**third carrier of [[the-red-power]]** — the one the wiki has carried for a long time as "a
Mystic, not yet named" — and he has used it twice: once to open the way to the [[werebeasts]] so
his society could harvest them, and once to open the way to the [[celestials]].

**Role:** npc · **Faction:** [[the-graceful-servants]] · **Home:** [[realm-04]]

## Name

**Coruscate** — a flash of lightning. He glows red when the power is in use, as all three carriers
do.

## Character

Not yet established as a person. What is established is his position: **ancient**, which in a realm
of dispersed cottage-magicians makes him an institution rather than a neighbour, and a
**Summoner** — the Mystics' Front Liner class, "dominators of lesser creatures"
([[the-six-classes]]). That is the same craft the homunculi come from, one rank up: [[conjurer-voisin]]
lives with the small version of what he does.

## In-Game Role

**An obstacle before he is a character.** The player meets the Servants' tower as a place to sneak
into ([[conjurer-voisin]] crossing to [[realm-03]]), not as a faction with a name. Whether Coruscate
is seen, avoided or fought there is not designed.

## What He Has Opened

| Gate | Why | Consequence |
|------|-----|-------------|
| **04↔09**, to the [[werebeasts]] | so [[the-graceful-servants]] could harvest werebeasts for Reagents, which the archive said they were rich in | the old war restarted; realm-09's intact gargoyles somehow did not wake |
| **04↔03**, to the [[celestials]] | unstated | the door [[conjurer-voisin]] uses to answer [[the-wand]], inside the Servants' tower and under guard |

**He manifests the gift as *dispelling*** — a seal treated as a working that can be undone, rather
than a wall to be forced ([[ninja-kazuma]]) or a lock to be turned ([[val]]). Of the three carriers
he is the only one who has used it more than once, and the only one who appears to understand it as
a technique.

## Ties

- [[the-graceful-servants]] — his society, and who acts on what he opens.
- [[the-red-power]] — the gift, and the other two who carry it.
- [[xipe-totec-the-red]] — its source, who gave it so that "compatible neighbours could reach each
  other again." Realm-04 and realm-09 are ring-opposites and enemies; what the gift was used for
  here is not what it was given for.
- [[conjurer-voisin]] — the Mystic protagonist, who crosses through his gate and never learns whose
  it is.

## Open

- **Whether he knows what the power is or where it came from.** Neither [[ninja-kazuma]] nor
  [[val]] does. He has used it twice, deliberately, which suggests he knows more than either — but
  understanding a technique is not the same as knowing a god handed it to you.
- Whether he and Voisin ever meet, and whether she ever learns that a Mystic opened the gate the
  Realm Champions have resolved to seal.
- Why he opened the Celestial gate at all. The Werebeast gate has a motive; this one has none on
  record.
- What "ancient" means for a Mystic, and whether he was alive for [[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] —
  which would make the harvest a continuation rather than a revival.
