# Gargoyle

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[green-skins]] · **Home:** [[realm-09]]

Protagonist of the [[green-skins]] — an infiltrator playing for the faction that every other faction in the material experiences as an invader.

## Character

**The Gargoyle is himself very ancient** — old enough that "ancient" is a description of him and
not just of the ruins he lives among. And he **wants to stop the war the [[green-skins]] are
fighting with the [[celestials]]**.

That makes him the first protagonist who is **opposed to his own faction's project**. The player
will spend other stories being attacked by green-skins and then play one who is trying to stop it
— the tension the roster has always implied, now with someone inside it.

His age is unexplained and is the most loaded loose thread in the wiki: a very ancient being, in a
setting whose antagonist is called the Ancients and whose disconnection has lasted long enough to
become myth. Whether he remembers the time before is not stated.
