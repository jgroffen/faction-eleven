# Huitzilopochtli the Blue

God of war, human sacrifice and bloodletting, lord of the **South**.

**Role:** god · **Faction:** [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] · **Home:** —

## Character

He backs [[tezcatlipoca-the-black]] and believes in **forced evolution through conflict** — that a
world only becomes better under pressure. But he resents how much progress the Black's total
destruction throws away, which is why he could be persuaded to seal the realms in the first place.

**What he is:** a **regular developer, male, late twenties** — the one who doesn't test, who makes
big sweeping changes with little thought for their impact or side-effects, and who ships. **He gets
more done than anyone, at the cost of quality.**

His domains are the fitting ones: war, sacrifice, bloodletting. He is the god of **spending things to
get a result** — losses accepted in advance, consequences discovered afterwards. Forced evolution
through conflict is a design philosophy and it is also just *ship it and see what survives.*

**He did not ask permission either, and he did not go first.** [[xipe-totec-the-red]]'s sneaked-in
change is the catalyst for his — once she had gone around the process and nothing happened to her, the
process was over, and he made his own changes the same way and very much larger.

He and the Red are regular developers; the Black and the White are seniors. **The difference between
them is scale and care, not licence:** she made a small, elegant, precisely targeted change without
review; he made a sweeping one. Both went around the same door, and his was the one that started a
war.

**He is the antagonist of the game's live conflict.** Seeing the Red and the White both intervening,
he renewed his alliance with the Black and shipped a **Secret Society System**: hidden groups seeded
into realms with their own convictions and their own agendas. [[the-guardians-of-night]] are an
instance of it.

That distinction matters. **The Guardians of Night are not a plot; they are a feature.** He did not
recruit [[val]] or instruct her. He built a system, it found a vampire already carrying
[[xipe-totec-the-red]]'s power, and it made her the founder of a secret society convinced that a war
was coming and that striking first was the only defence. Everything downstream — the smashing of the
gargoyles, the broken barrier, three realms marching on the [[celestials]] and [[mystics]] — followed
from a system doing exactly what it was built to do ([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]],
[[the-broken-barrier]]).

**He shipped a feature and stopped watching**, and that is the whole of it. He did not model what a
seeded society would do to three realms that were already at each other's throats, and he did not go
back and look. Three realms went to war as a side-effect of an untested change.

**He is "an Ancient".** The unnamed singular figure manipulating the Guardians of Night, which the
design carried unresolved for a long time, is him — and he is not manipulating them in any sense he
would recognise.

## Where He Sits Against The Red

He and the Red are the same problem from opposite ends — **velocity against quality**.

| | The Blue | The Red |
|---|---|---|
| Wants | it done | it right |
| Builds | big, sweeping, untested changes | big, elegant, complicated systems |
| Fails by | shipping without looking | never shipping |

**Neither is a bad developer, and each is the correction to the other.** The Red spent a thousand
years deliberating and the Blue spent an afternoon not thinking; her brake is what he lacks, his push
is what she lacks. That is not a character note; it is the mechanism of the ending
([[story-outline-requirement]]) — the four leveraging their differences to balance each other rather
than arguing about whose way is right.

## In-Game Role

Never met, and the most consequential of the four in the present day. Everything the player fights in
the opening chapters traces back to him through people who have never heard of him.

His colour marks him: **blue** in his avatar at work, in the avatar he wears inside the games, and in
the fragments of the outside that leak in where the player can find them ([[the-four-developers]]).

**Lead on:** [[pirates]] (06), [[werebeasts]] (07) and [[green-skins]] (09) — the arc that fought the
old war against the Red's. Three realms shaped by someone who builds fast and does not check: two of
them have never progressed for in-fighting, and the third came out of the linking better off than
anyone.
