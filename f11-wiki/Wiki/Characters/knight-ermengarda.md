# Ermengarda of Oluja, the Knight

**Role:** npc · **Faction:** [[celestials]] · **Home:** [[realm-03]]

A Celestial **Knight** who fights beside [[templar-will]] — a second hero unit on the defence
field, the person the Templar talks to, and the one who gets [[oluja-town]]'s people out.

## Name

**Ermengarda of Oluja.** Female. The [[celestials]] use the honorific **"Sister"** when they speak
to or of her, as they use **"Brother"** for [[templar-will]].

**Knight is her class, not her name** — the [[celestials]]' **Front Liner**, where the Templar is
their **Commander** ([[the-six-classes]]). They are different classes of the same faction, held by
different people: Ermengarda is the Knight, [[templar-will]] the Templar.

## Her Part In The Retreat

**She is at [[oluja-town]]** when the Templar arrives with word that the green-skin war machine is
coming for it. They join up and hold the waves together while the town empties behind them, and
abandon the position when the machine itself arrives — **Ermengarda leading the townsfolk out** to
[[templar-monastery]] ([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]).

**She fights the monastery waves** beside him, against green-skins and [[the-damned]], through the
battle in which the Templar decides to stop retreating ([[the-stand-at-the-monastery]]).

**Then she turns the faction around.** After the war machine is taken at the monastery she
**convinces the Templar to join her in driving it back the way it came** — recovering territory and
liberating towns, [[oluja-town]] among them ([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]). It is the
first Celestial action in their whole story that is not a defence, and **she is the one who argues for
it**: the Templar has just named an alliance and is about to leave it on her say-so.

That makes her the faction's hinge. Every Celestial decision until now has been the Templar's, spoken
to her; this one is hers, spoken to him.

## Character

**She is who the Templar speaks to.** The Celestials' story to this point is a commander alone
with a decision — hold the outpost until it cannot be held, lead the retreat, choose the monastery
because the faction cannot win by itself ([[the-fall-of-the-outpost]],
[[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]). Ermengarda gives those decisions somebody to be said out loud
to, which is what turns a plan into a scene.

A Front Liner beside a Commander who **fights in his own battle line** is also the natural pairing
of the two classes: the one who holds the line, and the one directing it from inside it.

And she is the one who **takes the civilians out** while he fights the rearguard — so the two
halves of what a retreat is, split between two characters who can argue about it.

## In-Game Role

**Hero unit** in [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]] — directly controlled on the top-down defence
field in the same way the Templar is, rather than a tower or a wave unit. The mechanic already
makes hero units the way allies join the wall; Ermengarda is the first one who is a **Celestial**,
so the pair is on the field before the multiverse arrives to reinforce it.

Whether the player moves her, or she fights under her own logic beside the Templar, is not
established.
