# Ember, the Phoenix

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[fey-folk]] · **Home:** [[realm-05]]

Protagonist of the [[fey-folk]]: the Phoenix, one of the six **Fey Beasts** who guard the Fey Folk
([[the-fey-and-the-fell]]), and the only one who can fight back. He is **reborn from ashes** on death.

## Name

**Ember.** Male.

## Character

Not yet established beyond what he does. **He survives the werebeasts' hunt** and drives
[[weresnake-taz]]'s pack back through the 09↔05 gate, then **stays to guard it** against werebeast
incursions ([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]]).

## In-Game Role

Player character, Heavy Weapons archetype. Currency: Crystals. One of six Fey Beasts on the selection
screen of [[fey-ley-cleansing]]:

- **Cleansing Fire** (active): burns away corruption, and it is the only skill in the Fey game that
  can fight back.
- **Rebirth** (passive): he comes through death ([[death-and-return]]).

A player who happens to be playing the Phoenix when the werebeasts attack survives, and never sees a
Fey Beast beheaded.

## His Story

**[[chapter-08]].** Taz comes back with a group built to beat him, with high damage and a fast member
to grab his ashes before Rebirth. The Gargoyle's party follows her through the gate and saves him.

**He joins [[the-realm-champions]] because they saved him.** Without that he would never trust a
group with a werebeast in it. He wants the gate closed and Taz stopped, and he and [[werewolf-gill]]
start out uneasy.
