# Will of Azylach, the Templar

**Role:** protagonist · **Faction:** [[celestials]] · **Home:** [[realm-03]]

Protagonist of the [[celestials]], holding a castle against attacks from two open gates at once.

## Name

**Will of Azylach.** Male. The [[celestials]] use the honorific **"Brother"** when they speak
to or of him.

**[[azylach]] is a place in [[realm-03]]**, this realm — and one the game does not visit. It gives
the name a home without adding a location the player goes to, and it may stay that way.

"Templar" is his class, not his name. "Knight" is a **different faction class of the same faction** —
the Celestials' Front Liner — and it belongs to [[knight-ermengarda]], a different character
([[the-six-classes]]).

## Character

A commander before a hero. What the Templar does across the first three chapters is **lose well**,
and then stop.

1. He holds [[the-outpost]] until it cannot be held and leads his people out
   ([[the-fall-of-the-outpost]]) — falling back to a place he **chooses**, the
   [[templar-monastery]], because he has worked out that the Celestials cannot win alone and need
   **ancient allies**.
2. Acting on that belief, he finds and uses [[the-wand]] and [[odie]] there to call the
   [[mystics]] and the [[robots]] ([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]).
3. Word comes that the war machine is making for [[oluja-town]]. He goes, fights beside
   [[knight-ermengarda]] to hold it **while its people get out**, and gives the town up when the
   machine arrives ([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]).
4. Back at the monastery, with the town behind his walls and waves of [[green-skins]] and
   [[the-damned]] at them, he **decides there will be no third retreat**: the machine is answered
   here ([[the-stand-at-the-monastery]]).

Two positions spent, each for something he could not have got by holding it — an archive, and a
population.

**And he is answered.** [[conjurer-voisin]] reaches the monastery first ([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]),
and together the two of them work out what the Templar has actually been holding: the relics are
**links to ancient allies, and a way to call for help** ([[allied-faction-signals]]). When [[ninja-kazuma]],
[[gargoyle-granite]] and [[mifix]] arrive, the two can name Mifix as the same thing for the
[[institute-of-eight]] ([[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]]).

**What the Templar does not know is where the gates are.** The [[celestials]]' awareness of their
own gates has faded; the Templar holds two open ones because the enemy comes through them, and could
not find a sealed one — which is why the party has to go and ask a gargoyle
([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]).

The longer arc from the prototype still stands: the Templar eventually **captures a green-skin war
machine**, upgrades it, fields it in defence, and takes it to the enemy.

**Death mechanic:** TBD.

## In-Game Role

Player character and **hero unit**, played as [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]: moved directly
around a top-down defence field, with platforming exploration between battles. Later allies from
other factions also become hero units with special powers, levelling and loot — so the wall fills
up with the multiverse as the story converges.

**Commander** archetype ([[the-six-classes]]). Currency: Relics.

The class and the game style agree: a Commander who **fights on the field he is directing** is
exactly what [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]] asks for, and it is why later allies joining as hero
units reads as reinforcement rather than as a cast change. The first of those allies is his own
faction's Front Liner: [[knight-ermengarda]], a second hero unit on the field — and the person he
speaks to, which is what gives the Celestials' decisions somewhere to be spoken.

**He names the alliance and then leaves it.** At the monastery he calls the assembled protagonists
[[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] ([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]) — and then, at
[[knight-ermengarda]]'s urging, does not travel with them. The war machine is **captured** at that
battle, and the two Celestials take it **back the way it came**, recovering territory and liberating
towns including [[oluja-town]].

**The counterattack is his arc's turn.** It carries on into Damned-held Celestial land, which the
[[the-damned|Damned]] defend with tower defences of their own — the Celestials' own genre played
against them ([[damned-undead-kingdom-rts]], [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]). The faction built
around retreating spends the rest of its story advancing.

## Ties

Defends against [[green-skins]] and [[the-damned]].

- [[knight-ermengarda]] — the Knight who fights beside him, and who he talks to.
- [[the-outpost]] · [[oluja-town]] · [[templar-monastery]] — the two he loses, and the one he
  stops at; [[azylach]] — the place in his name, unvisited.
- [[odie]] — the relic he wakes, and the first he understands.
- [[conjurer-voisin]] — the first ally to answer, and co-author of the relics insight.
- [[ninja-kazuma]], [[gargoyle-granite]], [[mifix]] — the second party to arrive.
