# Tezcatlipoca the Black

Creator god, lord of darkness, lord of the night, god of battles, lord of the **North**. The Smoking
Mirror. Old arch-nemesis of [[quetzalcoatl-the-white]].

**Role:** god · **Faction:** [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] · **Home:** —

## Character

**He makes worlds in order to watch them fail.** Creation, collapse, and a better creation built from
the wreckage — each iteration improving on the last. The destruction is not malice; it is the part of
the method where the learning happens.

**What he is:** the developer who wants to **scrap the franchise and rebuild from a better
architecture.** Not cruelty — the honest engineering instinct that the codebase is beyond saving and
the next one will be cleaner.

This is what makes him the game's most dangerous antagonist and its least villainous: **he is
arguably right.** Every developer who has ever wanted to rewrite instead of refactor is on his side.
The stake he represents is **deletion**, and it is frightening precisely because it is reasonable.

**He is the one who linked the realms** ([[the-linking-of-the-realms]]), because a stable reality was
a stalled one. It worked: reality raced toward destruction, several realms were damaged permanently,
and he then chose *not* to fight the other three when they sealed the gates — the conflict had
produced enough change to be going on with. He is patient in a way the other three are not.

**He is also, unknowingly, the source of the game's loose thread.** He built the liches as an
experiment in something that could act outside a realm's rules, and they used it to leave. They have
been out in [[the-void]] ever since, and he has no idea ([[the-damned]], [[aliens]]).

## In-Game Role

Not fought and not met. He is the position the ending argues against and cannot dismiss.

**Lead on:** [[the-damned]] (08) and [[aliens]] (10) — which is why his experiment in one is looting
the other with nobody watching.
