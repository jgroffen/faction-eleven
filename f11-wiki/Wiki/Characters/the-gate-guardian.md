# The Gate Guardian

The boss that holds the guarded gate in [[realm-01]] — the gate to [[realm-02]]. The
[[institute-of-eight]] sentence [[ninja]]'s family to fight it **endlessly**, generation after
generation, and no one has ever beaten it.

**Role:** boss · **Faction:** not established · **Home:** [[realm-01]]

## Character

Nothing is established about what it is, who set it there, or whether it is alive. What is
established is what it does:

- It **holds the gate**, and has for longer than anyone's reliable memory.
- **[[mifix]] cannot approach it.** This is the one place in the realm the Ninja's protector cannot
  follow, and therefore the only place the Ninja can truly die ([[death-and-return]]).
- It is **not defeated until every faction has been introduced** — the Institute's climax is a
  late-game turning point, not an early one.

In chapter one it is fought and not beaten, but the battle **cracks the gate**
([[the-cracked-gate]]) — the first break in the multiverse's silence.

## In-Game Role

The [[institute-of-eight]]'s recurring boss, fought under [[ninja-rhythm-platforming]]. The faction's
whole structure is a cycle of **boss fight → defeat → training**, so this character *is* the
faction's progression.

## Naming

Three names are in use: "the gate guardian", "the gate warden" and "the Gate Boss". **Gate
Guardian** is used as the in-fiction name here, with the others as aliases; "Gate Boss" reads as the
design-side term.

## Open

- What it is, and **who or what set it there**. If the [[institute-of-eight]] posted it, why guard
  the gate to a historically friendly neighbour ([[the-cracked-gate]])? If it was already there,
  the Institute's sentence is a punishment built on someone else's fortification.
- Whether it can be reasoned with, and whether it is a victim of the same disconnection.
