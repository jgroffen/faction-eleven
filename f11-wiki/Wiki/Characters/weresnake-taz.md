# Tazhra the Unblinking

A **Weresnake** of the [[werebeasts]] — the faction's Commander class ([[the-six-classes]]) — and the
**carrier of [[the-red-power]] in the Blue's games.** Almost always called **Taz**.

**Role:** npc · **Faction:** [[werebeasts]] · **Home:** [[realm-09]]

## Name

**Tazhra the Unblinking**, shortened to **Taz**. Female.

## Character

**A hunter.** She has used the Red Power to open [[realm-09]]'s gate to the [[fey-folk]] — not for an
alliance and not for a war, but **so she can hunt Fey Folk**. Mythical beasts make for great trophies,
which is the whole of the werebeasts' sport ([[werebeast-pack-hunt]]).

## Her Gate

| Gate | Why | How |
|------|-----|-----|
| **09↔05**, to the [[fey-folk]] | to hunt Fey Folk for trophies | her Red Power for the gate; realm-09's own barrier lets her pass |

**The barrier did not stop her, and it was not broken.** [[the-realm-barriers|Realm-09's barrier]]
on that gate still stands. Its gargoyles are loyal to [[realm-09]] and do not treat a werebeast
leaving as a breach, and the fortifications have a way through for locals.

## Why Her

**The Red set her power-granting code to reach one character in a game from each developer's
realms, on purpose** ([[xipe-totec-the-red]]). The four carriers are one per dev:

| Developer | Carrier |
|---|---|
| [[quetzalcoatl-the-white]] | [[ninja-kazuma]] |
| [[tezcatlipoca-the-black]] | [[val]] |
| [[xipe-totec-the-red]] | [[coruscate]] |
| [[huitzilopochtli-the-blue]] | **Taz** |
