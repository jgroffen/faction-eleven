# Xipe-Totec the Red

God of agriculture, fertility, the seasons, metalsmiths and disease, lord of the **East**.

**Role:** god · **Faction:** [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] · **Home:** —

## Character

He backs [[quetzalcoatl-the-white]] and believes in growth and the spread of life over non-life —
and he **resents the stagnation** that the White's order produces. A world that is not permitted to
change is not being saved, in his view; it is being embalmed.

**What he is:** the developer who wants **growth, content and connection** — more world, more life,
more reach.

**He broke the peace he helped make.** Watching the sealed realms decay, he acted without asking:
imbuing [[ninja]] and [[val]] with the power to unseal the gates to their realms' old allies. It was
a change sneaked in rather than argued for, because there was no room in the plan for it.

Two of the game's most important characters are carrying his power and neither knows:

- [[ninja]] strikes [[the-gate-guardian]] with it, fails to hurt the boss, and **cracks the gate**
  behind it — the first opening in the multiverse's silence ([[the-cracked-gate]]).
- [[val]] unseals [[the-damned]]'s gates to the [[green-skins]] and [[werebeasts]], and is then
  picked up by [[huitzilopochtli-the-blue]]'s system and made the founder of
  [[the-guardians-of-night]].

**So the Red is the first cause of both the reconnection and the war.** He intended the first and set
off the second, and he is the only god whose intervention is still walking around inside two
protagonists.

His disagreement with the Blue is the live one among the four: **more world versus more challenge.**
Both are diagnosing the same decline. Neither will accept the other's cure.

## In-Game Role

Never met. Present in the game as **capability the protagonists cannot account for** — the power in
the Ninja's hands and the reason Val could open a door that had been shut for a thousand years.

**Lead on:** [[celestials]] (03), [[mystics]] (04) and [[fey-folk]] (05) — the arc that fought the
old war against the Blue's.
