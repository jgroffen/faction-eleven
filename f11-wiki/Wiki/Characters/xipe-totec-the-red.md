# Xipe-Totec the Red

Goddess of agriculture, fertility, the seasons, metalsmiths and disease, lord of the **East**.

**Role:** god · **Faction:** [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] · **Home:** —

## Character

**She is the craftsman god, and her flaw is her craft.** Xipe-Totec's domains are the slow ones —
seasons that cannot be hurried, crops that ripen when they ripen, metal worked and reworked until it
is right. She is also the Flayed One, who sheds a skin so that a better one can grow underneath.
Everything about her is *refinement*.

**What she is:** a **regular developer, female, late twenties** — a perfectionist who wants everything
right before she does anything, who builds **big, elegant, complicated systems**, and who cannot
ship. She polishes the rock. She scope-creeps her own work. She is not a bad developer; she is a good
one whose good instinct has no brake on it.

**Her sin is not what she did, it is how long she took to do it.** She could see the sealed realms
decaying — the [[fey-folk]] stagnating, [[the-damned]] dwindling, the [[mystics]] losing the will to
innovate — and she deliberated for a thousand years while it happened. When she finally acted, she
was **right, and she was late**, and the realms she wanted to save had already been hollowed out.

And what she built was characteristic: not a blunt reopening of the gates but a small, elegant,
precisely targeted thing — power placed in **two specific people** to unseal **specific** doors to
compatible neighbours. Beautifully scoped. It still started a war, because an elegant change released
into a system nobody else knew you had touched is still an unreviewed change.

**Her domain includes disease**, which is the dark half of her: what happens to something that is
tended too carefully and never let out.

**She did not ask permission, and that is the hinge of the entire story.** The two great acts before
hers were argued for openly and agreed to — the Black won the case for linking the realms, the White
won the case for sealing them ([[the-linking-of-the-realms]], [[the-long-disconnection]]). Hers was
the **first change made without proposal, argument or review**, and it is the catalyst: it is what
licensed [[huitzilopochtli-the-blue]] to do the same thing on a far larger scale.

She and the Blue are regular developers; the Black and the White are seniors. She went around the
process because she did not have the standing to win an argument inside it — which is the quietest
and most damning thing in the game's history. **The world does not break when a god decides to break
it. It breaks when someone stops feeling able to ask.**

## Where She Sits Against The Blue

She and the Blue are the same problem from opposite ends — **quality against velocity**.

| | The Red | The Blue |
|---|---|---|
| Wants | it right | it done |
| Builds | big, elegant, complicated systems | big, sweeping, untested changes |
| Fails by | never shipping | shipping without looking |

**Neither is a bad developer, and each is the correction to the other.** His willingness to ship is
the push she has never had; her insistence on getting it right is the brake he has never had. That is
not a character note; it is the mechanism of the ending ([[story-outline-requirement]]). The
resolution is not one of the four being proved right — it is the four using their differences to
balance each other instead of arguing about them.

## Her Power, Still Walking Around

Two of the game's most important characters carry her work and neither knows:

- [[ninja]] strikes [[the-gate-guardian]] with it, fails to hurt the boss, and **cracks the gate**
  behind it — the first opening in the multiverse's silence ([[the-cracked-gate]]).
- [[val]] unseals [[the-damned]]'s gates to the [[green-skins]] and [[werebeasts]], and is then picked
  up by the Blue's Secret Society System and made the founder of [[the-guardians-of-night]].

**So the Red is the first cause of both the reconnection and the war.** She intended the first and
set off the second.

## In-Game Role

Never met. Present as **capability the protagonists cannot account for** — the power in the Ninja's
hands and the reason Val could open a door that had been shut for a thousand years.

Her colour marks her: **red** in her avatar at work, in the avatar she wears inside the games, and in
the fragments of the outside that leak in where the player can find them ([[the-four-developers]]).

**Lead on:** [[celestials]] (03), [[mystics]] (04) and [[fey-folk]] (05) — the arc that fought the old
war against the Blue's. Three realms shaped by someone who builds slowly and beautifully, two of which
have since stagnated for want of anyone willing to change them.
