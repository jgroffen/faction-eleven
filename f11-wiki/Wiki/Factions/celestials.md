# The Celestials

A besieged holy order defending a castle. Their realm has gates standing **open** to both the
[[green-skins]] and [[the-damned]], and they are under attack from both at once.

**Homeland:** [[realm-03]] — see [[the-wheel-of-realms]]

## Identity

Defenders by circumstance. Their protagonist is [[templar-will]], the **Commander** class. "Knight" is
a *different faction class of the same faction* — the Front Liner — and it belongs to
[[knight-ermengarda]], who fights beside him.

**Game style:** [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]] — top-down tower defence with the Templar as a
directly-controlled hero unit, plus platforming exploration between battles.

**Currency:** Relics · **Role archetype:** **Commander** (see [[the-six-classes]])

## Standing

The only faction in the material fighting a **two-front war**, and the clearest evidence that
realm gates are not uniformly sealed — the disconnection is uneven.

- [[green-skins]] — attack relentlessly, many small units with occasional war machines.
- [[werebeasts]] — **not an attacker.** Their gate to [[realm-03]] is closed and they are not in the
  present war ([[werebeasts]]); the [[the-guardians-of-night|Guardians]] meant to bring them in and
  could not reach them.
- [[the-damned]] — **in the waves at [[templar-monastery]]** ([[the-stand-at-the-monastery]]),
  arriving **through the [[green-skins]]' realm** rather than through any gate of their own:
  [[val]] unsealed [[realm-08]]'s allied gate to [[realm-07]], and [[realm-07]]'s gate into this
  realm is already open with its barrier broken ([[the-broken-barrier]]). This is
  [[the-guardians-of-night]]' intended expansion into the Celestial realm, arriving before anyone
  here can name it.

The Templar eventually **captures a green-skin war machine**, upgrades it, uses it in defence, and
then takes it to the enemy — the faction's turn from defence to offence.

## Brother And Sister

The Celestials address each other with **gendered honorifics**. Their protagonist, the
[[templar-will]], is **Will of Azylach** — **Brother Will**. Their Front Liner,
[[knight-ermengarda]], is **Ermengarda of Oluja** — **Sister Ermengarda**.

The Templar is not alone on the field: the Knight fights beside him as a second hero unit and is
the person he talks to ([[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]).

## The Retreat

The Celestials' story is a **fighting withdrawal that stops.** They lose [[the-outpost]] in chapter
one ([[the-fall-of-the-outpost]]) and fall back to [[templar-monastery]].

The retreat is a decision, not a rout: the Templar picks the monastery because he believes the
Celestials need **ancient allies** to defeat the war machine — and he is right. In its ancient
parts he finds [[the-wand]] and [[odie]] and calls the [[mystics]] and the [[robots]]
([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]). The monastery is chosen as an *archive*, not a fortress.

Then word comes that the machine is making for [[oluja-town]]. The Templar goes, joins
[[knight-ermengarda]] there, and they hold the town long enough to empty it before abandoning it
([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]) — a second position lost, and the first one spent on purpose.
They fall back to the monastery with the town's people, fight waves of [[green-skins]] **and
[[the-damned]]** at its walls, and the Templar **stops retreating**: the answer to the war machine
happens here ([[the-stand-at-the-monastery]]).

So the faction that appears to be simply losing is losing **forward** — each position traded for
something it could not have had otherwise: an archive, a population, and finally a place to stand.

It is also the faction that **works out what the game is about first**: nobody wins alone.

And he is answered: [[conjurer-voisin]] arrives first ([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]), and with the
Templar works out that the relics are **links to ancient allies and a way to call for help**
([[allied-faction-signals]]). Then [[ninja-kazuma]], [[gargoyle-granite]] and [[mifix]] arrive
([[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]]).

## What They Have Forgotten

**Their awareness of the gates has faded.** The Celestials hold two open gates because the enemy
comes through them; the sealed ones — to the [[robots]] and the [[mystics]], their old allies — they
can no longer find ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]). They kept the relics of two friendships and
lost the doors. It is why, when the party at the monastery wants to reach the Robots, nobody in the
Celestials' own house can say where the way is, and the answer has to come from a gargoyle.
