# The Fey Folk

Creatures of folklore and fairy tale, guarded by the **Fey Beasts**, six powerful mythical creatures
whose mere presence cleanses the world of the **Fell** and their corrupting **Fell Power**
([[the-fey-and-the-fell]]). Their protagonist is [[phoenix-ember]].

**Homeland:** [[realm-05]] — see [[the-wheel-of-realms]]

## Identity

**The realm is one endless contest, cleansing against corruption, along the Ley Lines.** The Fey
Folk themselves are small, simple, automatic units: Fairies, Pixies, Elves, Sprites, Brownies and
Dryads, different only for flavour. **No one rules them.** The Fey Beasts are their guardians, and
the Stag Lord is first among them.

**Game style:** [[fey-ley-cleansing]]. You play a Fey Beast clearing corruption from isometric maps,
built on the Mystics' base, in real time, with no combat.

**Currency:** Crystals, which Fey Rings grow slowly and which store and move Magic · **Role
archetype:** Heavy Weapons

## Standing

**At home, they are being hunted.** [[weresnake-taz]] of the [[werebeasts]] opened the 09↔05 gate
with [[the-red-power]] and takes Fell Beasts and Fey Beasts alike as trophies
([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]]). The Phoenix now guards that gate.

**[[the-guardians-of-night]] have not attacked them**, because they are last on the plan, and
[[the-damned]] fear them: Fey Power cleanses corruption, and it works very well on the Damned.

## History

**In [[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] the Fey Folk never left their realm and never fought.** They were
on the Red's side of the war as supporters, not invaders:

- **Werebeasts hunted into realm-05** while the [[mystics]] and [[celestials]] began the war with
  their own incursions.
- **When the werebeasts, [[green-skins]] and [[the-damned]] allied against them, the Mystics and
  Celestials went looking for allies**, and the Fey had good reason to oppose the werebeasts.
- **The Fey supported them.** Mystics helped protect realm-05 from the werebeasts, the Fey supplied
  the Mystics with Reagents, and Mystic and Celestial forces could strike through Fey gates.
- **To cut the Mystics' Reagent supply, the werebeasts, Green Skins and Damned invaded realm-05**,
  because a realm with no combat looked easy. That is when the Damned found out what Fey Power does
  to them.

**Fey Beasts are very powerful outside their realm, but have no wish to leave it.** The Mystics draw
Reagents from Fey Beast magic without killing them. Killing one causes corruption and destroys the
Reagents, so the Mystics never hunted them.
