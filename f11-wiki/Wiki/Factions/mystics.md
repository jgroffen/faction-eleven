# The Mystics

Magicians living in towers, castles and cottages across a **magic-rich** realm. The world above
is friendly; below it are dungeons and an underworld.

**Homeland:** [[realm-04]] — see [[the-wheel-of-realms]]

## Identity

Scholarly and dispersed rather than organised — individual practitioners in their own homes rather
than an army. Their protagonist is [[conjurer]], whose cottage is the hub: gear comes back from
dungeons to be refined and enhanced, and **homunculi** retrieve the Conjurer's gear and body and
revive them.

**Currency:** Scrolls · **Role archetype:** Specialist

## Standing

Deep in the underworld is an **open gate** to the [[green-skins]] realm — which is why the
dungeons keep repopulating. The green-skins have been **mining the magic-rich plane**, making the
Mystics a resource colony as much as a rival.

The prototype notes flag that [[gargoyle]] and [[conjurer]] may meet in this story.
