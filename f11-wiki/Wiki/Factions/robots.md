# Robots

A civilisation of old, defunct machines who **do not know how to fix themselves**. Their creators
destroyed the autofix bots — the only models able to repair damaged robots and build new ones from
spare parts — out of fear of losing control of their creation. The creators are long gone; the
robots have fallen into disrepair and despair.

**Homeland:** [[realm-02]] — see [[the-wheel-of-realms]]

## Identity

Fractured rather than unified. Some bots found a new purpose after the creators left; others lack
the neural network depth to develop one and have shut down or gone insane. The ones with purpose
have developed **varied goals, some constructive and some destructive** — so the faction is at war
with itself as much as anything else.

Their protagonist is [[autofix]], a late-model Autofix and the only known functioning example.

**Currency:** Parts — superseded in play by a chain of **scrap → construction materials → modules
and components** ([[autofix-skill-tree]]) · **Role archetype:** Medic

**Game style:** [[robots-incremental-exploration]] — an exploration platformer with incremental
growth, replacing the prototype's Civ-style region control. The **fiction was kept and the
strategy layer replaced**: the purge, the lone survivor and the mystery of Autofix's awakening
are unchanged.

## Standing

Not established beyond their own realm. The robots' story goal is internal — unite the robots by
controlling all regions — with the multiverse plot entering as **sealed interplane gates** they
discover and cannot open alone. That inability is the hook that forces contact with other
factions.

Notably, a **mini-fix bot** made by Autofix's line turns up inside the Institute of Eight's story
as an ancestor spirit housed in a toy car ([[institute-of-eight]]), which is the earliest
cross-faction thread in the material.

## Notes

Open mysteries the story is built on: how did Autofix survive the autofix purge, why was it shut
down, and how did it awaken?

The robots **lead chapter one** ([[chapter-based-release]]), which makes Autofix's story the
carrier of the game's first contact with [[faction-eleven-antagonist]]'s work: sealed gates the
robots cannot open alone.
