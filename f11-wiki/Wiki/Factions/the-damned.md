# The Damned

The faction most entangled with the multiverse's machinery. Their **high-level liches are opening
gates** to the alien worlds, and they hold **ancient Lovecraftian gods that dwell in a void** —
a void which connects alien space to the Damned's own realm.

**Homeland:** [[realm-08]] — see [[the-wheel-of-realms]]

## Identity

Not yet established as a playable faction — the prototype notes mark their game description and
story as TBD. Their protagonist is [[wraith]], a specialist tied to a **single spawn point / grave
to return to**.

**Currency:** _the prototype notes give "Return to grave / single spawn point" in the currency
row, which appears to be a misplaced death mechanic rather than a currency — unresolved._

**Role archetype:** Specialist

## Standing

- [[aliens]] — the Damned are attacking them, and their liches open the gates that let it happen.

The **void** is significant beyond this faction: it is the only named structure in the material
that is neither a realm nor a gate, and it connects two realms. It may be the mechanism
behind [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]] rather than a location peculiar to the Damned.
