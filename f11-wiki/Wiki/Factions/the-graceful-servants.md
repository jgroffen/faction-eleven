# The Graceful Servants

A secret society of [[mystics]] who hold a mage tower in [[realm-04]], keep the realm's oldest
records, and have quietly reopened [[the-war-of-the-six-realms|the old war]] for a resource. They are
the reason the [[werebeasts]] are being harvested, and the reason [[conjurer-voisin]] can cross to
the [[celestials]] at all.

**Homeland:** [[realm-04]]

## Identity

Scholars before conspirators. Their power is **the archive**: they hold records dating back to
[[the-linking-of-the-realms]], from the brief window when the realms could still see each other.
Nobody else in [[realm-04]] has that reach.

**Their existence is not public.** They are a secret society in the ordinary sense — most Mystics have
never heard of them, [[conjurer-voisin]] included, until she is standing in their tower.

They do not smash. Where [[the-guardians-of-night]] hammer gargoyle heads
([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]), the Servants **put things to sleep** — they walked past a barrier
rather than breaking it, and left the guardians behind it dormant rather than dead. The name is not
ironic: they regard what they do as tidy, reversible and owed.

## Standing

**They know werebeasts are rich in Reagents**, and they know it from the archive — a fact surviving
from the linking, when somebody last had the chance to look
([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). The yield is not marginal: a werebeast pays out an inordinate
amount, because Reagent yield scales with how magical a creature is.

**Acting on it took three things and they had all three.** [[coruscate]], an ancient Summoner among
them, unsealed the **04↔09 gate to [[realm-09]]** with [[the-red-power]]. Other Servants — high-level
Mystics with particular faction-class skills, carrying **artefacts crafted by historically significant
Mystics** rather than anything a vendor sells — got past realm-09's gargoyle barrier **without breaking
it**, so nothing woke. Then they put the werebeast gargoyles into
**stasis**, so nothing would wake later either. All of this is **recent**.

**It is a trickle, not an invasion.** Every stage needs the realm's best practitioners and equipment
that barely exists, so the harvest runs as small parties slipping through. That is why nobody has
noticed — and it is what [[conjurer-voisin]] reasons out for the council when the room asks how it could
possibly be happening under an intact barrier.

**And the harvesting itself happens at home.** Reagent yield is a Mystic-realm rule
([[cross-realm-power-and-progression]]), so the incursions are **captures**: werebeasts are brought back
through the gate and harvested in [[the-werebeast-caverns]], with **wagons** running the Reagent out to
the stockpile.

**They are stockpiling.** Massive amounts of Reagent, for a **mysterious and dangerous purpose** that is
not on record. It is far more than a society of scholars could spend on itself.

**They hold both locks on the road to [[realm-09]].** [[celestial-tower]] at one end and
[[the-werebeast-caverns]] at the other — so the [[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] cannot reach the
werebeasts without going through the Servants twice. And waking realm-09's gargoyles is **a direct act
against everything the Servants are doing**, which makes a secret society of scholars the antagonist of
the Mystics' story from [[chapter-05]] onward.

**This is the old war restarted, by the realm that started it the first time.** The Mystics were among
the three realms "constantly incurring" into the green-skin, damned and werebeast realms in
[[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] — exactly what [[the-gargoyle-guardians]] were built to stop.

**[[the-guardians-of-night]] are a separate project with the same author.** They want to reconnect with
the Werebeasts and **have not managed it**; the Servants managed it alone. Both are instances of
[[the-secret-society-system]], working different realms. Whether either knows about the other is
open.

## Notes

**They also opened the way out.** Coruscate has dispelled the **04↔03 gate to the
[[celestials]]** as well, and it sits inside [[celestial-tower]] under guard — a tower named, plainly
and publicly, for the one gate in it that everybody knows about. That is the door
[[conjurer-voisin]] has to sneak past to answer [[the-wand]], so the player's first encounter with the
Servants is as an obstacle.

**And it is where they are found out.** Inside the tower Voisin **overhears** that the werebeast gate
is open and that Reagent is being stockpiled, and infers the rest
([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). The secret is blown by a villager passing through on unrelated
business, who had never heard of them an hour earlier.

**The Conjurer tells on them.** At [[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]] Voisin discloses
everything she overheard — the open gate, her belief that the motive is harvesting werebeasts, the
stockpile — and the party resolves that woken Werebeast gargoyles should **seal the gate to the
Mystics**. A society that survived unnoticed is exposed to five realms within days of one villager
walking through its front door.

## Why They Exist

**They are the second known instance of [[the-secret-society-system]]** — the
[[huitzilopochtli-the-blue|Blue]]'s auto-firing answer to intrigue, conflict and stagnation. What it
detected in [[realm-04]] was stagnation in **the enemies of the Mystics' game**: a bestiary that had
stopped being interesting. The society it built solved that by finding better monsters in somebody
else's realm.

**The system did not foresee what that would do to the economy.** Reagent yield scales with how magical
a creature is, werebeasts are the richest seam on the wheel, and the fix for a stale bestiary turned out
to be an exploit that breaks the faction's whole progression curve. Where the Guardians solved three
problems at once, this instance solved one and created a larger one — **the exhaust of a machine trying
to help** ([[continuity-glitches]]).

## Open

- **What the stockpile is for.** It is the society's actual goal and nothing is known about it.
- **Whether the stasis on realm-09's gargoyles can be undone**, and whether the player is who does it.
- Whether they know [[the-guardians-of-night]] exist, or that they share an author.
- How many Servants there are, and whether ordinary Mystics could name them. What is settled is
  that the harvest is **not** common knowledge and most Mystics cannot do it.
- What they believe they are owed, and by whom. The name implies a master.
