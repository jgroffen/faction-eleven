# The Graceful Servants

A secret society of [[mystics]] who hold a mage tower in [[realm-04]], keep the realm's oldest
records, and have quietly reopened [[the-war-of-the-six-realms|the old war]] for a resource. They are
the reason the [[werebeasts]] are being harvested, and the reason [[conjurer-voisin]] can cross to
the [[celestials]] at all.

**Homeland:** [[realm-04]]

## Identity

Scholars before conspirators. Their power is **the archive**: they hold records dating back to
[[the-linking-of-the-realms]], from the brief window when the realms were connected and the
realms could still see each other. Nobody else in [[realm-04]] has that reach, and most Mystics —
dispersed, cottage-bound, working their own caves — have no idea the Servants exist.

They do not smash. Where [[the-guardians-of-night]] hammer gargoyle heads
([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]), the Servants put things to sleep. The name is not ironic: they
regard what they do as tidy, reversible and owed.

## Standing

**They know werebeasts are rich in Reagents**, and they know it from the archive — a fact
surviving from the linking, when somebody last had the chance to look. Acting on it required a
door, and they had one: [[coruscate]], an ancient Summoner among them, carries
[[the-red-power]] and can dispel a sealed gate. He opened the **04↔09 gate to [[realm-09]]**, and
the Mystics have been **harvesting werebeasts for Reagents** through it ever since.

**This is the old war restarted, by the realm that started it the first time.** The Mystics were
among the three realms "constantly incurring" into the green-skin, damned and werebeast realms in
[[the-war-of-the-six-realms]] — which is exactly what [[the-gargoyle-guardians]] were built to
stop. Realm-09's gargoyles are the only set never smashed, and they are the guardians whose whole
purpose is to wake when something comes through. **They did not wake.** Why they did not is the
Servants' second achievement and is not yet written.

**They are not working for [[the-guardians-of-night]], and the two are not the same project.**
The Guardians want to reconnect with the Werebeasts and **have not managed it**. The Servants
managed it, independently, and for a reason of their own. Whether either knows about the other is
open.

## Notes

**They also opened the way out.** Coruscate has dispelled the **04↔03 gate to the
[[celestials]]** as well, and it sits inside the Servants' tower under guard. That is the door
[[conjurer-voisin]] has to sneak past to answer [[the-wand]] — so the player's first encounter with
the Servants is as an obstacle, with no idea what they are or what else they have opened.

**The Conjurer agrees to close their door without knowing it is theirs.** At
[[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]] the party resolves that reactivated Werebeast
gargoyles should **seal the gate to the Mystics** — the 04↔09 gate. Voisin is in the room. She does
not know her own faction opened it, or what is coming back through it.

## Open

- **Whether they are an instance of [[the-secret-society-system]].** They fit its three goals
  (intrigue, conflict, balance) and the system is on record as having fired more than once — but
  nothing says it authored them, and a society that arose on its own would mean something
  different. See [[the-mystics-second-secret-society]].
- **What they did to the Werebeast gargoyles.** An intact set that does not wake for an active
  incursion needs an explanation; stasis magic is the standing candidate, and whether it is
  reversible — by the player — is the question the party is walking toward.
- How many Servants there are, whether ordinary Mystics could name them, and whether the village
  Reagent trade knows where the good Reagents are now coming from.
- What they believe they are owed, and by whom. The name implies a master.
