# Werebeasts — Pack Hunt

The [[werebeasts]]' game style: **pack hunting large monsters.** [[werewolf-gill]] organises a party
of **up to five, himself included**, to **plan, hunt and battle big monsters for trophies.**

**State:** concept · **Category:** combat

**Implemented by:** _(no feature notes yet — no code exists)_

## How It Works

**Plan, hunt, battle.** A hunt is three phases with one prize: the party is assembled and briefed,
the quarry is tracked, and the big monster is fought. What the player takes home is a **trophy**.

**It is built on the Mystics' game.** The presentation and movement are those of
[[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]] — isometric, simple 2D — so the two realms share one base.

**But it is semi-turn-based.** The fight runs on its own, and **the player can pause at any time and
adjust the party's orders.** Where the Mystics' game is one character's action, this one is a
commander's pause-and-plan over a pack.

**Every werebeast in the party has a highly specialised role**, set by their faction class
([[the-six-classes]]): Weresnake, Werewolf, Werebear, Werebird, Werefox and Werecat each bring one
thing the hunt needs, and a good party is the right mix for the quarry.

## Why It's Fun

The quarry is the puzzle and the party is the answer. A big monster is read, planned against and
broken down by five specialists — and the trophy on the wall is the proof.
