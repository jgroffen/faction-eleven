# Handover: Gargoyle, Ninja and the Celestials' Gate

## State

_What is already written down. Link it, do not restate it._

- [[gargoyle]] — see "After Val" and "A Later Chapter": his private doubt after
  [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]], and that he can get the Ninja through the gate to the
  Celestials, which the Ninja cannot manage alone.
- [[ninja]] — carries [[xipe-totec-the-red]]'s power to breach gates and barriers, felt as a
  compulsion rather than known as a tool, until [[odie]] identifies it.
- [[odie]] — the one who identifies the Ninja's power, at [[templar-monastery]].
- [[templar]] — where the meeting happens; already the point [[chapter-02]] and [[chapter-03]]
  converge on via [[the-second-signal]] and [[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]].
- [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]] — the scene immediately before this thread starts.
- [[the-realm-barriers]] — the gate/barrier distinction this whole sequence turns on: the Gargoyle
  moves the Ninja through a **gate**, the Ninja later breaches a **barrier**.

## Not yet written down

_What only exists in this conversation: what was mid-flight, what is unverified,
what was tried and rejected. Anything here that deserves to persist should become a
real note instead._

Round 31 of `Raw/Sources/interviews/faction-eleven-lore-and-design.md` (Q64) establishes the
sequence but not its mechanics. Unresolved, in order:

1. **How the Gargoyle gets the Ninja through the green-skins→Celestials gate.** That gate is
   already established as open/broken ([[the-broken-barrier]]), so "the Ninja has no chance without
   him" needs a reason — likely something about *surviving the crossing* (war zone, patrols,
   stealth) rather than the gate itself being shut. Not specified by the author; needs grilling.
2. **What "answers" the Gargoyle is actually looking for in the Celestial realm.** The Ninja
   proposes it as a reason to travel together; what he expects to find there — Odie's memory,
   Templar's records, something else — is unstated.
3. **The exact shape of Odie's detection.** "Odie determines the Ninja can breach realm barriers" —
   mechanism, whether it's instant or a process, and whether it surprises Odie too (he has never
   met a Red-Power carrier before) is all open.
4. **Sequencing against [[chapter-03]]'s convergence.** This thread and
   [[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]] both put the Gargoyle, Ninja, Odie and Templar in the same
   place — confirm whether this *is* that convergence scene, extends it, or precedes it.
5. **What becomes of the Gargoyle's own doubt.** He hides it from Val in
   [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]]; whether he voices it to the Ninja, to Odie, or keeps carrying
   it silently into the Werebeast-realm chapter is unresolved.
6. **Also pending on the incoming gate-topology rule.** Green Skins now sit at [[realm-07]] and
   Werebeasts at [[realm-09]] ([[the-wheel-of-realms]]); the Ancient Ruin's gate from [[realm-01]]
   that gets the Ninja to the Gargoyle in the first place no longer has a working distance
   justification (see [[the-second-signal]], [[the-ancient-ruin]]). That's a separate, earlier
   problem from points 1-5 above, but the same new rule (static gates between allied *and* enemy
   realms) may end up answering both — worth solving together rather than in isolation.
