Round 31 (Q64) of `Raw/Sources/interviews/faction-eleven-lore-and-design.md` established the
sequence; Round 37 (Q81–Q86) resolved three of its six open points. Now written down:

- **#2 — what "answers" the Gargoyle is after: resolved.** Two things, both only an intact gargoyle
  can give: a judgement he would trust on whether he is malfunctioning (he does not trust Val; he
  would trust his own kind), and the physical location of the Celestials' gate to the Robots. See
  [[gargoyle-granite]] ("The Council, And The Judge He Would Trust") and
  [[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]] ("The Council").
- **#4 — sequencing against the convergence: resolved.** This thread *is* the convergence. The
  Conjurer reaches the Templar first ([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]); the Ninja, Gargoyle and Mifix
  arrive second; the council follows. [[chapter-03]] carries the three-beat order.
- **#5 — what becomes of the doubt: resolved.** He voices it to the Ninja and Mifix at the start of
  chapter three; Mifix tries to diagnose him, fails, and glitches
  ([[the-diagnosis-of-the-last-gargoyle]]).

Still unresolved, in order:

1. **How the Gargoyle gets the Ninja through the green-skins→Celestials gate.** That gate is
   already established as open/broken ([[the-broken-barrier]]), so "the Ninja has no chance without
   him" needs a reason — likely something about *surviving the crossing* (war zone, patrols,
   stealth) rather than the gate itself being shut. Not specified; needs grilling. Also now
   entangled with the diagnosis scene: its Open list asks whether the scene sits before or during
   this crossing.
2. **The exact shape of Odie's detection.** "Odie identifies that the Ninja can breach a sealed
   gate" (gates only, per [[the-red-power]]) — mechanism, whether it's instant or a process, and
   whether it surprises Odie too (he has never met a Red-Power carrier before) is all open.
3. **The route from the monastery to realm-09 and what waits there.** The Celestials' gate to the
   Werebeasts stands open and the enemy is using it in the other direction. What state the Werebeast
   gargoyles are in ("need reactivating" is on record; why is not) belongs to
   [[the-mystics-second-secret-society]], which now holds a sketch that contradicts
   [[werebeasts]]'s "never smashed, barriers stand."
4. **Also pending on the gate-topology rule.** The Ancient Ruin's gate from [[realm-01]] that gets
   the Ninja to the Gargoyle in the first place is now explained as one of the Institute's three
   enemy gates ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]); whether [[the-second-signal]] and
   [[the-ancient-ruin]] read cleanly against that is worth a check rather than a session.
