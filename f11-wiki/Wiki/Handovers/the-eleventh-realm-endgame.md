# Handover: The eleventh realm end game

End-game ideas the author is **considering**, not yet settled. They are recorded here, not in
compiled notes, because several of them clash with the ending already written down. Settle them in a
session, then compile.

## Not yet written down

_The ideas, as the author gave them (Raw interview, Round 69)._

1. **The game starts hinting at an eleventh faction that causes the glitches.** The champions decide
   they must gather and face it, and work out how to reach its realm.
2. **The eleventh faction and realm isn't a faction at all.** It is the void between games,
   representing the infrastructure and development systems the games run on. Its inhabitants are
   really the systems and engines that operate across the realms — and the developers themselves.
3. **The champions 'defeat' monstrous representations of the retcon engine and the continuity
   system.** These beasts can only be defeated in the eleventh realm, and doing so equates to
   computer characters trying to break the software subsystems they interact with.
4. **Breaking those systems gets the developers' attention.** The good ending — branching endings
   are undecided — has the champions prove to the devs that, given enough agency and free will, they
   will self-regulate their realms, and the games will stabilise and play far better for it.
5. **Opening the way to the eleventh realm needs the champions, a Glitch Lich and multiple holders of
   the Red Power, maybe all four.** That turns gameplay into defeating or recruiting the other
   carriers.
6. **The fixbots on the path to full awareness.** [[mifix]], [[odie]] and [[autofix-alto]] are
   involved in the path to full awareness, and possibly to influence.

## Clashes to resolve

Each idea above contradicts something compiled. Decide which side wins before compiling.

- **[[the-stabilisation-of-the-realms]]**: the protagonists never address the developers and the
  developers never see them; the games appear to have *self-stabilised*. Idea 4 has the champions
  get the devs' attention and prove a point to them.
- **[[faction-eleven-antagonist]]**: the eleventh faction is the Ancients — the four developers —
  and is "not a faction the player fights so much as one they uncover". Ideas 2–3 make it the
  infrastructure, with systems the champions fight.
- **[[the-four-developers]] Player-Facing**: "no playable sequence at the studio" and the four stay
  "presences with hands". Idea 2 puts the developers among the eleventh realm's inhabitants.
- **[[the-revelation-schedule]]**: no outside view, at any point. A realm the champions can travel to
  that holds the systems and the developers is an outside view of some kind.
- **[[the-void]]**: currently the space between realms, full of abandoned builds, which the liches
  use to hide from the developers and where none of the four knows what is happening. Idea 2 makes it
  the developers' own realm.
- **Ending wording**: the recorded ending turns on the four developers learning to lean on each
  other's strengths; idea 4 turns on the devs learning to grant agency. Both can be true, but the
  ending note needs to say which carries it.
