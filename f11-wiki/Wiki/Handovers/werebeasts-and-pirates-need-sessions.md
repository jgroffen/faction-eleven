# Handover: Werebeasts and Pirates need sessions

Two factions need their own grilling session, in this order. The Fey Folk had theirs on 2026-10-13.
What it left open is in [[the-fey-beasts-need-skills-stats-and-names]] and [[the-eleventh-realm-endgame]]. The Mystics' session (rounds 56–63) is
the model: story first, then gameplay, landing each round before asking the next.

## State

_What is already written down. Link it, do not restate it._

**Werebeasts — first, and blocking.**

- [[werebeasts]] · [[werewolf-gill]] · [[realm-09]] — identity "not yet established", no story. **Game
  style settled as [[werebeast-pack-hunt]]**: Gill leads parties of up to five hunting big monsters for
  trophies; systems not specified. Faction classes simplified (Weresnake Commander, Werefox Medic).
  Gill has a death mechanic (reverts to a puppy) and nothing else as a person.
- **A second werebeast exists:** [[weresnake-taz]], the Blue's carrier of [[the-red-power]]. She hunts
  Fey Beasts through 09↔05, keeps their heads in [[tazs-trophy-hall]], and hunts the champions after
  [[chapter-08]] ([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]]).
- **Three chapters already depend on realm-09.** [[chapter-05]] ends with the champions stepping
  through the gate into it. [[chapter-06]] is set there, meets Gill, and recruits him. [[chapter-08]]
  has Gill and Voisin arguing over the Fey hunt, and has the party following Taz through 09↔05.
- **Gill has the start of a character:** he would hunt Fey Beasts too if he could
  ([[werewolf-gill]]).
- What is settled about them: they are **not at war with anyone in the present**; their gate to
  [[realm-03]] is closed; the [[the-guardians-of-night|Guardians]] wanted them in the war and
  [[val]] never found a path in; their gargoyles are the only intact set and are in **stasis**; and the
  [[mystics]] are capturing them for Reagents ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]],
  [[the-werebeast-caverns]]).
- So the faction has a strong situation and no character. That is exactly where
  [[conjurer-voisin]] was at the start of the Mystics' session.

**Pirates — second; they have a style but no story.**

- [[pirates]] · [[captain-leon]] · [[realm-06]] — [[pirates-open-exploration]] is **settled as a style,
  content not filled in**.
- The story hooks already exist and point at them hard: [[wraith-delahaye]] is trying to get home to
  realm-06 to kill [[captain-d-artigue]]; [[the-pirate-institute-war]]; [[tortuga]]; and
  [[chapter-07]] puts Delahaye one realm away.
- **Free will protects against glitches** ([[continuity-glitches]]): [[captain-leon]] seems protected
  and keeps more of his gameplay abroad, and a glitch forced on a character in realm-06 makes them aware
  and protected. The Pirates' story now carries part of the end game ([[the-eleventh-realm-endgame]]).
