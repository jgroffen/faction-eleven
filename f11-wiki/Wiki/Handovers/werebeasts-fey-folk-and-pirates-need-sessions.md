# Handover: Werebeasts, Fey Folk and Pirates need sessions

Three factions need their own grilling session, in this order. The Mystics' session (rounds 56–63) is
the model: story first, then gameplay, landing each round before asking the next.

## State

_What is already written down. Link it, do not restate it._

**Werebeasts — first, and blocking.**

- [[werebeasts]] · [[werewolf-gill]] · [[realm-09]] — identity "not yet established", no story, game
  style recorded as **RPG, unconfirmed**. Gill has a death mechanic (reverts to a puppy) and nothing
  else.
- **Two chapters already depend on realm-09.** [[chapter-05]] ends with the champions stepping through
  the gate into it; [[chapter-06]] is set there, meets Gill, and recruits him.
- What is settled about them: they are **not at war with anyone in the present**; their gate to
  [[realm-03]] is closed; the [[the-guardians-of-night|Guardians]] wanted them in the war and
  [[val]] never found a path in; their gargoyles are the only intact set and are in **stasis**; and the
  [[mystics]] are capturing them for Reagents ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]],
  [[the-werebeast-caverns]]).
- So the faction has a strong situation and no character. That is exactly where
  [[conjurer-voisin]] was at the start of the Mystics' session.

**Fey Folk — second.**

- [[fey-folk]] · [[phoenix-ember]] · [[realm-05]] — game style **bullet-hell / 1941, unconfirmed and
  explicitly not locked down** ([[faction-genre-mechanics]]).
- They are the realm nobody attacked, and the reason is somebody else's fear
  ([[the-guardians-of-night]], [[the-realm-barriers]]). [[realm-09]]'s barrier facing them is the one
  still standing.

**Pirates — third; they have a style but no story.**

- [[pirates]] · [[captain-leon]] · [[realm-06]] — [[pirates-open-exploration]] is **settled as a style,
  content not filled in**.
- The story hooks already exist and point at them hard: [[wraith-delahaye]] is trying to get home to
  realm-06 to kill [[captain-d-artigue]]; [[the-pirate-institute-war]]; [[tortuga]]; and
  [[chapter-07]] puts Delahaye one realm away.
