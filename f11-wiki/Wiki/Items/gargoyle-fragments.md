# Gargoyle Fragments

**Pieces of other gargoyles**, which [[gargoyle]] can use to restore himself. They are the whole of
his progression system: every ability he gains, he takes from the broken body of one of his own kind
([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]).

**Type:** collectible · **Rarity:** rare

## Effect

Each fragment restores a piece of what the hammer took — mobility first, then the stone abilities in
[[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]], up the ladder from stationary stealth to Gargoyle Legacy. Gains are
**permanent**; he never loses one.

Also called **Gargoyle lore fragments**, because the two jobs are one object: **restoring himself
and learning what he is are the same act.** There is no separate codex.

## How It's Obtained

**From the dead.** Gargoyles are **very rare** and scattered around [[realm-09]] **in important
places** — a map of where the realm's defences once stood, and therefore a map of what the realm
once feared. **All of them have had their heads smashed.** Some still hold fragments he can use.

The first three come in the opening minutes ([[the-waking-of-the-last-gargoyle]]):

1. He wakes able to **barely move**, and finds **some of his own fragments** nearby, which restore
   him somewhat.
2. Inspecting the other gargoyles in the chamber, he finds another — which grants the first gargoyle
   power, **stealth without moving**.
3. **His head remains damaged**, and stays that way.

## Flavour

Not written. The design note that matters more than any flavour text: the player is picking up
**pieces of people**, at sites that were chosen because they mattered, and every upgrade is
therefore also a small act of scavenging from his own dead. Nothing in the presentation should let
that become weightless.

## Open

- Whether a fragment carries anything of the gargoyle it came from — memory, personality, a name.
  Not established, and the answer decides whether this system is melancholy or horrifying.
- **Whether his head can ever be made whole.** It is still damaged after the opening, and
  **Gargoyle Legacy** sits at the top of the ladder as the obvious candidate.
