# The Ancient Relic

[[conjurer-voisin]]'s starting equipment: a **powerful magic wand**, handed to her by a
character-creation screen that chose it without being asked
([[the-ordinary-day-of-the-conjurer]]). It is one of the old
[[allied-faction-signals|allied-faction signalling devices]], and neither she nor the player knows
that for most of a chapter.

**Type:** weapon · **Rarity:** unique · **Source:** the starting-bonus list, selected by a glitch

## The Description On The List

> *"A historic treasure of forgotten lore and unexpected purpose."*

Every word of which is literally true and reads, in a menu of starting bonuses, as flavour text.

## What It Does

**As a weapon**, it is a strong wand — the reason a Conjurer with no levels can survive her first
cave.

**As a signal**, it is the **receiving half** of [[the-wand]] in [[templar-monastery]]'s cellar.
When [[templar-will]] uses his to call the [[mystics]] ([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]), hers
**lights up and starts leading her** — first out of the cave, then out of the realm
([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]).

**It talks.** Not directly: it speaks to Voisin **through the homunculi**, which is what turns her
household of small servants into a dialogue system. An object with no mouth borrows several.

**And it chooses the route.** It leads her past the [[werebeasts]] gate in the deep caverns and on
to the Celestial one in [[the-graceful-servants]]' tower. It knows the difference between the two
doors.

## Why It Matters

It makes the character-creation glitch **load-bearing**. The screen did not merely railroad the
player into a class — it put the plot in their hand and labelled it as a perk. When the Templar and
Conjurer work out what the relics are, the player is holding a third one they were *given* in a
menu.

It also gives the Mystics' arrival a mechanism. [[conjurer-voisin]] being the first ally to reach
the Templar had no explanation before this; now it is simply that she is the one whose wand
answered.

## Ties

- [[the-wand]] — its counterpart, and the one that calls.
- [[allied-faction-signals]] — the class of object both belong to.
- [[conjurer-voisin]] — who carries it without knowing what it is.

## Open

- **Whether it is a fixed relic or a randomly-rolled one.** "Ancient Relic" sits in a long list of
  starting bonuses; whether that list would ever have produced a different object, and what the
  other entries are, is unwritten.
- What it looks like, and whether it resembles [[the-wand]] closely enough for the Templar to
  recognise it on sight.
- How much it knows, whether it can be wrong, and whether it can lie.
- How it came to be in a Mystic's possession at all, when its counterpart spent the disconnection
  in a Celestial cellar.
- Whether it keeps working after the two wands are in the same room.
