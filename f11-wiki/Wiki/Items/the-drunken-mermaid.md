# The Drunken Mermaid

[[captain-anne]]'s ship, on which [[wraith-delahaye]] was First Mate. Its crew held [[tortuga]].
It sailed in the pirate armada that [[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]] betrayed
([[the-pirate-institute-war]]), and what became of it after is not known.

**Type:** ship · **Rarity:** unique

## Effect

Every ship in the Pirate game has a **unique name and flag** ([[pirates-open-exploration]]); this is
the one the protagonist of another faction died serving on. Whether it is a ship the player ever
sails, boards, or finds is not written.

## How It's Obtained

Not obtained — sought. Finding out what happened to it is one of Delahaye's goals in [[realm-06]]
([[wraith-delahaye]]).

## Flavour

No in-world text yet. The name is the crew's own.

## Open

- Its flag.
- Whether it survived the armada, and whether anything called *The Drunken Mermaid* still sails a
  thousand years on — the same hull, a namesake, or nothing.
- Whether Delahaye's memories of it are a mansion room ([[the-wraith-s-mansion]]).
