# The Holy Socks

[[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]]'s ship. **It did not join the fleet** when the pirate armada sailed
against the [[institute-of-eight]]'s invasion — and its absence is how the armada realised
d'Artigue must have betrayed them ([[the-pirate-institute-war]]).

**Type:** ship · **Rarity:** unique

## Effect

Every ship in the Pirate game has a **unique name and flag** ([[pirates-open-exploration]]). This
one's name is a piece of evidence: a ship that should have been in the line and wasn't.

## How It's Obtained

Not obtained. If it or its flag is ever seen again in [[realm-06]], it is a lead on where d'Artigue
went ([[wraith-delahaye]]).
