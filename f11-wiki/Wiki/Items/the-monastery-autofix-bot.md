# The Monastery Autofix Bot

An **autofix bot** lying dormant in the ancient parts of the [[templar-monastery]], which the
[[templar]] is **able to turn back on**. One of the two devices used to signal for aid before the
retreat. **It calls the [[robots]]** — and it is heard, because by then [[autofix]] has restored
remote communications ([[the-shrine-bot-awakens]]).

**Type:** signalling device · **Rarity:** unique · **Found:** [[templar-monastery]], chapter two

## What It Does

Signals the [[robots]]. See [[allied-faction-signals]].

## Why It Matters

This is the **second** dormant robot found in someone else's realm, after the mini-fix bot in
[[ninja]]'s family shrine. Two realms, two sleeping machines from [[realm-02]]. The robots were
evidently everywhere before [[the-long-disconnection]] — which sharpens the irony of their present
state, alone and unable to repair themselves.

It also means the Robots' remote-communication ability pays off **twice**, in two different
factions' stories, in two different chapters.

## It Is Also The Second Signal

Switching it on does more than call the [[robots]]. The mini-fix bot in [[ninja]]'s shrine
**detects it too** — a second signal of its own kind, distinct from the one coming through the
cracked gate — and that detection is what sends the Ninja out of [[realm-01]]
([[the-second-signal]]).

So [[templar]] unknowingly summons two factions with one switch: the robots deliberately, and the
Institute of Eight by accident. And because the Templar keeps the bot, **it becomes an object three
protagonists are converging on**, none of them aware of the others.
