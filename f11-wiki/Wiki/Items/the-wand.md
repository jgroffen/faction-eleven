# The Wand

One of the two **ancient devices** the [[templar]] finds in the ancient parts of the
[[templar-monastery]] and uses to signal for aid before retreating. **The wand calls the
[[mystics]]** — and the mystics receive it.

**Type:** signalling device · **Rarity:** unique · **Found:** [[templar-monastery]], chapter two

## What It Does

Signals the [[mystics]] across the disconnection. See [[allied-faction-signals]].

That a **wand** is what reaches the mystics follows the pattern: each device is a piece of the
faction it calls. A wand lying in a Celestial monastery is a mystic thing in a place it does not
belong — which is itself the evidence that the realms were once in contact.
