# Azylach

A place in [[realm-03]], the [[celestials]]' realm, and the second half of [[templar-will]]'s
name — **Will of Azylach**.

**Part of:** [[realm-03]] · **Controlled by:** [[celestials]]

## The Place

**Nothing about it is designed, and it does not appear in the game.** It may never. It exists so
that the Templar's name means something — he is from somewhere, as [[knight-ermengarda]] is of
[[oluja-town]] — and not as a place the player is expected to go.

This note records that deliberately. An unplayed place with a name is worth keeping straight; it is
not a gap waiting to be filled.
