# Celestial Tower

A mage tower in [[realm-04]], held by [[the-graceful-servants]]. It is **named for what it contains**:
the interplane gate to the [[celestials]], which is known to be there. What is *not* known is
everything else the Servants keep in it.

**Part of:** [[realm-04]] · **Controlled by:** [[the-graceful-servants]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its function: the **04↔03 gate** stands inside it, the
Servants guard it, and [[conjurer-voisin]] has to **sneak in** to use it
([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]).

The gate is already open — [[coruscate]] dispelled its seal ([[the-red-power]]) — so the tower is not
a puzzle about opening a door. It is a stealth section around a door somebody else has already unlocked
and does not want used.

**Which gate is in the tower is public knowledge.** The Mystics know which gates their realm has and
where each one is; the tower is named for the one it holds. What is not public is that it has been
**unsealed**.

## How It Is Played

**As the homunculi, not as Voisin.** The player takes control of her homunculi for this section and
sends them through the tower ahead of her. **A homunculus that is caught is destroyed**, and the player
sends another — so the tension is attrition of a resource they have plenty of and can watch run down,
and every verb is one they have had since breakfast ([[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]]).

It is also the tidiest possible answer to a stealth scene in a game with no stealth: a Conjurer gets
through a tower of Summoners using the one trick Summoners invented.

## What Voisin Overhears In It

**That the gate to the [[werebeasts]] is open, and that Reagent is being stockpiled.** She is not told
and nothing is explained to her; she hears it in passing while hiding, and **infers the harvest**
([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]) — which she can do without help, because that the Mystics once
harvested other realms' creatures is common knowledge at home.

This is the single most consequential thing any Mystic learns in the game, and the player gets it as
eavesdropping while trying to leave a building.

## Why It Is Named This

The realm's practitioners call it Celestial Tower because of the Celestial gate. It is a plain,
descriptive, entirely public name for a building whose actual business is a second gate nobody is
supposed to know about. **The name is accurate and tells you nothing.**

## Open

- **Whether the Werebeast gate is also physically in the tower**, or whether the tower only holds the
  *evidence* that it is open — records, the Reagent stockpile, a way through. The caverns on Voisin's
  route pass a Werebeast gate; whether that is the same door seen from below is unresolved.
- **What exactly she overhears**, and from whom.
- **What happens if the standby pool runs out inside the tower.** It does not refill in the field
  ([[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]]), so the section has a hard floor — whether that ejects her, fails
  the attempt, or forces the fight is not designed.
- Whether the Servants notice the intrusion, and whether [[coruscate]] is present.
- What the tower looks like, how large it is, and whether the player ever returns to it.
- **A naming collision to watch:** the [[celestials]]' own game is to be called *Celestial Tower
  Defence*, and this is a tower called Celestial Tower in a different realm. Both names are good
  independently.
