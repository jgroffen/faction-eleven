# Oluja Town

A [[celestials|Celestial]] town in [[realm-03]], and **the second position the faction loses**: held
just long enough for its people to get out, then abandoned when the green-skin war machine arrives
([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]).

**Part of:** [[realm-03]] · **Controlled by:** [[celestials]]

## The Place

A town rather than a fortification — the first place in the Celestials' story that is somebody's
home rather than a position on a line. That is what the battle here is about: the defence exists to
buy an evacuation, not to hold ground.

What it looks like, how big it is, and how far it stands from [[templar-monastery]] are not
established.

## Who's Here

**[[knight-ermengarda]] is here**, and shares its name. [[templar-will]] arrives when word reaches
the monastery that the machine is coming this way, and the two of them hold the waves together —
the first time the Templar fights beside another hero unit
([[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]).

**Its people are its point.** The townsfolk are led out to [[templar-monastery]] by the Knight
while the defence buys the time, which makes them the Celestials' only civilian population on
screen and the reason the faction's second defeat reads as a success.

## What Happens Here

- **[[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]** — the defence, the evacuation, and the abandonment.
- **The town is taken**, and holding it is what [[knight-ermengarda]] leaves to undo: after the
  battle at the monastery she leads a party back to **liberate** it.

## Open

- **Whether the Knight is from here** — the name is shared, but what she is to the town, and it to
  her, is not stated.
- **What holds it** after the Celestials leave — the war machine's column, a green-skin garrison,
  or the [[the-damned|Damned]] who turn up at the monastery next.
- Whether the liberation is played, and by whom; the [[celestials]] are a faction the player plays
  as the Templar, and the party is hers.
- Where the townsfolk go after the monastery, and whether a sheltering population changes what the
  monastery's defence is protecting.
