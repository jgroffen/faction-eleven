# Realm 02 — The Robots

The home realm of the [[robots]], entry **02** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[robots]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — silver** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 02" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-01]], [[realm-03]]
- **Opposite (distant, historically enemies):** [[realm-07]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

Holds **sealed** gates the robots cannot open on their own — the discovery that drives their story.

Its neighbour [[realm-01]] is where a mini-fix bot of this realm's making ended up, kept as an ancestor spirit in a toy car.
