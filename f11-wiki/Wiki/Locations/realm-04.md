# Realm 04 — The Mystics

The home realm of the [[mystics]], entry **04** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[mystics]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — magenta** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 04" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-03]], [[realm-05]]
- **Enemy gates (three, per [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]):** [[realm-08]], [[realm-09]] (the direct opposite), [[realm-10]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

A **magic-rich** realm: a friendly above-world of villages, towers, castles and cottages over an
underworld of caves, caverns and dungeons — and possibly ruins and castles besides. Mystics go down for
**Reagents**, the material all their magic and all their shopping runs on
([[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]]). **How those spaces relate to each other is not settled**, and no
progression order among them is fixed.

## Where Its Gates Are

**Gate locations are ordinary Mystic knowledge**, and they follow a pattern that says everything about
how the realm feels about its neighbours:

| Gate | Kind | Where it is |
|---|---|---|
| [[realm-03]] [[celestials]] | allied | **[[celestial-tower]]** — a structure built around it |
| [[realm-05]] [[fey-folk]] | allied | a structure built around it; unplaced |
| [[realm-09]] [[werebeasts]] | enemy | **[[the-werebeast-caverns]]**, deep underground — and still called that |
| [[realm-08]] [[the-damned]] | enemy | **catacombs**, underground; unplaced |
| [[realm-10]] [[aliens]] | enemy | underground; unplaced |

**Allied gates get a building. Enemy gates get put below ground and left.** A Mystic knows roughly the
way to any of them — finding the exact spot may take searching, but not asking.

**Two are open, and both were opened from the inside — recently.** [[coruscate]] unsealed the enemy gate
to the [[werebeasts]], which [[the-graceful-servants]] use for the harvest
([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]), and the allied gate to the [[celestials]] in
[[celestial-tower]], which they guard. **Neither unsealing is public knowledge** — which is the whole
secret, since the doors themselves never were.

## Open

  sits at ring-distance three where the network has nothing
  ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]). Realm-04's third enemy gate is to the [[aliens]], and it is
  recorded as theirs above. Needs confirming.
