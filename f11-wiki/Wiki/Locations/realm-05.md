# Realm 05 — The Fey Folk

The home realm of the [[fey-folk]], entry **05** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[fey-folk]]

## The Place

Not yet described beyond its shape: zones of maps laced with **Ley Lines** that cross at **Ley
Knots**, and a **Prime Knot** under each zone's lair ([[the-fey-and-the-fell]],
[[fey-ley-cleansing]]). It is mostly a place to pass through between realms and a stage for story
events. What is established is its **signature colour — pale lavender** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 05" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-04]], [[realm-06]]
- **Enemy gates (three, per [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]):** [[realm-09]], [[realm-10]] (the direct opposite), [[realm-01]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

**Its gate to [[realm-09]] is open**, unsealed from the other side by [[weresnake-taz]], who comes
through to hunt Fey Beasts for trophies. [[phoenix-ember]] guards it from this side
([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]]).
