# Realm 06 — The Pirates

The home realm of the [[pirates]], entry **06** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[pirates]]

## The Place

**A series of islands**, loosely based on the Caribbean islands where pirates were active. Its
**signature colour is deep indigo**, which stands in for the mood, feel and art style that make
this realm unlike the other nine ([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]). [[tortuga]] is one of the islands —
[[wraith-delahaye]]'s home, held in her day by [[captain-anne]]'s crew.

It is played as an open world with 4X elements ([[pirates-open-exploration]]), in which **every ship
has a unique name and flag** — [[the-drunken-mermaid]] and [[the-holy-socks]] are the two named so
far.

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 06" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-05]], [[realm-07]]
- **Enemy gates (three, per [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]):** [[realm-10]], [[realm-01]] (the direct opposite), [[realm-02]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

Which other islands there are, and which real ones they draw on, is not established.
