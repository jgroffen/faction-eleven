# Realm 07 — The Werebeasts

The home realm of the [[werebeasts]], entry **07** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[werebeasts]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — tan** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 07" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-06]], [[realm-08]]
- **Opposite (distant, historically enemies):** [[realm-02]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

Has an open gate to [[realm-03]], through which the werebeasts attack in small numbers with very powerful units.
