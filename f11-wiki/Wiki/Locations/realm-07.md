# Realm 07 — The Green Skins

The home realm of the [[green-skins]], entry **07** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[green-skins]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — dark green** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 07" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-06]], [[realm-08]]
- **Enemy gates (three, per [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]):** [[realm-01]], [[realm-02]] (the direct opposite), [[realm-03]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

The realm with the most traffic out of it: green-skins press into [[realm-03]], [[realm-04]] and
[[realm-01]] — the same three enemies as before the faction's realm number changed. **Open:** the
specific gate that lets them reach [[realm-01]] was justified as "two places round the ring" when
Green Skins sat at realm-09; at four places from realm-01, that justification no longer holds
arithmetically and is pending a new account of which realms a gate can join at all. Why they are
expanding at all has a partial answer — they are **being manipulated and used**
([[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]]) — though by whom is unwritten.

## As A Playable Space

Realm 07 is a **Metroid map**: one gated space opened by acquiring abilities
([[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]). Under [[realm-governs-game-style]] it is played by **two
protagonists** — [[gargoyle]] at home in it, and [[ninja]] passing through in [[chapter-02]] — each
with their own ability ladder and their own upgrade nodes, the Gargoyle's **lore fragments** and the
Ninja's **challenge rooms**.

That double use is deliberate: it is what pays for the most content-hungry genre on the roster.
