# Realm 08 — The Damned

The home realm of the [[the-damned]], entry **08** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[the-damned]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — dark grey** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 08" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-07]], [[realm-09]]
- **Enemy gates (three, per [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]):** [[realm-02]], [[realm-03]] (the direct opposite), [[realm-04]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

Connected to alien space by a **void** in which ancient Lovecraftian gods dwell. High-level liches here are **opening** gates into [[realm-10]] — the only faction shown deliberately making new ways through.

**It is the world map of the Damned's game** ([[damned-undead-kingdom-rts]]) — the board an undead
kingdom expands across — and one of the two spaces the wraith minigame is played in
([[wraith-memory-puzzle]]), the other being [[the-wraith-s-mansion]], which is not a place in the
realm but a representation of the [[wraith-delahaye]]'s mind. The Wraith's grave, her single spawn
point, is here; once she breaks out of the minigame, the realm's map is what she searches
([[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]) — and [[val-s-castle]] is where the search leads.

It is also **where the Black's AI experiment lives** ([[the-lich-experiment]]): the one realm holding
characters who can read the rules their world runs on, and who left it to hide from the gods.
