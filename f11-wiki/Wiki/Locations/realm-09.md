# Realm 09 — The Green Skins

The home realm of the [[green-skins]], entry **09** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[green-skins]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — dark green** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 09" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-08]], [[realm-10]]
- **Opposite (distant, historically enemies):** [[realm-04]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

The realm with the most traffic out of it: green-skins press into [[realm-03]], [[realm-04]] and
[[realm-01]]. Why they are expanding now has a partial answer — they are **being manipulated and
used** ([[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]]) — though by whom is unwritten.

## As A Playable Space

Realm 09 is a **Metroid map**: one gated space opened by acquiring abilities
([[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]). Under [[realm-governs-game-style]] it is played by **two
protagonists** — [[gargoyle]] at home in it, and [[ninja]] passing through in [[chapter-02]] — each
with their own ability ladder and their own upgrade nodes, the Gargoyle's **lore fragments** and the
Ninja's **challenge rooms**.

That double use is deliberate: it is what pays for the most content-hungry genre on the roster.
