# Realm 10 — The Aliens

The home realm of the [[aliens]], entry **10** in the Ancients' catalogue of the realms.

**Part of:** [[the-wheel]] · **Controlled by:** [[aliens]]

## The Place

Not yet described. What is established is its **signature colour — light green** — which stands in
for the mood, feel and art style that make this realm unlike the other nine
([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]).

**The faction's own name for this place is not yet authored.** "Realm 10" is the Ancients'
number, which is canon but is not what the people who live here call home.

## Position On The Wheel

- **Neighbours (near, historically friendly):** [[realm-09]], [[realm-01]]
- **Opposite (distant, historically enemies):** [[realm-05]]

See [[the-wheel-of-realms]].

Home to **many alien species** rather than one people. Being invaded from [[realm-08]] through a void, by gates its inhabitants did not open.
