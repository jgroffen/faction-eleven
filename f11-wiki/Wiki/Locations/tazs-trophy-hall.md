# Taz's Trophy Hall

Where [[weresnake-taz]] keeps what she has taken, in [[realm-09]]. Among her trophies are **the heads
of the Fell Beasts and Fey Beasts** she has taken from [[realm-05]] ([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]]).

**Part of:** [[realm-09]] · **Controlled by:** [[werebeasts]]

## The Place

Not yet described.

## What Is Kept Here

**The heads cannot be put back.** Taking one is as if some of the Fey Beast code had been removed from
the Fey Folk's realm. Nothing in realm-05's [[the-setting-statement|Setting Statement]] can restore it,
and neither [[the-continuity-system]] nor [[the-retcon-engine]] can repair it ([[the-fey-and-the-fell]]).
The hall holds a permanent scar on another realm.

## Why The Player Comes Here

**The champions have to go there at some point, to confront or recruit Taz.** This is where her story
with them comes to a head.

## Ties

- [[weresnake-taz]] — whose hall it is.
- [[werebeast-pack-hunt]] — trophies are the point of the werebeasts' sport.
- [[phoenix-ember]] — the trophy she says was stolen from her.
