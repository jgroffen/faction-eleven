# The Ancient Ruin

A ruin in [[realm-01]] holding an **old abandoned gate** — and that gate is **already cracked**. It
faces [[realm-09]], the [[green-skins]].

**Part of:** [[realm-01]] · **Controlled by:** nobody

## The Place

Abandoned, unguarded, and forgotten — the opposite of the gate the [[institute-of-eight]] sentences
a family to hold. Two gates in one realm, and the Institute watches the wrong one: they guard the
door to their historic friends while the door to a hostile realm stands cracked open in a ruin
nobody visits.

The Ninja is led here in chapter two, when the shrine's mini-fix bot detects **a second mini-fix
signal** — one that is not coming through the cracked guardian gate ([[the-second-signal]]).

## Why It Matters

It is the Ninja's way out of [[realm-01]], and the route to [[gargoyle]]. It also proves gates are
**individual doors** rather than one shared seal — this one has been cracked all along and has
never let the robot signal through, because it does not face [[realm-02]].
