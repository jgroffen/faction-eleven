# The Outpost

A [[celestials]] outpost in [[realm-03]], and the whole of the Templar's chapter-one appearance.
It falls.

**Part of:** [[realm-03]] · **Controlled by:** [[celestials]]

## The Place

Not yet described beyond its function: a small defensible position, the frame for **a very simple
tower defence** ([[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]) that the player is meant to lose on the last
wave, when the siege machine arrives.

Being an *outpost* does useful work — it says the Celestials hold ground beyond their walls, and
that losing it is a retreat rather than a rout. What the outpost is guarding, and how far it sits
from [[templar-monastery]], is unwritten.
