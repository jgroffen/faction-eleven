# The Werebeast Caverns

The deep caverns in [[realm-04]] that hold the **enemy gate to [[realm-09]]**. Everyone in the Mystic
realm knows they are there — **the place is still called the Werebeast Caverns** — and nobody has had
reason to go for a thousand years. [[the-graceful-servants]] guard them now.

**Part of:** [[realm-04]] · **Controlled by:** [[the-graceful-servants]]

## The Place

Deep underground, in the pattern realm-04 uses for all its enemy gates
([[realm-04]]): allied gates get a building, enemy gates get put below ground and left. The
caverns are **large enough to search** — [[conjurer-voisin]] knows which caverns hold the gate but not
exactly where in them it is.

**The name is the signpost.** Nobody renamed the place after the war ended, so a thousand years later
the way to the werebeasts is still labelled.

## What Is Happening In Them

**The harvest's near end.** Harvesting only works inside the Mystic realm
([[cross-realm-power-and-progression]]), so the Servants' incursions do not harvest werebeasts in
[[realm-09]] — they **bring them back through the gate** and harvest them here.

And it is not subtle once you are looking at it: **wagons of Reagent run out of the caverns** to the
Servants' stockpile. Guards, a gate, a supply line, and cargo.

## Why They Matter

**They are the second lock on the road to [[realm-09]]**, and the [[the-realm-champions|Realm
Champions]] have to get through them. The party's route runs [[celestial-tower]] → [[realm-04]] →
these caverns → the gate ([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]), and the Servants hold both
ends of it.

They are also where the champions stop taking Voisin's word for the harvest and see it.
