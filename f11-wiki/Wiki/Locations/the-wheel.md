# The Wheel

The multiverse itself: ten realms in a ring, each holding one faction, joined by gates the
Ancients built. Every other location in the game sits inside one of its ten realms.

**Part of:** nothing — this is the outermost place · **Controlled by:** nobody, though
[[faction-eleven-antagonist]] built its gates and set its numbers

## The Place

Not a place you stand in so much as the arrangement of all the places there are. Its shape is
described in [[the-wheel-of-realms]]; its nature in [[the-multiverse-of-realms]].

Whether the Wheel has an *interior* — a between-space the gates pass through — is unrecorded, and
matters: [[the-damned]]'s liches open gates via a **void** where ancient gods dwell, which may be
that interior, or may be something else entirely.
