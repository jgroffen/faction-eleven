# The Wraith's Mansion

A mansion that is a **representation of the [[wraith]]'s mind and memories** — one of the two spaces
[[the-damned]]' story is played in, the other being [[realm-08]] itself.

**Part of:** — · **Controlled by:** —

## The Place

Not a place in the realm. The mansion is the protagonist turned inside out: its rooms are memories,
and the [[wraith-memory-puzzle]] is played by opening them. What it looks like, how it is laid out
and how it changes as the Wraith recovers is not yet described.

It has no `parent` because nothing establishes it as standing anywhere in realm-08 — it is a
representation, and how the player enters it is not stated.

## Who's Here

The [[wraith]]. Whether anyone else is — remembered people from her life as a Pirate First
Mate ([[pirates]]), say — is not yet defined.
