# Tortuga

An island of the Pirate realm — [[wraith-delahaye]]'s **home island**, held in her lifetime by
[[captain-anne]] and the crew of [[the-drunken-mermaid]].

**Part of:** [[realm-06]] · **Controlled by:** [[pirates]]

## The Place

Not yet described. [[realm-06]] is a series of islands loosely based on the Caribbean islands where
pirates were active, and Tortuga is one of them — the name is the real one. What it looks like, how
big it is, and what "controlled" meant for a captain and crew is open.

## Who's Here

- [[captain-anne]] and [[the-drunken-mermaid]]'s crew — who held it, a thousand years ago.
- Whoever holds it now. **Whether that is the descendants of Delahaye's crew** is one of the things
  she is going home to find out.

## Notes

**The end of Delahaye's road, or one of them.** Revenge on [[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]] is one
goal; the other is Tortuga — what happened to her crew and her ship, and whether her home is still
her crew's ([[wraith-delahaye]]). She does not know what she will find.

  world lets the player take.
