# 2026-08-08 — Interview: Faction Eleven lore and design (round 4)

## Details

Adopted 'realm' as the canonical term, renaming 14 notes and keeping 'reality' as aliases. Added faction-design-status recording review depth per faction and the agreement not to invent the five unsettled factions.
