# 2026-08-08 — Interview: Faction Eleven lore and design (round 5)

## Details

Settled the Institute of Eight: ninja-rhythm-platforming, a Super Meat Boy-like skill platformer with a Bit.Trip Beat rhythm layer where music beats hint at inputs and the Ninja's own actions sound against them; training sessions teach timing and new moves. The prototype's rogue-like label is dropped. Chapter one carries the Ninja in genuinely reduced form (one boss-fight/defeat/train cycle) and the gate does not open until all factions are introduced, making the Institute's climax a late-game turning point. Added the-first-signal, the first quest note and chapter one's spine. Flagged the conflict between the generational death fiction and instant respawns.
