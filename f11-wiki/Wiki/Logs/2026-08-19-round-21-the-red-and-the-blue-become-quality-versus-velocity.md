# 2026-08-19 — Round 21: the Red and the Blue become quality versus velocity

## Details

Replaced the Red and Blue's motivations. The old axis (content and connection versus systems and pressure) is superseded by a developer failure pair: the Red is a perfectionist who builds big elegant complicated systems, polishes the rock, scope-creeps and cannot ship; the Blue makes big sweeping untested changes with no thought for side-effects and gets more done at the cost of quality. Neither is a bad developer and they are best working together - which is now recorded as the mechanism of the ending. Added seniority and demographics: the Black and White are senior developers (late 30s and late 40s), the Red and Blue are regular devs in their late 20s; the Red is female, the other three male. Seniority is load-bearing - it explains why the two younger devs sneaked changes in rather than proposing them, so every present-day catastrophe is an unreviewed commit by someone without the standing to ask. Corrected the Red's pronouns throughout. Added avatars as a third evidence channel: each dev's colour appears in their avatar at work, their avatar inside the games, and in leaked fragments of lore and the real world that characters can find - recorded as a new glitch kind in continuity-glitches and a section in the-four-developers. Also mapped the craft failings onto the existing god domains: Xipe-Totec as smith, seasons and the flayed skin regrown reads as refinement; Huitzilopochtli as sacrifice and bloodletting reads as spending things to get a result.
