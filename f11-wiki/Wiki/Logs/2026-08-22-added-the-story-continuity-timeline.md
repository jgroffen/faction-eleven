# 2026-08-22 — Added the story continuity timeline

## Details

New topic note story-continuity-timeline tracks four things on one surface across pre-game history and chapters 1-10: which factions have gameplay, key events, counts of subtle and strong continuity glitches, and god actions. Chapter notes remain the source of truth for faction balance and beats; the timeline owns the glitch budget and the god-action schedule, which are cross-chapter by nature. continuity-glitches gains a subtle/strong loudness tier, independent of the five kinds - any kind can be authored at either tier, subtle can be dense, strong is rationed against deniability. The page records gaps rather than inventing counts: no glitch instance exists in any genre, and only one god action is on record in the present day, which the note names as a scheduling problem against the-revelation-schedule. Deeper work on glitch causation - mechanical leakage between the ten games, versus ten sets of lore reconciling into one narrative - is deferred to the glitch-causes-and-manifestation handover.
