# 2026-08-22 — Corrected: glitches date from the Linking, not chapter two

## Details

The claim that chapter two is where glitches must start was an unsourced inference and contradicted the interview twice. The author's round 20 answer states that connecting the games caused over half of them to develop issues, some never fixed, and that the White added protections to stop code from other games getting in and breaking them further. The round 20 answer on the four undeclined factions states the Institute of Eight - realm 01, chapter one's realm - still has lore glitches that the lore keeps adjusting to fit. Glitches therefore date from the linking and were never cured; the sealing reduced spread without curing instability. The reasoning was also internally inconsistent: continuity-glitches establishes that the player is the detector and protagonists mostly cannot see glitches, then justified a chapter-two start by pointing at the only protagonist who can. Gargoyle is now recorded as the tutorial for the mechanic - the first character who can name a contradiction out loud - not the first instance of one. Corrected in continuity-glitches, story-continuity-timeline, chapter-02 and the glitch-causes-and-manifestation handover. Also noted in that handover: the mechanical-leakage cause is present in the interview but has never been compiled, and the Red's motive is recorded too narrowly.
