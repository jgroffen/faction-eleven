# 2026-08-22 — Round 22: the studio's pressure is internal, and the four are friends

## Details

Withdrew the round-19 framing of off-screen commercial pressure. There are no publishers, funding cliffs, deadlines or business problems in the story. The four developers are friends who built the games and put the company together themselves, so there is nothing above them at all - the ladder does not merely stop at four, there is no rung to stop at. New fact: the games are actively destabilising and getting worse, and the four cannot agree how to fix it. The disagreement has grown bad enough that the group may break up, and the friendship rather than the business is what is at stake. This upgrades the Black's position from preference to diagnosis, gives the White a second thing he is losing for the same reason he is losing the first, and reframes the Black-White arch-enmity as a friendship that soured. The player progressing the story is what helps the four sort it out. Design consequence recorded: the rate of continuity glitches climbs across the chapters because the world genuinely is coming apart, so density is a storytelling instrument rather than a difficulty setting. Real-project notes that legitimately discuss commercial intent - chapter-based-release, godot-as-engine, project-scope-and-constraints - were left untouched.
