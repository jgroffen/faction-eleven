# 2026-08-22 — Round 23: the four are still friends, and their differences are the solution

## Details

Corrected the previous round's compiled inference. The Black and White's enmity is not a friendship that soured - all four are still friends, met through work or study, and built the games and company together. Their differing approaches were never the problem; rising stakes turned difference into conflict. The whole thing is present tense: nothing has broken yet, which is why there is still something for the protagonists to save. Recorded the three stakes as one stake at three distances - the games destabilising, the company going under, the friend group breaking up - each following from the last, with the company failing as a consequence rather than an external business problem. Added the resolution mechanism in full: compromise and collaboration, with each of the four named as the correction to somebody else (the Red's brake for the Blue's push, the Black's willingness to tear down against the White's care for what exists). It is all or nothing - they succeed together or lose company and friendships both. Recorded the structural identity this creates: ten factions uniting and holding a balance is the same move as four friends compromising, made twice at two scales, which is what lets the in-world goal and the goal beneath it converge in one ending rather than merely rhyme.
