# 2026-08-22 — Round 24: the revelation schedule, and two motives corrected

## Details

Corrected the compiled account of how the Black and White acted. Neither overrode anyone. The Black argued for linking the games because they had gone stagnant and connecting them would liven up gameplay, and he won the argument; the White argued for closing the links to stabilise the games, and won his. Both were open, good-faith proposals that carried the room. This makes the Red's sneaked-in change the first illegitimate act in the entire history and the catalyst for the Blue's much larger ones - the process worked until it didn't, and it broke at the point where someone stopped feeling able to ask. Removed the earlier claim that seniority let the Black simply decide. Also recorded that after the sealing both failures compound: instability was contained rather than cured, and containment produced stagnation on top of it, so each god watches the thing he warned about get worse. New topic note the-revelation-schedule captures the perception model and pacing: the player sees everything through the eyes of characters inside the games, who read dev actions and arguments as divine acts, and the mystery climbs two ladders at once - identity (nothing, two gods at war with two switching sides, four gods, four developers) and motive (simple and malevolent or protective, then nuanced). Added the two-gods-at-war stage to the layer table in faction-eleven-antagonist and to the-four-gods, since it was missing and is the reading the realms actually hold.
