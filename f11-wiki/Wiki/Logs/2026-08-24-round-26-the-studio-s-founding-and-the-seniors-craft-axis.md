# 2026-08-24 — Round 26: the studio's founding, and the seniors' craft axis

## Details

The Black built an open world, persistent game system that evolves its own gameplay and could not stabilise it; the White stabilised it; the two of them then made game worlds on it and hired the Red and the Blue to contribute worlds of their own. The studio therefore has two founders and two hires, which revises the compiled claim that the four built the games and the company together - corrected in the-four-developers, faction-eleven-antagonist, tezcatlipoca-the-black and quetzalcoatl-the-white. The seniority table is now a history rather than a ranking, and the account of why the Red and Blue went around the process is structural rather than temperamental: they were hired to contribute worlds on an engine they did not build, in a company that was not theirs. New craft axis for the two seniors, alongside the existing quality-versus-velocity axis for the two juniors: the Black is an early adopter and tinkerer who has the ideas but starts many things and finishes none; the White learns deeply, stays with known approaches, deep-dives implementations and course-corrects architecture, but is slow to change and slow to adopt. The Black would never have finished the engine and the White would never have started it. Recorded as inference, not established: the founding is the linking and the sealing in miniature. Recorded as unconfirmed in the-retcon-engine: a system that evolves its own gameplay may be the mechanism behind lore that adjusts itself to fit, which would make the retcon engine the Black's engine and a feature rather than a fault. Left open: what the Red's and the Blue's stake is, given the three stakes are written as though all four own them equally.
