# 2026-08-24 — Round 27: the Continuity System, and the Red and Blue bought in

## Details

Two resolutions. First, the round-26 candidate connection was wrong: the retcon engine is not the Black's self-evolving engine. It is the White's, introduced to stabilise the realms after the linking. A second and earlier system is now named - the Continuity System, also the White's, introduced to stabilise the Black's original persistent self-evolving world engine. It tracks the world's evolution into key continuity events and rejects any continuity-breaking events. New lore note the-continuity-system records both systems and the prevention-versus-mitigation split between them; the-retcon-engine's unconfirmed section is replaced with the correct attribution. The White now has three stabilisation layers built at three times on the Black's engine: the Continuity System at the founding, the retcon engine after the linking, and the gate guardians recently. This gives continuity-glitches a mechanism it never had - a glitch is the Continuity System failing, being bypassed by a change made outside the process it inspects, or being overruled by a god's edit - and makes the term diegetic rather than descriptive. Second, the Red and the Blue were approached rather than hired: they were offered investment and ownership and agreed, so all four are owners. This corrects the round-26 compiled line that they worked in a company that was not theirs, and closes the open stake question - all three stakes land on all four. Their position is ownership without seniority. Friendship topology recorded: White and Red were prior colleagues and friends, Black and White were prior colleagues and friends, Black and Blue were prior colleagues and friends - which supplies the cause for the alignments the compiled notes previously left as preference.
