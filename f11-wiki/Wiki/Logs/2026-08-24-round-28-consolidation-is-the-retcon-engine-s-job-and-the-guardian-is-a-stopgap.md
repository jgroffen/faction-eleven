# 2026-08-24 — Round 28: consolidation is the retcon engine's job, and the guardian is a stopgap

## Details

Corrects the interviewer's round-27 guess at why the Continuity System stopped being sufficient. It did not fail and it was not overruled by the gods' edits - that explanation is withdrawn. It refuses continuity errors as they occur and worked flawlessly while the worlds were disconnected. The linking presented it with no event to reject: ten already-established, individually valid continuities that now had to be one, a contradiction inherited retroactively rather than committed. A prevention layer has nothing to bite on there. The retcon engine's purpose is therefore consolidation - retroactively rewriting history to force ten continuities into a single narrative - and covering an individual divine edit is a case of that job rather than the whole of it. The consolidation never finishes, and the seams are the game's evidence trail. This mechanises the second half of the deferred glitch-causation brief, which described lore trying to reconcile into a single narrative; the handover now carries the remaining question of whether the engine is still converging, re-runs per edit, or settled long ago. The gate guardian is reframed as a stopgap and a sign of the White's frustration: a quick mitigation against a failure he can see coming, buying time to push for a longer-term fix. He is therefore not arguing for nothing, as the compiled note had it - he is arguing for time and for a proper fix rather than a rebuild. Recorded as inference: his three layers are a degradation curve, and shipping an unexamined stopgap with a catastrophic side effect is the failure mode the notes attribute to the Blue.
