# 2026-08-24 — Round 29: the retcon cascade is the cause of the glitches

## Details

The retcon engine's operating model is now recorded, and it supplies the glitch causation the design has been missing. It is reactive - it can only apply a retcon once a continuity error is detected, and only to that error. Its scope is local - it never has the full scope of every realm history when it chooses a change. And the repair is violent - brute-forcing changes into settled history is itself a cause of glitches and instability. So a fix that solves the detected problem can seed unforeseen errors in realms whose histories were never compatible, which are then detected and fixed in turn. The system built to end contradictions is a generator of them, still running, and diverging rather than converging - which answers the question left open in round 28. Three consequences compiled. First, this is why instability keeps climbing through a thousand years of sealing: disconnection stopped new contact but did nothing about the tangled histories already inside or the machine grinding at them, so it is a feedback loop rather than decay. Recorded in the-long-disconnection and the-four-developers. Second, the design rule that a realm's account is airtight from inside and contradicts the realm next door is no longer asserted - it is the necessary output of a repair mechanism with one realm's scope, and continuity-glitches now derives it. Glitch density should accelerate rather than rise steadily, updated in continuity-glitches and story-continuity-timeline. Third, characterisation: the White's own second stabilisation layer is the main engine of the decline he is fighting and he does not know it, and the Black's beyond-repair position becomes technically correct in one specific way - the repair mechanism is the damage. The lore-reconciliation half of the deferred glitch brief is now closed on causation; what a glitch looks like in each genre remains open.
