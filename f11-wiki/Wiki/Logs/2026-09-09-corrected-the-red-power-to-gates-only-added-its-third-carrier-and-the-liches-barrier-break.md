# 2026-09-09 — Corrected the Red Power to gates-only, added its third carrier and the liches' barrier-break

## Details

The Red Power (Xipe-Totec-the-Red's gift to Ninja and Val) was miswritten in ninja.md and val.md as covering both gates and barriers, with odie naming Ninja's version as breaching barriers specifically. Corrected: it opens gates only, never a realm barrier, in all three carriers. Centralized in a new lore note, the-red-power, covering all three manifestations (Ninja breaks a gate, Val unlocks one, an unnamed Mystic dispels one) and the third, unnamed Mystic carrier (new). Also newly recorded: the liches of the-damned are the only ones who can break a realm barrier directly, without disabling the gargoyle first, added to the-void and cross-linked from the-realm-barriers. Updated ninja, val, xipe-totec-the-red, mystics, sealed-interplane-gates, the-cracked-gate, the-gate-guardian, the-realm-barriers, the-void.
