# 2026-09-12 — Round 36 — the setting statement, the lich experiment, and the purge that never happened

## Details

Wrote the engine layer the wiki had never recorded: every realm is a different game on one engine, each defined by its own Setting Statement, and no character — protagonists included — is aware of any of it. Their code self-corrects foreign content into their home realm's terms, which causes cross-realm imbalance and four system-level failure modes: hard-retcon, bug, freeze, crash.

New lore note the-setting-statement carries this. New lore note the-lich-experiment splits the liches' origin and nature out of the-void: AI agents instead of deterministic code, enough access to read the engine and their realm's Setting Statement, rule bypass, self-healing via behaviour-code edits, immunity to the retcon engine (they detected it editing them and hardened against external change), and the decision to hide from the developers — which is what sent them into the void.

Removed an event the wiki had treated as fact. robots, autofix, autofix-skill-tree and no-machine-repairs-itself all asserted the Creators destroyed the autofix line 'out of fear of losing control'. The purge never happened: it is a retcon covering the fixbots' real failure — code-awareness degrading under exposure to another realm's Setting Statement — and the generated history supplies no motive at all. The missing motive is now recorded as a seam, and as the retcon engine's second worked example.

Answered autofix's three standing mysteries. He froze rather than crashed, on a paradox in his logic loop hit when he tried to fix a foreign object from another realm; mifix's activity log resolves it, teaching him 'you can fix things that aren't robots if you study them enough — if you don't know how to fix them yet, don't try'. Mifix was built by this Autofix.

Chapter one restructured from two beats to three: crack, wake, answer. the-first-signal ran Autofix to Mifix and protected 'Nothing wakes'; that is retired. The chain is now strictly ordered, which leaves chapter-01 a new open question about how much of realm-02 is playable before the wake. Quantum Comms, the two-condition dependency and the-second-signal all survive.

Closed the open half of the glitch-causes-and-manifestation handover — 'the mechanical-leakage cause in full, nothing exists' — leaving concrete glitch instances as the remaining work. realm-governs-game-style left untouched as an immutable accepted ADR.
