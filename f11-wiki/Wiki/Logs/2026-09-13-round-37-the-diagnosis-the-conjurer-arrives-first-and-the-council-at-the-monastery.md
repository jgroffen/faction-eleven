# 2026-09-13 — Round 37 — the diagnosis, the Conjurer arrives first, and the council at the monastery

## Details

Three new story events for the stretch from the Trial into chapter three, plus four clarifications.

The diagnosis scene now opens chapter three: after Val, the Gargoyle tells the Ninja and Mifix he may be malfunctioning and cannot tell. Mifix knows he cannot fix a gargoyle but offers to diagnose him; the attempt fails and glitches Mifix — the game's first concrete on-screen glitch instance. New quest note the-diagnosis-of-the-last-gargoyle, with manifestation, duration and placement relative to the crossing left open.

The Conjurer is confirmed as the Mystics' protagonist and reaches the Templar first; the Ninja, Gargoyle and Mifix arrive second. The convergence is no longer simultaneous. New quest note the-answering-of-the-wand carries the Conjurer's arrival, almost entirely open beyond the sequencing and the relics insight.

The convergence quest gains the council: the three say they want the Robots; the Templar and Conjurer have worked out that the Templar's relics are links to ancient allies and a way to call for help, and that Mifix is the same for the Institute of Eight; Odie does not know why he is in the Celestial realm; Odie knows gates exist but not where, and the Celestials' awareness of their own gates has faded. Everyone knows the network's shape (Odie, the Gargoyle's chapter-two fragment map) and nobody knows where in realm-03 the gate to the Robots stands. They decide to seek a Werebeast gargoyle for four reasons: an intact gargoyle knows where the gates are; the Gargoyle would trust another gargoyle where he does not trust Val on whether stopping the war is what he was built for; reactivated Werebeast gargoyles should seal the gate to the Mystics; Val means to destroy them and needs warning. 'The Crusader' in the author's text is the Templar.

Deliberately not compiled: the author's sketch of a Mystic Red-Power carrier reaching the Werebeasts via Mystics→Aliens→Werebeasts, putting the Werebeast gargoyles in a stasis spell, and destroying a gargoyle barrier to the Mystic realm so the Mystics could invade. The author said 'not sure yet'. It contradicts the-realm-barriers (no 09↔04 barrier), werebeasts/gargoyle/the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles (never smashed, barriers stand) and mystics (the invaded party), so it is recorded verbatim in the the-mystics-second-secret-society handover with the three contradictions listed, and surfaces in compiled notes only as Open items. The compiled notes say the Werebeast gargoyles 'need reactivating' without a reason, which is the one word of this the author stated as settled.

Handovers: gargoyle-ninja-and-the-celestials-gate marks points 2, 4 and 5 resolved and narrows to the two crossings; the-mystics-second-secret-society takes the new sketch and its contradictions; faction-eleven-story-outline notes the first glitch instance and chapter two's settled ending.

Also fixed while there: odie and gargoyle still said the Ninja could breach realm barriers; corrected to gates only per the-red-power.
