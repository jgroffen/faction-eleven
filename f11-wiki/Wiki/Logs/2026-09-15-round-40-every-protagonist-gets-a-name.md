# 2026-09-15 — Round 40 — every protagonist gets a name

## Details

Names for nine of the ten protagonists, recorded on each character note under a new Name section, as the H1 title and as aliases; slugs are unchanged so existing wikilinks stand. Granite (the Gargoyle, male) learns his name from a fragment in the first room — added to the-waking-of-the-last-gargoyle and narrowing an open item on gargoyle-fragments. Sato Kazuma (the Ninja, male; kanji 佐藤和真 with meanings recorded) — Mifix calls him Kaz. Ermengarda of Oluja (the Templar, female) — Celestials use the honorific Sister; on celestials too. Leon (the Captain, male) — after Leon Treich, who contributed to the Delahaye stories; whether the shared legend connects Leon and Delahaye in-fiction is open on both notes. Phoenix remains unnamed.
