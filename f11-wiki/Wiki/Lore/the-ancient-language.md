# The Ancient Language

The [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] use a **braille-like written language**, with its own custom character set.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** predates the ten factions' knowledge of each other

## The Story

A script of raised dots rather than drawn letters — a language you could read without seeing it.
Left behind by the hidden faction, it is the trace the Ancients could not help leaving in every
realm they touched.

## Ties

- [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] — whose language it is.

## Player-Facing

Intended to be player-facing and **decipherable**:
building the custom character set "should be fun to make". A braille-like cipher is the classic
shape of a puzzle the player solves themselves across a whole game — the kind of thing found in
one realm and read in another.

Whether it is a genuine solvable cipher or set dressing is not yet decided.
