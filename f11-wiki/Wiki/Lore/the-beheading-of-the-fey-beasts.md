# The Beheading Of The Fey Beasts

[[weresnake-taz]] came through the 09↔05 gate to hunt, and the beasts of [[realm-05]] make great
trophies. **Her pack takes heads**, Fell Beasts and Fey Beasts alike, in a realm whose rules have no
combat and no death ([[the-fey-and-the-fell]]). The Fey Folk's game is built to let the player find
this out the hard way.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** present

## How The Player Meets It

The Fey game ([[fey-ley-cleansing]]) is deliberately simple until it isn't.

1. **Zone 3 is too easy.** Its Fell Beast is barely active and there are few Fell Folk to deal with,
   only corruption. The Fey Folk and Fell Folk **behave strangely, as if they don't know what to do any
   more.** In the lair, the Fell Beast is **already dead**, its head cut off and taken. Werebeasts are
   seen stalking at the edges of the maps. They are careful hunters.
2. **Zone 4: the ambush.** Partway through a map, Taz and a pack she leads attack. With no combat
   mechanic there is nothing to fight back with. **Any Fey Beast but the Phoenix dies**, and a cutscene
   shows its head cut off and taken as a trophy. On the select screen its slot is **greyed out, with a
   corrupted portrait**. The player picks another Fey Beast and starts zone 4 again.
3. **It's down to chance.** Which Fey Beast the player picks doesn't set the zone or the Fell Beast,
   and any of them reaches zone 4. **Only the [[phoenix-ember|Phoenix]] survives**: it fights back
   with **Cleansing Fire** and comes through death with **Rebirth**. A player who happens to be
   playing the Phoenix never sees a beheading. Everyone else loses beasts until they pick it.
4. **The game turns to hunting the werebeasts.** Zones 5 and 6 still have Fell Beasts to clear, but
   Taz's pack moves through them, leaving trails of **glitch-corruption**. Cleansing Fire drives
   werebeasts off but never kills them.
5. **The trail leads to the 09↔05 gate.** The Phoenix **routs Taz's party back through it** and stays
   to **guard the gate** against werebeast incursions. Taz is seen on screen, and she gets away.

## Why It Glitches

**Neither the Fell Beasts nor the Fey Beasts were ever meant to be in direct combat, or beheaded.** A
Fell Beast is meant to be expelled. A Fey Beast is meant to be pushed back or to "die" and come back.
The werebeasts bring their own rules: hunting, and trophies. Three things glitch at once:

- **Werebeasts are in realm-05 at all.**
- **There is combat in a realm with no combat mechanic.**
- **Beasts are beheaded.** It is as if some of the Fey Beast code had been removed from the realm.
  Neither [[the-continuity-system]] nor [[the-retcon-engine]] can repair a missing head, because
  realm-05's [[the-setting-statement|Setting Statement]] has no way of bringing one back. The heads
  hang in [[tazs-trophy-hall]].

The register entries are in [[continuity-glitches]].

## What Comes Next

**Taz retreats and plans how to beat the Phoenix.** Her new group has **high damage output** and a
**fast member to grab the ashes** before the Phoenix can be reborn. It is Rebirth she is after, not
Cleansing Fire. The Gargoyle's party follows her through the gate and saves him ([[chapter-08]]).

**She gets away.** Taz wants the Phoenix, and she sees the rescue as **the theft of a trophy she won
fairly**. Once she regroups she starts actively hunting the champions, over **two chapters and three
encounters**. In the end they have to go to [[tazs-trophy-hall]] to confront her, or recruit her.
