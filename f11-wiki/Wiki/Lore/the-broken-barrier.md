# The Broken Barrier

The barrier sealing the gate to the [[celestials]]' realm was broken — and the [[green-skins]]
believe **the Celestials broke it**, as the opening of another incursion. They are wrong. It was
broken from the inside, by [[the-guardians-of-night]], to start a war.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** the opening of the game

## The Story

**This is the game's false flag.** For centuries the barrier was the proof that the old war had
ended. Its breaking is read, immediately and by everybody, as the proof that the war has resumed —
because that is the only thing a broken barrier has ever meant ([[the-realm-barriers]]).

The green-skins are not being lied to in words. They are being shown **a piece of evidence that
lies by itself**, which is why it works so completely and why it will be so hard for one damaged
gargoyle to argue against.

**What actually followed:** the realm was unsealed so that the [[green-skins]], [[the-damned]] and
the [[werebeasts]] could **attack the Celestials** — the invasion that every other faction in the
game meets as an unprovoked horde. This is the concrete mechanism behind
[[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]], and the reason the [[celestials]]' gate stands open in
[[sealed-interplane-gates]].

**And it woke him.** The gargoyles are bound to the stone they shaped; the barrier's failure sent
their own imbued power back to them as the bolt that ends the torpor. It should have woken all of
them. It woke the one whose head the hammer failed on ([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]).

## The Chain Of Deception

Three layers, and each one believes it is the ground floor:

| Who | What they think is happening |
|-----|------------------------------|
| The [[green-skins]] rank and file | the Celestials have invaded again; we are defending ourselves |
| [[the-guardians-of-night]] | the unsealing proves the Celestials, Fey Folk and Mystics mean to invade; we are striking first |
| **an Ancient** | *unstated* — [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] is manipulating the Guardians into starting trouble |

Nobody in the chain thinks of themselves as an aggressor. That is what makes it a good conspiracy
and a hard one to unpick, and it means the Gargoyle's opponents are all, in their own eyes,
defenders — exactly like him.

## How The Gargoyle Learns It

Staged across his story, and the staging matters:

1. He wakes, sees his fellows destroyed, and concludes **the realm is under threat somehow** —
   correctly, and for entirely the wrong reasons ([[the-waking-of-the-last-gargoyle]]).
2. He discovers the **Celestial gate barrier was broken**, and that the realm's explanation is
   another Celestial incursion.
3. He uncovers that it was broken **by a group secretly working to incite** the green-skins, the
   werebeasts and the damned to invade the Celestials.

## Ties

- [[the-guardians-of-night]] — who broke it, and why they think they were right to.
- [[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]] — the thing this is the mechanism of.
- [[the-realm-barriers]] — what a barrier is and what breaking one does.
- [[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]] — the preparation.
- [[celestials]] — blamed for it, and invaded because of it.

## Player-Facing

**Yes — it is the Gargoyle's investigation.** The player is told in the cutscene that something is
being arranged, and then spends the chapter finding out what. Note the asymmetry this creates
across the game: the [[templar]] is defending a wall in [[chapter-01]] against an invasion whose
cause the player only learns in [[chapter-02]], from the other side.

## Open

- **Which barrier, and where.** The gate to [[realm-03]] is established as standing open, but the
  barrier's location in [[realm-09]] is not authored.
- **How** they broke it, given that it is gargoyle-shaped stone with gargoyle magic in it.
- Whether the Guardians understood that breaking it would wake the gargoyles, or whether smashing
  them first was luck.
