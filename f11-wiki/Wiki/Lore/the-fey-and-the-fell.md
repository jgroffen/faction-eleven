# The Fey And The Fell

[[realm-05]]'s whole world is one contest: the **Fey**, who cleanse, against the **Fell**, who corrupt.
It runs along the Ley Lines and it **never ends**. That is how [[xipe-totec-the-red]] built it.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** always

## Who Is Who

| Side | Kind | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| **Fey** | **Fey Beasts**: the guardians, the six faction classes | Stag Lord, Ent, [[phoenix-ember\|Phoenix]], Moon Hound, Unicorn, Hippogriff |
| | **Fey Folk**: small, simple, automatic units | Fairy, Pixie, Elf, Sprite, Brownie, Dryad |
| **Fell** | **Fell Folk**: they spread corruption | Gremlins, Boggarts, Redcap, Bogle, Hags, Will-o'-the-wisps |
| | **Fell Beasts**: one leads each zone | Barghest, Banshee, Kelpie, Baobhan sith, Fomorian Ettin, Wyvern |

**The kinds of Fey Folk differ only for flavour.** **No one rules them.** The Fey Beasts are their
guardians, and the **Stag Lord**, the Commander, is first among them.

The **Moon Hound** is based on the **Cŵn Annwn**, the white spectral hounds of Welsh myth with red ears.

**Each Fell Beast mirrors a Fey Beast, in theme only:**

| Fey Beast | Fell Beast |
|-----------|-----------|
| Moon Hound | Barghest |
| Unicorn | Kelpie |
| Ent | Fomorian Ettin |
| Stag Lord | Baobhan sith |
| Hippogriff | Wyvern |
| Phoenix | Banshee |

## The Loop Has No Death In It

**As designed, nobody in realm-05 is ever destroyed.**

- **A Fell Beast is never killed.** Fey Magic from the Prime Knot overwhelms it, and it is
  **expelled**.
- **A Fey Beast can be overwhelmed by spreading corruption**, pushed back down its Ley Line. If its
  health runs out, it "dies" and comes back.
- **Neither side is meant to meet the other in direct combat.** The Fey Beasts cleanse and the Fell
  Folk corrupt. There is no fight in the rules.

**So decapitation is the one thing the loop cannot undo.** A head taken as a trophy is permanent
removal, as if some of the Fey Beast code had been taken out of the realm, and nothing in realm-05's [[the-setting-statement|Setting Statement]] says how to put it back.
When werebeasts bring their own rules in, the result is a glitch
([[the-beheading-of-the-fey-beasts]], [[continuity-glitches]]).

## Crystals

**A Fey Ring slowly makes Crystals over time.** They are how the Fey Folk **store and move Magic**,
and they are the faction's currency ([[faction-currencies]]).

**They also break economies.** The [[mystics]] seek them out as great sources of Reagents. It's the
same kind of imbalance between games as the Reagents from killing werebeasts
([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). The trade ran in the old war and stopped when the gates were
sealed. It becomes a risk again only if the 04↔05 gate reopens.

## Outside Their Realm

**Fey Beasts are very powerful in other realms.** They were programmed to be great, powerful beasts.
Their own realm has no combat to show it, but other realms' rules do. **They are not inclined to leave
their realm**, though.

**Fey Power cleanses corruption, and it works very well on [[the-damned]].** The Damned learned this
in the old war, and it is why they fear the Fey ([[the-war-of-the-six-realms]]).

**The [[mystics]] can draw Reagents from Fey Beast magic without killing the beasts.** Killing a Fey
Beast causes corruption and destroys the Reagents, so the Mystics were never tempted to hunt them.
The werebeasts were the reverse case: their Reagents came from killing them.
