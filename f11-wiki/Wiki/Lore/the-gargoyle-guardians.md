# The Gargoyle Guardians

Gargoyles are **magical constructs**: dead orcs, resurrected and golem-ified, imbued with magical
power — and with a fixed set of instructions they cannot act outside of. They were made **jointly**
by **necromancers of [[realm-08]]** and **shamans of [[realm-09]]**, to defend three allied realms
against a war they were losing.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** long before the game

## The Story

**What they are made of matters.** A gargoyle is a green-skin who died and was raised as stone. The
faction's guardians are its own dead — which is why they are loyal, and why smashing them is an act
against the green-skins themselves and not merely against a set of statues.

**They are autonomous, and that was the point.** A gargoyle follows only the instructions imbued
into it. It has no commander. This was deliberate: **so that the gargoyles could not be used against
another faction.** An army that takes orders can be pointed anywhere; a construct with a fixed
purpose cannot be turned into somebody's invasion.

**The instructions:**

- They are **realm guardians**.
- They **cannot act except in defence of a realm being invaded**.
- They **cannot work against their own faction**.

**Each realm's gargoyles are loyal to that realm.** There are gargoyles in [[realm-09]], in
[[realm-08]] and in [[realm-07]] — green-skin, damned and werebeast — and each set answers to its
own home. They are not one order; they are three, built to the same design by the same two hands.

**They shape rock.** Alongside fighting, gargoyles are builders in stone — the capability that
produced [[the-realm-barriers]], and the reason a barrier breaking is something they can feel.

**Their makers were both Medics** ([[the-six-classes]]). **Necromancer** is
[[the-damned]]'s Medic class and **Shaman** is the [[green-skins]]'. The guardians were not built by
two realms' warriors or rulers but by their **healers** — the people whose role is to keep others
alive, building something out of the dead to stop a war.

**Two further abilities** sit outside the current ladder
([[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]): gargoyles **move through stone**, and **when their wings are
folded away they can appear to be flesh and bone**. The second is a *disguise* — a way to hide in
plain sight rather than by holding still — and it is the strongest unclaimed rung available.

## What They Were Built For

They were created to defend against **[[celestials]], [[mystics]] and [[fey-folk]]**, who were
"constantly incurring into" the green-skin, damned and werebeast realms. In the old world the three
allied realms were **the invaded**, not the invaders.

This inverts how every other faction in the game experiences the [[green-skins]] — as a horde
breaking on somebody's wall. The player will have been attacked by them for a whole chapter before
learning that they were, first, the ones being attacked.

The gargoyles fought the incursions back, **sealed the gateways** to the enemy realms, returned, and
were **instructed to rest until needed again**. They entered stone torpor, and the world moved on
without them. See [[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]] for what happened next.

**They know nothing of what passed while they slept.** Whatever occurred between the torpor and the
waking is simply missing — which is why [[gargoyle]] wakes with his history intact and his present
a blank.

## What They Do Not Know

**Gargoyles have no knowledge of [[faction-eleven-antagonist]].** They know the other realms —
they fought three of them — but the eleventh faction is as absent from their memory as from
everyone else's. Their knowledge is deep, and it stops exactly where the game's secret starts.

## Player-Facing

**Directly, and first.** The making of the gargoyles is the opening cutscene of the Gargoyle's
story — so the player learns what he is, and what he is forbidden to do, before controlling him.
Everything he then fails to do makes sense.
