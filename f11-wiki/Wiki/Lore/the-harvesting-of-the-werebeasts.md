# The Harvesting Of The Werebeasts

**Reagent yield scales with how magical a creature is.** That single rule, harmless inside one realm,
became an exploit the moment the realms were joined — and the [[mystics]] have acted on it twice, a
thousand years apart, against the same victim.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** the old war, and again now

## The Rule, And Why It Broke

**As designed, the Mystics' realm is a closed ladder.** Clear monsters, slowly build strength, clear
progressively harder dungeons, reach an end-game. Reagents come out of monsters at a rate the ladder
was balanced around ([[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]]).

**Then the realms were linked** ([[the-linking-of-the-realms]]) and the world filled up with
**magic-imbued beings** the ladder had never been priced against. Whoever worked out what that meant
had found a way to break the economy without breaking a rule: invade somewhere full of magical
creatures and the Reagent problem goes away forever.

**The [[werebeasts]] are the extreme case.** Harvesting a werebeast yields an **inordinate** amount of
Reagent. Realm-09 is therefore not a rival or a threat to the Mystics; it is the richest seam in the
wheel.

**This is [[the-war-of-the-six-realms]]' cause on the Mystics' side.** The Mystics were one of the
three realms "constantly incurring" into the green-skin, damned and werebeast realms, and
[[the-gargoyle-guardians]] were the answer to it. The incursions were a harvest.

## It Is Happening Again, And It Is New

[[the-graceful-servants]] found records of the yield in an archive reaching back to the linking, and
acted on them **recently**. Three steps, none of which one person could do alone:

1. **[[coruscate]] unsealed the 04↔09 gate** with [[the-red-power]], which opens gates and nothing
   else. It is in [[the-werebeast-caverns]], deep under [[realm-04]] — a place everyone knew about and
   nobody had visited in a thousand years.
2. **Other Servants got past the gargoyle barrier behind it without breaking it.** Breaking a barrier
   wakes the gargoyles bound to it ([[the-realm-barriers]]); passing it quietly does not. This took
   **unique, powerful gear** and **high-level Mystics with particular faction-class skills**.
3. **They put realm-09's gargoyles into stasis**, so the guardians could not wake later either.

**It cannot be done at scale.** Every stage needs the realm's most capable practitioners and gear that
barely exists — artefacts crafted by historically significant Mystics, held rather than bought
([[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]]). The harvest is therefore a **trickle run by specialists** — small
parties, slipping through — not an army. That is also why nobody has noticed.

**The door itself was never hidden.** Mystics know which gates their realm has and where
([[realm-04]]); the caverns that hold this one are **still called the Werebeast Caverns**. The secret is
only that the gate is now **open**.

## The Harvest Happens In Realm-04

**Reagent yield is a Mystic-realm rule and does not apply abroad**
([[cross-realm-power-and-progression]]). So the Servants cannot harvest a werebeast in [[realm-09]] —
the incursions are **captures**, and the werebeasts are brought **back through the gate** and harvested
in [[the-werebeast-caverns]].

Which is why there are **wagons**. Reagent runs out of the caverns in carts, to the stockpile. It is a
supply line, and anyone who gets as far as the caverns will see it.

**And it is the road the [[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] take.** With the Celestials' own gate to
realm-09 closed, the party reaches the werebeasts by this gate — through [[celestial-tower]] and out the
harvest route, the door they have just resolved to seal
([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]).

## What It Is For

**The Servants are stockpiling massive amounts of Reagent for a mysterious, dangerous purpose.** What
the purpose is has not been established. What is established is the scale: far more than a society of
scholars could spend on itself.

## The Record Is Skewed

**That the Mystics once harvested creatures of other realms is common knowledge in [[realm-04]].** It
is not a scandal; it is history, taught and remembered. What that history *also* says is that **the
werebeasts were the aggressors**. They were not.

This is [[continuity-glitches]]' stated rule arriving as ordinary propaganda: *a realm's account of
itself is airtight from inside and contradicts the realm next door.* No character is lying to the
player. The Mystics' written history is wrong in the direction that flatters the Mystics, every
educated Mystic believes it, and there is nothing in realm-04 to check it against.

## What Voisin Works Out

**She overhears it.** Sneaking through [[celestial-tower]] on her way out of the realm,
[[conjurer-voisin]] hears enough to know the gate to the werebeasts is open and that the people in the
tower are stockpiling Reagent. **She infers the rest** — because the old harvest is common knowledge,
she does not need to be told what a Mystic does with an open door to realm-09.

She had **never heard of [[the-graceful-servants]]**; their existence is not public.

**She does not like werebeasts and objects anyway.** She distrusts them — the histories she was
raised on cast them as the aggressors — and still concludes the harvest is **immoral**. That is the
whole of her position, and she takes it into the alliance.

## What The Player Does And Doesn't Do

**The player never harvests a werebeast.** Voisin's game has her clearing her own realm's caves. The
harvest is something she overhears, once, on her way out.

That keeps the exploit as **fiction rather than a verb**: the player spends Reagents constantly, for a
whole chapter, without once being shown where the good ones come from.

## Ties

- [[mystics-isometric-action-rpg]] — the economy the exploit exploits.
- [[the-linking-of-the-realms]] — what made it possible, argued for and won on the merits.
- [[the-secret-society-system]] — which authored the Servants, to fix Mystic stagnation, and did not
  foresee this.
- [[the-realm-barriers]] — the lock they passed without breaking.
- [[faction-genre-mechanics]] — which already holds that carrying a capability across realms is a
  balancing problem *by construction*. This is that principle with a body count.
- [[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]] — where Voisin tells the room, and the room resolves to
  wake realm-09's gargoyles and shut the door.

## Player-Facing

**Indirectly for a whole chapter, then all at once.** The player meets the yield rule as an ordinary
RPG stat relationship and meets its consequence as a story beat, with no scene connecting them except
their own memory of the resource bar.
