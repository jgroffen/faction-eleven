# The Long Disconnection

The game **begins after an extended period of disconnection** between the realms. The factions
have been apart long enough that they do not know each other — which is why the story of Faction
Eleven can be a story of *discovery*.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** the state of the world at game start

## The Story

Long enough apart that contact is news. Every cross-faction meeting in the material is treated as
a revelation rather than a reunion, and the ten stories converge precisely because the
disconnection is ending.

**What caused it — settled 2026-08-08.** Both accounts are true, and they are the same account:

- There **is** a natural cycle in which the realms drift from easy to almost impossible to
  traverse ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]).
- [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] — the Ancients — **extended that cycle**, holding it at its trough
  far past its natural length. Their reasons are unknown.

The consequence is the game's starting condition: the disconnection has run so long that **the
existence of the other factions has become a myth to most factions**. Not forgotten history —
*myth*. The player's protagonists do not begin by wanting to reach other realms; they begin
not really believing there are any.

Deeper still, **all awareness that an eleventh faction ever existed has been lost.** The Ancients
did not merely lock the doors; they outlasted the memory of themselves. That is why the mystery
has three parts — *that they existed, what they did, and why* — and why it takes ten stories to
answer.

## Ties

- [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] — the Ancients, who extended the cycle.
- [[the-wheel-of-realms]] — the arrangement the disconnection froze.
- [[sealed-interplane-gates]] — the physical evidence the factions can actually see.

## Player-Facing

The disconnection is the player's starting condition, so it is felt before it is explained: every
protagonist starts alone in their own realm, in a world where other realms are a story people
tell. The *cause* is the mystery the game is about.
