# The Manipulation Of The Green Skins

The green-skins are not the authors of their own war. Somebody is **manipulating and using** them
— and the [[gargoyle]] knows it.

**Canon:** proposed

## The Story

Stated by the author on 2026-08-14, in full: the Gargoyle "wants to protect and save the green
skins and wants to stop the Green Skins faction from being **manipulated and used**."

That is one clause, and it moves more of this game than anything else said about the faction. Every
other faction in the material meets the green-skins as an invading horde: swarms breaking on the
[[celestials]]' walls, an open gate repopulating the [[mystics]]' dungeons, a sealed door in
[[realm-01]] holding them out. The horde has always had an unanswered question attached to it —
**why are they expanding?** ([[green-skins]]). This is the first answer: they may not be expanding
of their own accord.

It also reframes the [[gargoyle]]. He is not a traitor to his faction, and he is not merely a
pacifist objecting to a war. He is a **protector**, doing what gargoyles believe they are for, and
what he is protecting them from is **whoever is using them**.

## Ties

- [[gargoyle]] — knows, and acts on it. It is his motive.
- [[green-skins]] — the used party, apparently unaware.
- [[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]] — why he has no combat verb. You do not fight the people you are
  trying to save.

## What Is Not Established

**Who is doing the manipulating.** The author has deferred the lore and story of this faction to a
later session and it should not be invented here.

The obvious candidate is [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] — the Ancients, whose motive is the single
largest gap in the wiki ([[story-outline-requirement]]) — and a manipulated horde would give the
eleventh faction its first concrete *action* in the present day rather than only in prehistory. It
would also explain why the [[institute-of-eight]]'s gate was "sealed and guarded **for a reason**",
a prototype beat currently homeless ([[the-cracked-gate]]).

**That is inference and is recorded as inference.** The author has not said who is behind it.

## Player-Facing

Not decided. One route is already implied by the design: the [[gargoyle]]'s abilities are unlocked
by finding **Gargoyle lore fragments**, so the faction's own history is a collectible — and what is
being done to his people could be told through the same channel that makes him stronger.
