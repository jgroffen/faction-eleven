# The Realm Barriers

The gargoyles could **shape rock**, and what they shaped were **barriers on the inside of the realm
gates**. A gate is a door somebody else built; a barrier is a wall the realm built against it. The
gargoyles then **imbued their own magic into the stone** — and that magic is the thread that runs
back to them. **Break a barrier and the gargoyles wake.**

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** long before the game

## The Story

**There is a barrier on every enemy gate into a gargoyle realm.** [[realm-07]], [[realm-08]] and
[[realm-09]] each have three enemy gates ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]) and each walled all
three from the inside — **nine barriers**. They were raised **a long time ago**, at the end of the
War of the Six Realms, and they are the reason that war stopped.

**A barrier is an alarm as much as a wall.** The gargoyles built themselves into it. While the stone
holds, they sleep; when it breaks, a **bolt of their own imbued power** returns to them and brings
them out of torpor. Nothing else wakes them.

**The logic is airtight and that is what makes it exploitable.** A gargoyle may act only in defence
of a realm being invaded, and a broken barrier means precisely one thing: *something has come
through from outside*. The construct does not need to reason about it. Neither, therefore, does
anyone who wants to predict exactly what the construct will do.

## Two Layers Of Sealing

[[faction-eleven-antagonist]] locked the gates and the gargoyles sealed them. **Both are true,
because they are different layers:**

| Layer | Who | What |
|-------|-----|------|
| **The gate** | [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] | built the doors between allied and enemy realms, then locked and guarded them ([[sealed-interplane-gates]]) |
| **The barrier** | the gargoyles | walled the gate from **inside** their own realm, and left themselves listening to it |

So the seals on the green-bloc's gates are **faction-made** — the realms did that part to
themselves, out of an entirely reasonable fear. What the Ancients then did was seal the realms shut
behind them and hold that seal for a thousand years ([[the-long-disconnection]]). It is a quieter
and better-hidden kind of antagonism than turning the key personally, and it is why nobody
remembers them doing anything.

**Not every seal in the game is a barrier.** [[realm-02]] is sealed on every side with no gargoyles
anywhere near it, so more than one mechanism is at work across the wheel. **Only enemy gates into
realms 07, 08 and 09 carry barriers**; the ring's ten allied gates and the six enemy gates that
touch none of the three carry none.

**No power breaks a barrier without waking what built it.** [[the-red-power]] — carried by
[[ninja-kazuma]], [[val]] and [[coruscate]] — opens sealed *gates* and stops there; it has no
purchase on a barrier at all. The liches of [[the-damned]] are the only ones who have worked out how
to go through a barrier directly ([[the-void]]).

**But a barrier can be got past without being broken**, and that is a different act with a different
consequence: nothing rings. [[the-graceful-servants]] sent high-level Mystics with unique gear and
particular faction-class skills through [[realm-09]]'s 04-facing barrier **intact**, then put the
gargoyles behind it into **stasis** ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). It cannot be done at
scale — it takes a small party of the realm's most capable practitioners — which is why the harvest
is a trickle run by specialists rather than an invasion.

## The Nine, And Which Still Stand

Each barrier sits inside a gargoyle realm, facing one enemy gate. **Six of the nine are "old-war"
gates** — the ones between the gargoyle bloc (07, 08, 09) and the three realms whose incursions
started the war (03 [[celestials]], 04 [[mystics]], 05 [[fey-folk]]). The other three face
[[realm-01]] and [[realm-02]], which were never fronts.

| In | Facing | Old-war | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[realm-07]] [[green-skins]] | [[realm-03]] [[celestials]] | yes | **broken**, from the inside ([[the-broken-barrier]]) |
| [[realm-07]] | [[realm-01]] [[institute-of-eight]] | no | unstated |
| [[realm-07]] | [[realm-02]] [[robots]] | no | unstated |
| [[realm-08]] [[the-damned]] | [[realm-04]] [[mystics]] | yes | **broken**, in the same pattern |
| [[realm-08]] | [[realm-03]] | yes | unstated |
| [[realm-08]] | [[realm-02]] | no | unstated |
| [[realm-09]] [[werebeasts]] | [[realm-05]] [[fey-folk]] | yes | **standing** |
| [[realm-09]] | [[realm-04]] | yes | **standing — and snuck past**, with the gargoyles behind it in stasis |
| [[realm-09]] | [[realm-03]] | yes | **standing** — and the gate in front of it is closed ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]) |

**Realm-09's barriers survive because of somebody else's fear.** [[the-guardians-of-night]] are
commanded from [[realm-08]], and [[the-damned]] are afraid of the [[fey-folk]]. Realm-09's gargoyles
were spared and its barriers left intact not out of mercy or oversight but because the people giving
the orders did not want those doors opened. It is the fact a much later chapter is built on.

**And realm-09 is the only realm whose guardians have been dealt with by something other than a
hammer.** They are not smashed and their barriers are not broken; they are **asleep and held
asleep**. Waking them is what the [[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] are travelling to do
([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]).

## Player-Facing

Yes, and structurally. The Gargoyle wakes because of a barrier he cannot see breaking somewhere
else in the realm — so the player's first question is the same as his: *which one, and who?*
[[realm-09]]'s barriers are **still standing**, which is the fact that makes a later chapter
possible.

The diagram in [[realm-nearness-and-traversal]] shows which gates carry gargoyles.
