# The Realm Barriers

On the inside of a gargoyle realm's enemy gates stand **barriers**: **yellow, shimmering, translucent
shields** of the gargoyles' own magic, which **resist** anyone trying to push through them. The
gargoyles can **shape rock**, and they **fortified** each shield with stone. A gate is a door somebody
else built; a barrier is the realm's own guard on it — and the magic in it is the thread that runs back
to the gargoyles. **Breach a barrier and the gargoyles know.** A shield stops bodies, not signals.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** long before the game

## The Story

**There is a barrier on every enemy gate into a gargoyle realm.** [[realm-07]], [[realm-08]] and
[[realm-09]] each have three enemy gates ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]) and each shielded all
three from the inside — **nine barriers**. They were raised **a long time ago**, at the end of the
War of the Six Realms, and they are the reason that war stopped.

**A barrier is an alarm as much as a shield.** The gargoyles built themselves into it. While it holds,
they sleep; when it is **breached** — broken, or simply pushed through — a **bolt of their own imbued
power** returns to them. It brings a sleeping gargoyle out of torpor, and tells a waking one that
something has come through. Nothing else wakes them.

**The logic is airtight and that is what makes it exploitable.** A gargoyle may act only in defence
of a realm being invaded, and a breached barrier means precisely one thing: *something has come
through from outside*. The construct does not need to reason about it. Neither, therefore, does
anyone who wants to predict exactly what the construct will do.

## Two Layers Of Sealing

[[faction-eleven-antagonist]] locked the gates and the gargoyles sealed them. **Both are true,
because they are different layers:**

| Layer | Who | What |
|-------|-----|------|
| **The gate** | [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] | built the doors between allied and enemy realms, then locked and guarded them ([[sealed-interplane-gates]]) |
| **The barrier** | the gargoyles | shielded and fortified the gate from **inside** their own realm, and left themselves listening to it |

So the seals on the green-bloc's gates are **faction-made** — the realms did that part to
themselves, out of an entirely reasonable fear. What the Ancients then did was seal the realms shut
behind them and hold that seal for a thousand years ([[the-long-disconnection]]). It is a quieter
and better-hidden kind of antagonism than turning the key personally, and it is why nobody
remembers them doing anything.

**Not every seal in the game is a barrier.** [[realm-02]] is sealed on every side with no gargoyles
anywhere near it, so more than one mechanism is at work across the wheel. **Only enemy gates into
realms 07, 08 and 09 carry barriers**; the ring's ten allied gates and the six enemy gates that
touch none of the three carry none.

**Nobody crosses a barrier without alerting what built it — unless they get round it.**
[[the-red-power]] — carried by [[ninja-kazuma]], [[val]] and [[coruscate]] — opens sealed *gates* and
stops there; it has no purchase on a barrier at all. A barrier can be **pushed through** — a healthy
one resists hard, a decayed one less — but that is a breach, and the gargoyles know. The liches of
[[the-damned]] never meet one: they travel between realms without using gates at all ([[the-void]]).

**But a barrier can be circumvented**, and that is a different act with a different consequence:
nothing rings. [[the-graceful-servants]] — high-level Mystics with unique gear and particular
faction-class skills — used magic to get round [[realm-09]]'s 04-facing barrier and its
fortifications, put the gargoyles behind it into **stasis**, and then **dismantled enough of the
fortifications** to run a harvest through ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). It cannot be done at
scale — it takes a small party of the realm's most capable practitioners — which is why the harvest
is a trickle run by specialists rather than an invasion.

## The Nine, And Which Still Stand

Each barrier sits inside a gargoyle realm, facing one enemy gate. **Six of the nine are "old-war"
gates** — the ones between the gargoyle bloc (07, 08, 09) and the three realms whose incursions
started the war (03 [[celestials]], 04 [[mystics]], 05 [[fey-folk]]). The other three face
[[realm-01]] and [[realm-02]], which were never fronts.

| In | Facing | Old-war | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[realm-07]] [[green-skins]] | [[realm-03]] [[celestials]] | yes | fortifications **broken** from the inside ([[the-broken-barrier]]); the **shield still stands**, because [[gargoyle-granite]] does, and is **breached every time** a Damned or green-skin force crosses |
| [[realm-07]] | [[realm-01]] [[institute-of-eight]] | no | **crumbling** from disrepair, shield and fortifications — and pushed through by [[ninja-kazuma]] ([[the-second-signal]]) |
| [[realm-07]] | [[realm-02]] [[robots]] | no | unstated |
| [[realm-08]] [[the-damned]] | [[realm-04]] [[mystics]] | yes | **shield down**, every Damned gargoyle smashed; fortifications still in place, gate still sealed; guarded by the Minotaur until Val collects him, and seen by [[wraith-delahaye]] ([[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]) |
| [[realm-08]] | [[realm-03]] | yes | **shield down**; fortifications unstated |
| [[realm-08]] | [[realm-02]] | no | **shield down**; fortifications unstated |
| [[realm-09]] [[werebeasts]] | [[realm-05]] [[fey-folk]] | yes | **standing** — the gate unsealed by [[weresnake-taz]], who passes because the barrier lets werebeasts through |
| [[realm-09]] | [[realm-04]] | yes | **standing — and circumvented**, its gargoyles in stasis and its fortifications partly dismantled |
| [[realm-09]] | [[realm-03]] | yes | **standing** — and the gate in front of it is closed ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]) |

**A shield stands only while at least one gargoyle of its realm still exists.** When the last is
destroyed its shields go down and only the stone fortifications are left. [[realm-07]]'s shields stand
because [[gargoyle-granite]] survived the smashing — **if he is destroyed, every one of them falls**; [[realm-08]]'s are down, because every Damned
gargoyle was smashed; [[realm-09]]'s stand, because its gargoyles are asleep rather than destroyed. A
shield that is up but summons nothing is therefore a **tell**: its gargoyles exist and cannot come.

**[[val]] understands this rule.** Once every gargoyle in realm-07 had been smashed, its shields should
have fallen. They did not — so she knew one had survived, and went looking for him
([[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]]).

**Realm-09's barriers survive for two reasons.** [[the-guardians-of-night]] are mostly [[val]] and
[[the-damned]], and the Damned are afraid of the [[fey-folk]] one of those barriers faces — the Fey
Folk are the last realm on the Guardians' list. And Val has never found the road into realm-09 at all
([[the-guardians-of-night]]). It is the fact a much later chapter is built on.

**And realm-09 is the only realm whose guardians have been dealt with by something other than a
hammer.** They are not smashed and their barriers are not broken; they are **asleep and held
asleep**. Waking them is what the [[the-realm-champions|Realm Champions]] are travelling to do
([[the-first-meeting-of-the-realm-champions]]).

## Player-Facing

Yes, and structurally. **Breaches are something the Gargoyle feels**, and the game can use them: the
Celestial shield is breached again and again as the Guardians push forces through, and one breach at a
gate nobody uses is how he learns the Ninja has arrived ([[the-second-signal]]).

The Gargoyle wakes because of a barrier he cannot see breaking somewhere
else in the realm — so the player's first question is the same as his: *which one, and who?*
[[realm-09]]'s barriers are **still standing**, which is the fact that makes a later chapter
possible.
