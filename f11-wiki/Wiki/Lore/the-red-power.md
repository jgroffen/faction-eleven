# The Red Power

[[xipe-totec-the-red]] placed a gift in four people — **one in a game from each developer's realms, by design** — so that compatible neighbours could reach each other again before their realms dwindled away. It does exactly one thing: it **opens a closed interplane gate** ([[sealed-interplane-gates]]). It does not affect a [[the-realm-barriers|realm barrier]] — the gargoyle-built wall on the *inside* of a
gate is a different lock entirely, and the Red's gift has no key for it. This gift comes to be known as 'The Red Power'. It was granted by 'The Red' and users of it glow red when using it.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** present, granted before the game

## The Story

**Same gift, four carriers.** Each carrier experiences and expresses it differently — the
power is identical, only the flavour changes. **The Red coded the grant to reach one character
per developer on purpose** — the White's, the Black's, her own and the Blue's:

| Carrier                 | Manifestation                                                                                               | Gate                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[ninja-kazuma]]               | **breaks** it — force, a blow that lands wrong and still gets through                                       | [[realm-01]]'s guarded gate to [[realm-02]] ([[the-cracked-gate]])                |
| [[val]]                 | **unlocks** it — deliberate, precise, like a key she was always going to find                               | **two**: [[the-damned]]'s allied gate to the [[green-skins]] (08↔07), and the green-skins' enemy gate to the [[celestials]] (07↔03) |
| [[coruscate]] ([[mystics]])  | **dispels** it — the seal is treated as a working that can be undone rather than a wall that must be forced | **two**: [[realm-04]]'s gate to the [[werebeasts]] (04↔09), and its gate to the [[celestials]] (04↔03) |
| [[weresnake-taz]] ([[werebeasts]]) | unstated | [[realm-09]]'s gate to the [[fey-folk]] (09↔05), to hunt Fey Folk for trophies |

None of them is known to understand the gift for what it is. [[ninja-kazuma]] does not even know it is
happening — [[odie]] is the one who eventually identifies it in him ([[templar-monastery]]).
[[val]] knows she carries *something*, but not that it came from a god, or that three other people
across the wheel carry the same thing ([[val]]).

**It is gates-only, and that boundary matters.** The realm barriers the gargoyles raised
([[the-realm-barriers]]) are a separate, faction-made lock on top of the Ancients' gate — and
every barrier broken so far was broken the hard way: [[the-guardians-of-night]] smashed the
sleeping gargoyles' heads first, to disable the alarm, and only then broke the fortifications behind them — the shield outlasts that, for as long as any gargoyle of its realm does
([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]], [[the-broken-barrier]]).

Until the Liches of [[the-damned]] discovered how to traverse [[the-void]], there was no other way to traverse between realms except via the interplane gates, making control of the gates strategically important. This void traversal ability belongs to the Liches alone.

## Ties

- [[ninja-kazuma]] — carries it as force; cracked [[realm-01]]'s guarded gate without meaning to.
- [[val]] — carries it as precision; opened 08↔07 and 07↔03 on purpose.
- [[xipe-totec-the-red]] — the source, and the reason it is scoped so narrowly.
- [[coruscate]] — carries it as dispelling; opened two of [[realm-04]]'s gates.
- [[weresnake-taz]] — the Blue's carrier; opened 09↔05 to hunt the [[fey-folk]].
- [[sealed-interplane-gates]] — what the power actually opens.
- [[the-realm-barriers]] — what it explicitly cannot touch.
- [[the-void]] — the gap between planes that only the Liches have recently worked out how to traverse.

## Player-Facing

Background the player assembles rather than reads outright: [[ninja-kazuma]] is told what he carries by
[[odie]], mid-story; [[val]] never learns where hers came from at all. [[coruscate]]'s is met through its result — an unsealed
gate in [[celestial-tower]].
