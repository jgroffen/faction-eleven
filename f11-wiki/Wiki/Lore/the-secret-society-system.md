# The Secret Society System

[[huitzilopochtli-the-blue]]'s answer to seeing the Red and the White both intervene: a system that
seeds hidden factions into realms, each with its own convictions and its own agenda. It was built
for **three goals at once**, and it can act on any of them without him.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** shipped after the Red's imbuement

## The Story

**Three goals, one system.** The Secret Society System exists to:

1. **Introduce interesting events and intrigue** into a realm — a conspiracy is content.
2. **Incite conflict** — a realm at peace is a realm with nothing for the player to do.
3. **Fix game balance issues** — by giving a secret society goals that *rebalance the game*, rather
   than patching numbers directly.

**A secret society can be created automatically, whenever the system detects the need for any of
the three.** The Blue did not design [[the-guardians-of-night]]. He designed a system that watches
for exactly the conditions the Guardians of Night turned out to satisfy, and it fired.

## Why The Guardians Of Night Are Elegant

**One instance, three problems solved at once.** [[the-guardians-of-night]] is the system's answer
to all three goals landing in the same place:

| Goal | What was wrong | How the Guardians fix it |
|---|---|---|
| Balance | The [[gargoyle]] guardians were too powerful — an autonomous, uncommandable defence that a pre-emptive war could never get past | Smash them first ([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]) |
| Balance | [[the-damned]] had been stagnating | Give their vampire commander a war to fight |
| Conflict | The old war had been over for a thousand years — minimal conflict anywhere on that side of the wheel | Restart it, on false evidence ([[the-broken-barrier]]) |

**This is the elegance the system produced, not the Blue.** He shipped the capability and never
watched what it did with it — consistent with a developer who "gets more done than anyone, at the
cost of quality." The specific instance, the specific vampire it found already carrying
[[xipe-totec-the-red]]'s power, and the specific three-for-one solution are the system reasoning
autonomously, unsupervised, exactly as built.

**It is also why the Ancient has no motive.** [[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]] asks what the
eleventh faction wants from manipulating the Guardians of Night. It wants nothing. There is no
Ancient in the sense the Guardians imagine — only a system running its own goals, with a shipped
feature nobody is watching.

## A Second Instance Is Coming

The system is not a one-off. A second secret society, working the same three goals against the
[[mystics]] and the [[werebeasts]], is deferred design work for [[chapter-03]] — see
[[the-mystics-second-secret-society]]. **Open:** whether the system has fired more than these two
times, unnoticed, anywhere else on the wheel.

## Ties

- [[huitzilopochtli-the-blue]] — who built it, and does not watch what it does.
- [[val]] — the instance it found and used, not recruited.
- [[the-guardians-of-night]] — its first known output.
- [[the-manipulation-of-the-green-skins]] — the question this note answers.
- [[the-four-developers]] — the studio, and why an untested system is exactly what he would ship.

## Player-Facing

Not directly, and probably never by name. What reaches the player is the Guardians of Night as a
conspiracy to uncover — the system underneath stays implicit, the same way [[the-continuity-system]]
and [[the-retcon-engine]] stay implicit behind [[continuity-glitches]]. A player who tracks *both*
conspiracies (this one, and chapter three's second instance) and notices the shared shape is
finding the system without ever being told it exists.

## Open

- **Whether the system is ever named or shown as a mechanism**, or stays permanently inferred from
  its outputs, matching how the rest of the eleventh faction's infrastructure is handled.
- **How many times it has fired.** Two instances are known; nothing bounds the system to two.
