# The Six Classes

There are **six classes**, and **each faction has a 'faction class' that fulfils the role of that
class for that faction**. A Medic among the [[robots]] is not a Medic among the [[aliens]] — same
role, different people, different idiom.

The author is explicit about its weight: this is **lore the faction stories can leverage, rather
than rigid mechanics**.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** all of it

## The Story

The six roles, and the protagonist who carries each:

| Class | Protagonists |
|-------|--------------|
| Medic | [[autofix]] ([[robots]]), [[squidling]] ([[aliens]]) |
| Infiltrator | [[ninja]] ([[institute-of-eight]]), [[gargoyle]] ([[green-skins]]) |
| Front Liner | [[templar]] ([[celestials]]), [[werewolf]] ([[werebeasts]]) |
| Specialist | [[conjurer]] ([[mystics]]), [[wraith]] ([[the-damned]]) |
| Heavy Weapons | [[phoenix]] ([[fey-folk]]) |
| Commander | [[captain]] ([[pirates]]) |

Ten protagonists over six classes: four classes are doubled, and **Heavy Weapons and Commander are
held by one faction each**. That asymmetry is on record but unexplained.

Note what the doubled classes pair up: two Infiltrators are [[ninja]] and [[gargoyle]] — the two
protagonists the prototype notes have meeting first. Two Specialists are [[conjurer]] and
[[wraith]]. Two Front Liners are [[templar]] and [[werewolf]], who are on opposite sides of the
Celestials' war.

## The Full Matrix

Recovered 2026-08-16 from the labs website. **Every faction has all six** — sixty faction classes,
ten by six, with no gaps. The **protagonist of each faction is one of its own six**, which is why
the asymmetry above exists: it is an artefact of *which* class each protagonist was drawn from, not
of any faction lacking a role.

### What the matrix answers

- **The minotaur and the giant are green-skins.** Both are green-skin faction classes — Heavy
  Weapons and Front Liner. So the two who smashed the gargoyles' heads
  ([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]) were **green-skins destroying green-skin dead, on a damned
  woman's order**. An open question in that note, now closed.
- **[[val]] is a Vampire, the [[the-damned]]'s Commander class.** Fitting for someone who gives an
  order and leaves.
- **The gargoyles were made by two factions' Medics.** Necromancer is the Damned's Medic; Shaman is
  the green-skins'. The healers of two realms built the guardians out of the dead
  ([[the-gargoyle-guardians]]) — which is a far better fit than warriors doing it, and nobody
  planned it.
- **Gargoyle is the green-skins' Infiltrator** — matching both the prototype notes and
  [[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]. Its labs description adds two abilities not in the current
  ladder: gargoyles **move through stone**, and **with wings folded can appear to be flesh and
  bone**. The second is a disguise, and it is a strong candidate for a rung the ladder does not yet
  have.
- **Wraith appears twice** — the Damned's Specialist and the protagonist's name. Same thing.

### Where the matrix disagrees with the wiki

**[[templar]]'s class.** The labs site makes **Templar the Celestials' *Commander*** and **Knight
their Front Liner**. The wiki and the factions-retro notes both have the Templar as **Front Liner**.

This is worth a decision rather than a silent pick, because the author has already **rejected
renaming Templar to Knight** (round 3) — so the name is settled and only the class is in question.
Commander also sits oddly well with [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]], where the Templar directs a
defence while fighting in it. **Recorded, not resolved.**

## Player-Facing

As flavour and story material rather than as a systems layer the player builds against — the
author's framing is that stories *leverage* it. Whether the player ever sees the word "class" is
undecided.

## Open

That six roles recur across ten severed realms is a **clue with no explanation attached**. It
could be coincidence, convergent evolution, or evidence that [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] shaped
all ten. The material does not say, and the last reading is free plot if it's wanted.

**The full matrix makes that clue much louder.** It is no longer six roles loosely recurring — it is
a **complete ten-by-ten-by-six grid with no gaps**, in ten realms that have been out of contact long
enough to forget each other exists. Robots and fey folk and undead all field exactly one Commander,
one Medic, one Infiltrator. Convergent evolution does not usually produce a spreadsheet.

The realms were also **numbered** by the Ancients ([[the-wheel-of-realms]]), and catalogued as
opposites and neighbours. A faction that numbers things and arranges them in a ring is a faction
that might also have decided each realm should have six roles.

Also unresolved:

- **Templar's class** — see above.
- **Which faction classes are still current.** This is legacy material; the author has confirmed
  only that the protagonists and their archetypes hold.
- Whether faction classes are *populations* (all green-skin Minotaurs are Heavy Weapons) or
  *individuals*. The [[gargoyle]] is one of very few of his kind
  ([[the-gargoyle-guardians]]) while Orcs are clearly a whole people, so the grid mixes species
  with professions with — in Autofix's case — a single surviving machine.
