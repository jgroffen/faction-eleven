# The Six Classes

There are **six classes**, and **each faction has a 'faction class' that fulfils the role of that
class for that faction**. A Medic among the [[robots]] is not a Medic among the [[aliens]] — same
role, different people, different idiom.

The author is explicit about its weight: this is **lore the faction stories can leverage, rather
than rigid mechanics**.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** all of it

## The Story

The six roles, and the protagonist who carries each:

| Class | Protagonists |
|-------|--------------|
| Medic | [[autofix]] ([[robots]]), [[squidling]] ([[aliens]]) |
| Infiltrator | [[ninja]] ([[institute-of-eight]]), [[gargoyle]] ([[green-skins]]) |
| Front Liner | [[templar]] ([[celestials]]), [[werewolf]] ([[werebeasts]]) |
| Specialist | [[conjurer]] ([[mystics]]), [[wraith]] ([[the-damned]]) |
| Heavy Weapons | [[phoenix]] ([[fey-folk]]) |
| Commander | [[captain]] ([[pirates]]) |

Ten protagonists over six classes: four classes are doubled, and **Heavy Weapons and Commander are
held by one faction each**. That asymmetry is on record but unexplained.

Note what the doubled classes pair up: two Infiltrators are [[ninja]] and [[gargoyle]] — the two
protagonists the prototype notes have meeting first. Two Specialists are [[conjurer]] and
[[wraith]]. Two Front Liners are [[templar]] and [[werewolf]], who are on opposite sides of the
Celestials' war.

## Player-Facing

As flavour and story material rather than as a systems layer the player builds against — the
author's framing is that stories *leverage* it. Whether the player ever sees the word "class" is
undecided.

## Open

That six roles recur across ten severed realms is a **clue with no explanation attached**. It
could be coincidence, convergent evolution, or evidence that [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] shaped
all ten. The material does not say, and the last reading is free plot if it's wanted.
