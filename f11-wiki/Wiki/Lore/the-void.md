# The Void

The space **between** the realms — not a realm, not a gate, and not somewhere any god intended
anyone to go. The high liches of [[the-damned]] have been out there for some time, and they are the
only inhabitants of any realm who have stood outside their own world and come back.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** present

## The Story

[[tezcatlipoca-the-black]] built the liches as an experiment — creatures driven by something other
than the rules the realm runs on. It worked better than he knew. **The liches can bypass the rules
of their own world**, and they used that to leave it entirely, out into the void, and have been
quietly stealing from the [[aliens]] ever since: taking pieces of a neighbouring realm home to use in
their own.

They cover their tracks. **None of the four gods knows this is happening.**

## What Dwells There

Ancient, vast, incoherent things — worlds that were, and are no longer anyone's. These are
**abandoned realities**: previous iterations that the Black destroyed and never fully unmade,
still running in the dark with nobody in them and nothing left to want.

They are the hardest physical evidence in the game that **reality has been built and destroyed
before**. The player does not have to be told about the cycle; the liches can take them to see the
corpses of the last three attempts. And it makes the stakes of the Black's position visible from the
middle of the game rather than the end: this is what "destroy it and start again" leaves behind.

## Why The Aliens Are Suffering

The [[aliens]]' realm is being taken apart piece by piece, and they do not know why. **This is
recent** — it postdates the sealing and has nothing to do with it, which sets them apart from every
other declining realm. It gives the Aliens a mystery of their own whose answer is the game's central
one, and it ties realm 10 to realm 08 through the two games the Black leads
([[the-four-developers]]).

## The Liches Are Awake

The liches are the one group in the multiverse that is **provably** more than the rules that made
them — they demonstrated it by breaking out. Everyone else's inner life is an open question; theirs
is settled. That the only certainly-sentient faction in the game is also its most repellent one —
corpse-stealing, gate-breaching, looting a neighbour — is the point, and worth protecting.

It also makes them the game's **oracle**: the only entities who have been outside and can testify to
what is there.

## Ancient Eldritch Knowledge

**The liches are the only characters who both see the glitches and can explain them.** Their AI nature
gives them the self-awareness to notice, and having hacked out into the void gives them real working
knowledge of the realms and of **the infrastructure the games run on** — the layer beneath the world,
which nobody else has ever stood in.

**They present all of it as ancient eldritch lore.** Not a lie, exactly: a costume. What they actually
possess is systems knowledge, and what they offer is the forbidden wisdom of things that have looked
upon the outer dark. The framing is doing two jobs — it fits what a lich is supposed to be, and it
keeps the source of the knowledge to themselves.

**This makes them the game's most dangerous exposition**, and the reason they work as an oracle at
all. Anything they say is simultaneously true and theatrical, so the player receives real information
in a register that invites them to discount it. By the time it can be checked, it has been sitting in
the player's head for chapters ([[continuity-glitches]], [[a-heal-is-a-patch]]).

**Planned gag, recorded as intent:** the **Glitch Lich** — *liches cause glitches*. Placement and
tone are undecided, but the joke lands on something true, since the liches breaking containment is a
real source of instability.

**Yes, and it is one of the three channels the final reveal arrives through.** The [[the-damned]]'s
story is the one that can go out there — currently the only reason that faction's game style needs to
support somewhere that is not a realm.
