# The War of the Six Realms

The war of the linked era, fought along the opposition axis of [[the-wheel-of-realms]] between two
contiguous arcs of three realms each. It is the war the gargoyles were built to end
([[the-gargoyle-guardians]]), the war [[gargoyle-granite]] remembers, and the one every other
faction has reduced to legend.

**Canon:** proposed · **Era:** before the thousand years

## The Six

| Bloc | Realms |
|------|--------|
| **The invaded** — built the gargoyles | 07 [[green-skins]] · 08 [[the-damned]] · 09 [[werebeasts]] |
| **The invaders** — "constantly incurring" | 03 [[celestials]] · 04 [[mystics]] · 05 [[fey-folk]] |

**Four realms took no part:** 01 [[institute-of-eight]], 02 [[robots]], 06 [[pirates]] and 10
[[aliens]]. The Pirates and the Institute fought their own war in the same era —
[[the-pirate-institute-war]] — and it is **not** part of this one.

## The Story

Told in full at [[the-linking-of-the-realms]]: the green-skins and werebeasts raided rather than
warred, the Damned took the dead of other realms home, the Celestials, Mystics and Fey Folk allied
and pushed back hard, and the invaded built the gargoyle guardians. The barriers those gargoyles
raised ([[the-realm-barriers]]) still stand on the 07↔03, 08↔04 and 09↔05 gates, and the sealing
that followed ([[the-long-disconnection]]) froze the war's last shape for a thousand years.

**Underneath, it is the Red's arc against the Blue's** — two gods who disagree about improvements,
fought by proxy ([[the-four-developers]]). The present crisis is this war restarted by deception
([[the-broken-barrier]], [[the-guardians-of-night]]).

## Player-Facing

As history and legend, never as a scene — see [[the-linking-of-the-realms]]. The player meets it
first as a green-skin horde breaking on somebody's wall, and only later learns the green-skins
were the invaded.

## Open

- Whether "War of the Six Realms" is what any faction *calls* it in-fiction, or only the design's
  name for it. A realm that remembers only its own neighbours as legend may not know there were six.
