# The Ambush On The Road

[[val]]'s assassins come for the Ninja, the Gargoyle and Mifix; [[wraith-delahaye]] sees the wraith
among them coming, thwarts the ambush, reveals herself, and is invited to travel with the party.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-07]]

## Hook

Val has sent an **assassin team** after the party: **kill the Ninja, destroy the Gargoyle, capture
Mifix.** She does not want [[gargoyle-granite]] interfering with her plans, and she has become
**curious as to what [[mifix]] is.** One of the assassins is a wraith — and a wraith sneaking up on a
camp is exactly what Delahaye has been doing for days.

## Objectives

1. **See it coming.** During a camp minigame ([[the-shadowing-of-the-ninja]]), Delahaye spots the
   assassins' approach — specifically **the wraith among them**, moving on the group the way she
   would.
2. **Thwart the ambush.** Delahaye intervenes. How the fight plays — wraith against wraith, what
   she can do to the living assassins, what the party does once woken — is not written.
3. **Reveal herself.** The intervention gives her away. The party learns a ghost has been following
   them.
4. **Explain.** She was following because she **needs to find a way back to the Pirate realm**, and
   this group knows how to traverse realms.
5. **Be invited.** They invite her to join them and **promise to help her — as long as it doesn't
   interfere with their own missions.**

## Rewards

- No item. Delahaye has a party ([[ninja-kazuma]], [[gargoyle-granite]], [[mifix]]) and a conditional
  promise of a way home.

## Prerequisites

- [[the-shadowing-of-the-ninja]]

## Notes

- **The assassins are Val's move after the trial.** She left the Gargoyle to the Minotaur without
  confidence it would win; this is what she does about it, and about the two she met alongside him.
  Capturing Mifix rather than killing him is the tell: she has noticed he is not from anywhere she
  knows.
- **Two wraiths.** One of the Damned's, doing what a recruited wraith does; one who broke out. The
  scene is the two exits of [[wraiths]] meeting.
- **The condition matters.** "As long as it doesn't interfere with their own missions" is a promise
  built to be tested: her mission is revenge in [[realm-06]], theirs run through
  [[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]] and beyond. Whether she still means to thwart the Ninja when
  she accepts is not stated.
- **She stops being unseen here.** From this scene on Delahaye is a member of the converging party,
  and the wraith minigame's sneaking is something she does *for* the group rather than around it.
