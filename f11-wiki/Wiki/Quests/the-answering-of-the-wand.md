# The Answering Of The Wand

[[conjurer-voisin]] hears [[the-wand]] and comes. The [[mystics]]' protagonist is the **first** ally to
reach [[templar-will]] at [[templar-monastery]] — before [[ninja-kazuma]], [[gargoyle-granite]] and [[mifix]] arrive
([[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]]).

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-04]] · **Giver:** [[templar-will]] ·
**Location:** [[realm-04]] → [[celestial-tower]] → [[templar-monastery]]

## Hook

The Templar's call reached the Mystics ([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]). Somebody answers.

## Objectives

1. **The wand lights up.** Deep in a cave on an ordinary adventuring trip
   ([[the-ordinary-day-of-the-conjurer]]), the relic the character-creation screen chose for her
   wakes and starts **leading** her.
2. **The wand talks.** It speaks to Voisin **through the homunculi** — so the player gets dialogue
   with an object, carried by the small creatures they had breakfast with. The homunculi are the
   Conjurer's interface to a thing that has no mouth.
3. **Follow it out.** The wand leads her **up out of the cave** and across the realm to the **04↔03
   gate to the [[celestials]]** — which is inside [[celestial-tower]], because in [[realm-04]] an allied
   gate always has a building around it and everybody knows which one.
4. **Get in — as the homunculi.** The gate should be sealed and is not: [[coruscate]] dispelled it, and
   the Servants guard it. The player takes control of Voisin's **homunculi** and sends them through ahead
   of her; one that is caught is **destroyed**, and another can be sent ([[celestial-tower]]).

   **This is her second question, and she asks it before the first.** A Mystic knows the Celestial gate
   is in that tower and knows it is supposed to be shut. Somebody has opened it, and she wants to know
   why.
5. **Overhear what the tower is actually for.** Hiding, she hears that **the gate to the werebeasts
   is open** and that **Reagent is being stockpiled**, and infers the rest
   ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]). She needs no explanation: that the Mystics once harvested
   other realms' creatures is common knowledge at home. What the histories at home *also* say is that
   the werebeasts were the aggressors — they were not — and she does not know that part yet.
6. **Reach the Templar** at the besieged monastery — the first ally to arrive, carrying something
   she has not decided what to do with.
7. **Together they work out what the relics are.** The Templar's relics — [[odie]], [[the-wand]] —
   are **links to ancient allies, and a way to call for help** ([[allied-faction-signals]]). It is
   the first time any character understands what the devices are for, and Voisin is holding a third
   one.

## Rewards

- Narrative: the Celestials' "ancient allies" are now understood as a *mechanism* rather than a
  hope. This understanding is what the Templar and Conjurer bring to the council in
  [[chapter-05]].
- **The story's first moral problem for a protagonist about their own faction.** Voisin leaves
  realm-04 believing her people are harvesting another realm — a realm she does not like and does not
  trust — and objecting anyway. Nobody sent her to find that out; she overheard it on the way to
  somewhere else.

## Prerequisites

- [[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]] — the signal going out.
- [[the-ordinary-day-of-the-conjurer]] — the day it interrupts.

## Notes

- **One building, two secrets.** She goes to the tower because the wand does, and finds that the gate
  she expected to be sealed is open and that the people who opened it are shipping Reagent. Neither is
  what she came for.
- **The player never harvests a werebeast.** The exploit is something she learns about, not a verb
  she is given ([[the-harvesting-of-the-werebeasts]]).
- **The wand chooses.** It leads her past the Werebeast gate to the Celestial one, which means the
  object knows the difference. Whether that is navigation or intent is not established.
- **She is a hero unit when she gets there.** [[celestials-hero-tower-defence]] is built to take
  allies from other factions as hero units with their own powers and levelling; Voisin is the first
  one it takes. She fights a **final wave** before the machine arrives, to great effect.

## Open

- **What the Servants do about an intruder**, and whether Voisin is caught, seen, or clean. Whether
  [[coruscate]] appears at all.
- **What exactly she overhears and from whom.**
- Whether the caverns' Werebeast gate and the tower's secret are the same door from two sides.
- Whether the wand's dialogue is a character or a voice — how much it knows, and whether it lies.
- How the Templar and Conjurer arrive at the relics insight — what each knew that the other didn't.
- Whether the cave she starts in is the tutorial cave or a later one, and how far across the realm the
  wand walks her.
- Whether she recognises [[the-graceful-servants]] as an organisation at all, or only as "people in a
  tower".
