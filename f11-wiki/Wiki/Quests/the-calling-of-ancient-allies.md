# The Calling Of Ancient Allies

The climax of chapter two for the [[celestials]], and **the moment the multiverse starts talking
to itself.**

Having lost [[the-outpost]], [[templar-will]] has fallen back to [[templar-monastery]] — chosen
deliberately, because he believes the Celestials need to find **ancient allies** to beat the war
machine. Exploring the monastery's ancient parts between battles, the Templar finds and uses
**two ancient devices** to signal for aid: [[odie]], which he is able to
turn back on, and [[the-wand]].

**The signal is received by the [[robots]] — whose remote communications are working again — and by
the [[mystics]].**

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[templar-monastery]]

## The Beat

A defeat, a deliberate retreat, and then a search through the oldest part of a building nobody has
understood for generations — played as platforming between tower-defence levels
([[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]).

**It happens before the next battle, not after it.** The Templar calls for help from a position he
still holds — and the answer takes two chapters to arrive, during which he loses
[[oluja-town]] ([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]) and makes his stand back here
([[the-stand-at-the-monastery]]).

Two devices, two calls, two factions who hear them. See [[allied-faction-signals]].

## Why It Matters

- It is the **first deliberate act of reconnection in the game.** [[the-first-signal]] was
  an accident — a signal that happened to land. This is somebody *choosing* to call.
- It **pays off the Robots' chapter-one ability a second time**, in a different faction's story
  and a different chapter. [[autofix-alto]]'s remote comms are what make the monastery bot audible.
- It makes the [[mystics]]' realm a **major setting in chapter two**.
- The Templar is reaching for "ancient allies" and reaching, in fact, for two of the ten factions.
  Whether he is also reaching for [[faction-eleven-antagonist]] without knowing it is the
  question [[allied-faction-signals]] leaves open.

## Prerequisites

Follows [[the-fall-of-the-outpost]].

## What Follows

Word comes that the war machine is heading for [[oluja-town]], and the Templar goes
([[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]).

## Open

- Whether the two signals arrive simultaneously, and whether the player chooses which to send.
- The [[mystics]]' answer is [[conjurer-voisin]] reaching the monastery in [[chapter-04]]
  ([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]); the journey itself is unwritten. What the [[robots]] do about it
  is open.
