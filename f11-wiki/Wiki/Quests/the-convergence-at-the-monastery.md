# The Convergence At The Monastery

The culmination of a chapter, and the first time protagonists from four factions stand in the same
place: **[[ninja]], [[mifix]], [[gargoyle]] and the [[mystics]]' protagonist all reach
[[templar]]**.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-03]] · **Location:**
[[templar-monastery]]

## The Beat

Two journeys arrive at once. The Ninja, Mifix and the Gargoyle come following the faint signal they
have been chasing since [[the-second-signal]]. The Mystics' protagonist comes in answer to
[[the-wand]] — and, per the author, **to support the Templar**.

They arrive at a monastery under siege by a war machine the Celestials cannot beat, holding a relic
the Celestials do not understand.

## What It Unlocks

- **[[mifix]] meets [[odie]].** The corrupted memory meets the intact one, and the lost history of
  the Robot Realm can finally be told ([[no-machine-repairs-itself]] is why only one of them still
  has it).
- **Hints toward `ACCESS DENIED`.** Odie knows something about what is needed to unblock the
  autonomous-robotics branch of [[autofix-skill-tree]] — the [[robots]]' forbidden future.
- **The Templar gets his ancient allies**, which is what he retreated to the monastery to find.

## Why It Matters

It is the first time the game's structure delivers on its premise in full: four protagonists,
four genres, four realms, one room. Everything before it is signals; this is arrival.

It also resolves the Celestials' framing. The Templar believed the Celestials needed **ancient
allies** to defeat the war machine and chose where to retreat on that belief. He turns out to be
right — and the allies were partly in his own cellar the whole time.
