# The Cracking Of The Gate

[[ninja]]'s entire chapter-one arc, and the event that makes the rest of chapter one possible: a
single cycle of **boss fight, defeat, training** against the gate guardian. The Ninja **loses** —
but the battle **cracks the gate** ([[the-cracked-gate]]).

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-01]]

## The Beat

The prototype's opening still stands: the player is sent at the guardian badly unprepared and is
beaten, because they do not have the skills the fight demands. They return home, the family's
back story opens up, and training begins — teaching timing and the moves needed to progress
([[ninja-rhythm-platforming]]).

What is new is the consequence. The defeat is not clean. The gate is **cracked**, and through the
crack a signal will pass.

## Why It Matters

It makes chapter one causally tight. The Ninja's failure is the **precondition** for
[[the-first-signal]] — so the chapter's two reduced factions and its lead faction are not
three parallel demos but one chain of events:

> Ninja fights and loses → the gate cracks → [[autofix]]'s restored remote comms reach through →
> the shrine bot wakes.

Losing is load-bearing. That is worth protecting through every later revision.

## Death Rule

This is a **boss fight**, so this is one of the only places the Ninja can actually die — see
[[death-and-return]].

## Open

- Whether the player can somehow win, and what breaks if they do.
- Whether the crack is visible to the player at the time, or only in hindsight.
