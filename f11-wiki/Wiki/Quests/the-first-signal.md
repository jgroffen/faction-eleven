# The First Signal

The story event that ties chapter one together. In [[autofix]]'s story the player unlocks **Quantum
Comms** — an upgrade for controlling his *own* mini-fix bots, in his *own* realm — and once
[[ninja]]'s boss battle has cracked the guarded gate, that signal escapes and reaches [[mifix]] in
[[realm-01]].

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-01]] · **Location:** [[realm-02]]

> **Superseded framing.** This beat was originally written as *"the shrine bot awakens"*. It does
> not: [[mifix]] has been **online for generations**, mentoring the Ninja's
> family and repairing the Ninja between fights ([[death-and-return]]). Nothing wakes. What changes
> is that it can suddenly **hear**.

## The Beat

For generations the [[institute-of-eight]] have kept an ancestor spirit in a small toy car. It is
really a **mini-fix bot** from [[realm-02]], awake the whole time, training the family — so that
one day it can get home.

It has been alone the whole time, too. The realms are sealed, and a sealed realm stops signals as
well as travellers ([[allied-faction-signals]]), so nothing of its own kind has reached it in
living memory.

In chapter one [[autofix]] unlocks **Quantum Comms** ([[robots-incremental-exploration]]) to better
control the minibots he has learned to build. It is a local upgrade with a local purpose. **Every gate from [[realm-02]] is
sealed, and Quantum Comms cannot operate without an opening.** The robots can neither leave nor
call.

The call goes nowhere until [[ninja]] progresses to the boss battle with a newly trained skill,
loses to [[the-gate-guardian]], and **cracks the gate** ([[the-cracked-gate]]). That crack is the
only hole in the Robot Realm's seal — and through it Autofix's signal reaches **both** mini-fix
bots: [[mifix]] in [[realm-01]], and [[odie]] in [[realm-03]].

**They are detectable, not reachable.** The distance is too great to communicate — Mifix knows
something is there and nothing more. Contact is a fact, not a conversation, which is what makes it
worth crossing realms to chase.

**Both conditions are required**, in either order:

| # | Condition | Faction |
|---|-----------|---------|
| 1 | [[autofix]] unlocks **Quantum Comms** | [[robots]] |
| 2 | [[ninja]] cracks the guarded gate in the boss battle | [[institute-of-eight]] |

## Why It Matters

This is the **first cross-realm contact in the game**, and it happens between [[realm-01]] and
[[realm-02]] — **neighbours on [[the-wheel-of-realms]], historically friendly**. The oldest
alliance in the setting is the first one to come back.

It is also the **first worked example of [[protagonist-swapping-and-story-gating]]**, and a
two-sided one: it needs an ability earned in the [[robots]]' story *and* a defeat suffered in the
[[institute-of-eight]]'s. Neither faction alone can produce it.

And it is a better beat than an awakening. A machine that has been awake and alone for generations,
mentoring the children of a disgraced family, suddenly hearing one of its own kind — that lands
harder than switching something on.

Note who does what: the robots earn the ability and cannot use it; the Ninja makes the opening and
does not know what they have done. **Neither party is aware they are cooperating.**

  something an NPC hands over.
- **Ordering within chapter one:** whether the game enforces an order between the two conditions,
  or the contact simply fires when both are done, is undecided.
- **Which trained skill** lets the Ninja progress to the boss battle and crack the gate — to be
  determined ([[ninja-rhythm-platforming]]).
- Does [[autofix]] know the signal was received? The stronger version is that the player knows and
  Autofix doesn't.
