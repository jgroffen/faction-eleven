# The Ordinary Day Of The Conjurer

The [[mystics]]' entire introduction: a character-creation screen that makes its own choices, a
cottage full of homunculi, breakfast, a walk into the village, and a cave. It is the only peaceful
home in the game and the player is given a full ordinary day in it before anything happens.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-04]] · **Location:** [[realm-04]]

## Hook

The player starts a new faction and is handed a character-creation screen — and then watches it
fill itself in.

## Objectives

1. **Create the character, or watch it be created.** The screen offers the [[mystics]]' six faction
   classes ([[the-six-classes]] — Oracle, Summoner, Battle Mage, Illusionist, Witch Doctor,
   Conjurer) and a long list of starting bonuses. **Every class but Conjurer glitches and refuses
   to be picked**, and **"Ancient Relic" selects itself** from the bonus list. Its description
   reads: *"A historic treasure of forgotten lore and unexpected purpose."* The player names
   nothing; they end up with [[conjurer-voisin]] and [[the-wand|a powerful magic wand]] they did not
   choose.
2. **Wake up and have breakfast with the homunculi.** She lives with many of them. This is where
   the player learns what a homunculus is by sitting at a table with several.
3. **Walk out into the village.** Other Mystics, other cottages, Reagent vendors. The above-world
   of [[realm-04]] is friendly and this is the beat that establishes it.
4. **Go adventuring in a cave.** The routine trip every Mystic makes, for the Reagents magic
   crafting runs on. The tutorial for the isometric action RPG, and the place the player first
   dies — and first sees a homunculus walk out from home to reconstitute her.

## Rewards

- Reagents, and the whole Mystic system vocabulary: Health, Magic, Reagents; the skill tree; the
  town vendors; the homunculi as death-return and as fast travel.

## Notes

**The glitch is the point and the player must not be told so.** This is
[[continuity-glitches]] at the **user-interface layer**, the same trick as `ACCESS DENIED` in
[[autofix-skill-tree]] — a refusal the player can see, from an author they cannot. Played straight
it reads as a game being linear about its protagonist. Re-read later it is evidence that Voisin was
**built to specification**, and that something enforced the specification while the player watched.

**It is the second glitch in this chapter.** [[mifix]] glitches in
[[the-diagnosis-of-the-last-gargoyle]], the chapter's opening beat. Chapter four therefore opens
and re-opens on the seams, in two different factions, without either scene explaining the other.

**Fast travel is taught at the moment it matters.** The wand calls at the bottom of a cave
([[the-answering-of-the-wand]]) — the furthest from home the player has been — and the answer is to
de-constitute into a homunculus and be back in the village at once.

## Open

- **The village has no name**, and neither does the cave. [[realm-04]] itself has no name its own
  people use — "Realm 04" is the Ancients' catalogue number.
- Whether the player names the character, or "Voisin" is filled in like everything else.
- What the rest of the starting-bonus list contains, and whether the player can read it before it
  chooses for them.
- Whether any player could plausibly *not* notice — the beat only works if a first-time player
  reads it as linearity rather than as a bug report.
- Whether other factions get a creation screen at all. If only the Mystics do, that asymmetry needs
  a reason.
