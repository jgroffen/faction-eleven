# The Recruitment Of The Wraith

The player's undead kingdom reaches a map that needs a wraith and plays the recruitment minigame;
[[wraith-delahaye]] wakes, recovers her death, and breaks out — **freezing the RTS.** Opens the
Damned's story.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-08]]

## Hook

The player is a kingdom, named by them, expanding across the Damned's world map
([[damned-undead-kingdom-rts]]). A map comes up that requires a wraith. Specialist units are
recruited by minigame, so the game hands the player a grave and a ghost who does not know her name.

## Objectives

1. **Wake as a wraith.** [[wraith-memory-puzzle]] begins: no name, no past, a grave as the one fixed
   point ([[death-and-return]]), and [[wraith-object-reading]] as the verb.
2. **Recover who she was.** A Pirate — a First Mate — named **Delahaye** ([[pirates]]). The name is
   one of the memories; her body solidifies as the self returns.
3. **Recover how she died.** Fighting in a pirate armada against the [[institute-of-eight]]'s
   invasion ([[the-pirate-institute-war]]) — and **betrayed**: an ally sold the armada's plans to the
   enemy ([[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]]). She died with many of her allies. Why the ally did it, she cannot know.
4. **Refuse both exits.** A wraith either resolves its business and is released, or joins the ranks
   of the Damned — the recruitment ([[wraiths]]). Delahaye's business is not resolvable and she is
   not recruited: **her power of will breaks her out of the game.** The player cannot fail this.
5. **The RTS freezes.** The kingdom's campaign stops, still there, going nowhere. Delahaye is loose
   on its map with one intention: **go and rescue her allies.**

## Rewards

- No item. The reward is a protagonist — and a frozen kingdom the protagonists will need back
  later ([[damned-undead-kingdom-rts]]).

## Prerequisites

- None; this is where the Damned's story starts. How far into the kingdom's campaign the wraith map
  comes is not decided.

## Notes

- **The break-out is not will.** It is [[pirates-open-exploration|freedom]], a mechanic built into
  Pirate characters, running inside a game with no rule for it — a cross-realm glitch, and the
  Freeze of [[continuity-glitches]] at the scale of a whole game ([[the-setting-statement]]). The
  fiction claims it as her will; the player is never told otherwise here.
- **She wakes believing the war is still on.** The memories she recovers end at her death; she
  remembers that realms could be traversed and knows nothing of the sealing, which happened after
  ([[the-long-disconnection]]). "Rescue my allies" is a thousand-year-old plan. The next quest is
  about her finding that out ([[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]).
- **Which memories the puzzle hands over and which it withholds** matters: the betrayal is
  recovered here, but whether the betrayer's *identity* is, is open ([[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]]).

## Open

- How the kingdom's freeze is presented to the player at [[chapter-02]]'s close — a cliffhanger,
  not a bug ([[continuity-glitches]]).
- How much of the kingdom's campaign the player plays before the wraith map — and whether other
  recruitment minigames come first.
- What triggers the break-out — a memory threshold, or the minigame running its course.
