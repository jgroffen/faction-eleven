# The Search Of The Frozen Map

Loose on the frozen world map, [[wraith-delahaye]] searches for her allies, learns how much time
has passed, finds [[the-guardians-of-night]] working to reopen gates, and follows [[val]] toward
the [[green-skins]]' realm.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-08]]

## Hook

She broke out to rescue her allies. The map she is standing on is not one she knows, the kingdom on
it is frozen mid-campaign, and every object she touches tells her something is wrong with the date.

## Objectives

1. **Search the map.** The RTS's board, frozen, played with the wraith minigame's mechanics —
   [[wraith-object-reading]], investigation, infiltration ([[wraith-memory-puzzle]]).
2. **Realise where she is.** This is the Damned's realm ([[realm-08]]), which she does not know.
   She remembers that realms could be traversed; she does not know they were sealed.
3. **Realise when she is.** Slowly, as the map is explored: **much time must have passed since she
   died.** The rescue is impossible. **Her intention changes to revenge** — on [[captain-d-artigue|d'Artigue]], who
   must be found, if anywhere, in the Pirate realm — and, as the scale of it sinks in, to **not
   knowing what she will find**: what happened to her crew and [[the-drunken-mermaid]], and
   whether [[tortuga]] is still her crew's ([[captain-anne]]).
4. **Find the Guardians of Night.** Investigation and infiltration reveal that
   [[the-guardians-of-night]] exist and **are working on ways to reopen gates between realms** —
   the first thing she has found that could get her home.
5. **Follow the trail to a vampire castle** — [[val-s-castle]]. Just as the Wraith moves close,
   **Val appears on the world map, moving toward the castle.**
6. **Learn where Val is going next**: to the Green Skins' realm ([[realm-07]]). **Delahaye decides
   to follow.**

## Rewards

- No item. Delahaye leaves realm-08 with a goal (revenge), a lead (the Guardians reopen gates), and
  a guide she is tailing (Val).

## Prerequisites

- [[the-recruitment-of-the-wraith]]

## Notes

- **Rescue → revenge is the quest's spine.** The frozen map is the instrument: a thousand years is
  not announced, it is read out of objects one at a time. This is the wraith minigame's puzzle
  turned outward — the same verb that recovered her life now dates the world.
- **Val's timing.** Delahaye does not enter the castle and meet its owner; she arrives as Val does,
  and what she gets is Val's *next* destination. The castle is a signpost ([[val-s-castle]]).
- **The gate she follows Val through** is the Damned's allied gate to the green-skins, which Val
  herself opened ([[sealed-interplane-gates]], [[the-red-power]]). Delahaye has no way to know that.
- **What Delahaye thinks the Guardians are** — allies, a tool, a threat — is not stated; she follows
  them because they are the only people she has found who can open a door.
