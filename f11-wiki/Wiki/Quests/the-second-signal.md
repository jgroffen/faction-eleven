# The Second Signal

In chapter two, the mini-fix bot in the family shrine reports that it can detect **another mini-fix
bot signal — different from the one coming through the cracked gate.** Following it leads [[ninja]]
to [[the-ancient-ruin]] and its abandoned, already-cracked gate into [[realm-09]] — and through it,
to [[gargoyle]].

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[the-ancient-ruin]]

## The Beat

The shrine bot, awake since [[the-shrine-bot-awakens]], is now listening — and hears something the
crack cannot explain. A second machine of its own kind, calling from somewhere else.

The trail leads out of the Institute's territory to a ruin, and to a gate nobody has guarded for a
very long time, standing cracked. The Ninja crosses.

On the other side is [[gargoyle]].

## What They Find, And Who They Look For

The signal's source is [[the-monastery-autofix-bot]] — the machine [[templar]] switched back on in
[[templar-monastery]] ([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]). **The Templar has it.**

So the Ninja and the Gargoyle set out together to find a bot that is already in a third faction's
hands, in a third realm. Three of chapter two's four factions converge on one object, and none of
them knows the others are looking.

## Why It Matters

- It is the **second** cross-faction connection, and the first one a protagonist *pursues* rather
  than receives.
- It brings [[gargoyle]] into the story and gives the [[green-skins]] their first real material.
- It confirms gates are separate doors: this one was cracked all along and never carried the robot
  signal, because it faces the wrong realm.

## Open

- **The route to the Templar.** [[realm-09]] and [[realm-03]] are four places apart on
  [[the-wheel-of-realms]], yet the green-skins already attack the Celestials through an open gate.
  The likeliest reading is that the Ninja and Gargoyle travel the green-skins' own invasion route
  to reach [[templar-monastery]] — which would turn a long-standing anomaly in the wheel into a
  plot device. **This is inference, not the author's claim.**
- Whether the Ninja can return home, and what the Institute makes of a sentenced son crossing a
  gate he was not sentenced to.
