# The Shadowing Of The Ninja

Having watched [[val]] confront the protagonists, [[wraith-delahaye]] follows [[ninja-kazuma]] as
her road to the Institute's realm — and so to the Pirates' — sneaking his camps to learn who these
people are.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-07]]

## Hook

Delahaye followed Val into the Green Skins' realm and, unseen, **observed the confrontation
between Val and [[gargoyle-granite]], [[ninja-kazuma]] and [[mifix]]**
([[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]]). Val is not her way home. But one of the three is a Ninja of
the [[institute-of-eight]] — and Delahaye knows that the Institute's realm **is directly connected
to the Pirate realm** ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]). He could lead her there.

## Objectives

1. **Choose the Ninja.** After the confrontation, Delahaye decides to follow [[ninja-kazuma]] —
   to reach [[realm-01]], and from there [[realm-06]].
2. **Treat him as an enemy.** The Institute invaded her realm and killed her
   ([[the-pirate-institute-war]]). She is also thinking about **how to thwart the Ninja's plans.**
3. **Sneak the camps.** Whenever the Ninja camps, a **wraith minigame** plays: the Wraith sneaks
   around the camp and uses her investigation skills to find out about Kazuma, Granite and Mifix
   ([[wraith-memory-puzzle]]).
4. **Learn how much has changed.** What the camps teach her: the world is not the one she died in,
   and **the Ninja is not aware of the Pirates at all.** The enemy she is stalking does not know her
   realm exists.

## Rewards

- No item. Delahaye knows the party before the party knows she exists — and her picture of the
  Ninja as an enemy has started to come apart.

## Prerequisites

- [[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]
- [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]] — she watches it; the party does not know she is there.

## Notes

- **Kazuma and Mifix are present at the trial**, with Granite. Delahaye is a fourth presence nobody
  counts.
- **The camp minigame is the wraith minigame in a new setting**: not her own memories but other
  people's — sneaking, investigating, reading. It is the first time the mechanic is pointed at
  living protagonists.
- **Her route is the network's.** 08 → 07 by the gate Val opened; 07 → 01 by the ruin gate the
  Ninja came through ([[the-ancient-ruin]], [[the-second-signal]]); 01 → 06 by the enemy gate she
  remembers from life. Every leg is a gate that exists ([[realm-nearness-and-traversal]]). What she
  cannot know is which of them are sealed.
- **Spoiler-safe**: this note does not say how the trial ends, only that she saw it.
