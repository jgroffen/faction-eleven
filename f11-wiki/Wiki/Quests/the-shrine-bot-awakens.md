# The Shrine Bot Awakens

The story event that ties chapter one together. In [[autofix]]'s story, the player gains the
ability to **remotely communicate with their auto-fix bots** — and that signal reaches across the
wheel and **wakes the mini-fix bot in [[ninja]]'s family shrine**.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Location:** [[realm-02]]

## The Beat

For generations the [[institute-of-eight]] have kept an ancestor spirit in a small toy car. It is
really a **mini-fix bot** from [[realm-02]], and it has been training the family — so that one day
it can get home.

In chapter one, [[autofix]] earns remote communication with their bots. But the realms are sealed,
and a sealed realm stops signals as well as travellers ([[allied-faction-signals]]) — so the call
goes nowhere until [[ninja]] loses to the gate guardian and **cracks the gate**
([[the-cracked-gate]]). Through that crack, the two bots reach each other. The shrine bot wakes.

Two factions who have never met, in two realms that the disconnection separated, are connected —
not by a gate, but by a radio signal one of them didn't know it was sending, through a fracture
the other one made by losing.

## Why It Matters

This is the **first cross-realm contact in the game**, and it happens between [[realm-01]] and
[[realm-02]] — **neighbours on [[the-wheel-of-realms]], historically friendly**. The oldest
alliance in the setting is the first one to come back.

It is also the **first worked example of [[protagonist-swapping-and-story-gating]]**, and a
two-sided one: it needs an ability earned in the [[robots]]' story *and* a defeat suffered in the
[[institute-of-eight]]'s. Neither faction alone can produce it. Chapter one therefore demonstrates
the mechanic that defines the game in its strongest possible form — genuine mutual dependency,
not a one-way trigger.

## Objectives

Not yet designed. What is fixed is the *shape*: an ability earned in the Robots' incremental skill
tree ([[robots-incremental-exploration]]) whose payoff lands in someone else's story.

## Open

- Does the shrine bot's waking **unblock** Ninja content the player couldn't otherwise reach, or
  merely **enrich** it? The gating mechanic is stronger if it's the first.
- Does [[autofix]] know the signal was received? The stronger version is that the player knows and
  Autofix doesn't.
- Who "gives" this quest is undefined — it is a story beat inside the Robots' own line rather than
  something an NPC hands over.
- **Ordering within chapter one:** the player must reach the Robots' remote-comms ability *and*
  lose the Ninja's boss fight. Whether the game enforces an order, or the awakening simply fires
  when both are done, is undecided — and it is the first real test of how
  [[protagonist-swapping-and-story-gating]] is implemented.
