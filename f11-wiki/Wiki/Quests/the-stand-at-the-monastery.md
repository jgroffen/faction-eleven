# The Stand At The Monastery

The [[celestials]] stop retreating. Back at [[templar-monastery]] with [[oluja-town]]'s people
behind their walls, [[templar-will]] and [[knight-ermengarda]] fight **waves of [[green-skins]] and
[[the-damned]]** — and the Templar decides that **this is where he answers the war machine**, rather
than falling back a third time. The chapter ends with the machine **in sight and still coming**.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-03]] · **Location:**
[[templar-monastery]]

## Hook

Two positions lost and nowhere left worth going. The monastery is the archive the Templar chose on
purpose ([[templar-monastery]]), the signals have gone out of it
([[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]]), and a town's worth of civilians is now sheltering in it. The
faction that has been **built around losing** picks a place to stop.

## Objectives

1. **Hold the monastery** through waves of green-skin and Damned attackers.
2. **Make the stand.** The Templar commits: no third retreat, and the answer to the machine happens
   here.
3. **See the machine coming.** It is **sighted, not arrived** — the Templar makes his decision
   looking at it on the horizon. That is the chapter's ending; the machine spends the whole of
   [[chapter-04]] closing, and reaches the walls at that chapter's end.

## The Beat

**A second faction in the waves.** Every Celestial battle so far has been green-skins
([[the-fall-of-the-outpost]], [[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]]). Undead in the line is the player's
first evidence, from inside the Celestials' own story, that [[realm-03]] has more than one
invader — and the [[the-guardians-of-night|Guardians]]' intent to push the Damned kingdoms into
this realm becoming visible before anyone in the room can name it.

**They come through the green-skins' realm**, in two legs the player has already been shown from
the other end: [[val]] unsealed [[the-damned]]'s allied gate to the [[green-skins]]
([[the-red-power]], [[sealed-interplane-gates]]), and the Celestials' enemy gate to the green-skins
stands open with its barrier smashed from the inside ([[the-broken-barrier]]). So the undead at
these walls walked through the door a Guardian opened and then through the hole the Guardians made —
**the same two acts the [[gargoyle-granite|Gargoyle]] is investigating a realm away, arriving as
enemy waves in somebody else's tower defence.**

**The decision is the content.** The waves are the tower defence the player already knows; what is
new is that the Templar stops looking for the next fallback. It is also the decision that makes the
rest of the faction's arc possible — the war machine cannot be **captured** by an army that keeps
withdrawing ([[celestials-hero-tower-defence]]).

## Why It Matters

- It **ends [[chapter-03]]** on the machine in sight, which is a cliffhanger the player has been
  promised since chapter one — and one that now pays off a chapter later rather than immediately.
- It puts the monastery **under siege before [[chapter-04]] begins** — besieged by green-skin and
  Damned waves, with the machine closing — which is the state [[conjurer-voisin]] and then the
  Ninja's party arrive into ([[the-answering-of-the-wand]],
  [[the-convergence-at-the-monastery]]).
- **The machine's approach is chapter four's clock.** Everything the Mystic does in realm-04 is
  happening while it walks.
- It is the hinge from defence to offence for the whole faction, and the last Celestial beat
  before help arrives.

## Prerequisites

Follows [[the-evacuation-of-oluja-town]] — the Celestials come here from the town, with its people.

## Open

- Whether the green-skins and the Damned are **allied** in these waves or arriving independently,
  and whether the Celestials can tell.
- Whether the stand is a scene, a decision the player makes, or an objective they can fail.
- What the sheltering townsfolk do to the defence — something to protect, a resource, or scenery.
- Where [[knight-ermengarda]] is when the battle resolves; she leaves to retake [[oluja-town]]
  after it, and the chapter that happens in is not set.
