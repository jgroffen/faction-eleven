# The Trial Of The Last Gargoyle

[[val]] has learned a Gargoyle survived, found him damaged and running on limited power, and hunts
him down herself. Closes [[chapter-02]].

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-02]] · **Giver:** [[val]] ·
**Location:** [[realm-07]]

## Hook

By this point the [[gargoyle]] has traced the broken barrier back to [[the-guardians-of-night]] —
loud enough that Val notices him noticing. She seeks him out to settle it herself, the same way she
settled the original smashing: in person, briefly, and leaving the violence to somebody else.

## Objectives

1. **Val confronts him.** She demands to know what happened to the gargoyles — why they've been
   destroyed. He asks; she claims not to know. **"They're just old."**
2. **The lie doesn't land.** He isn't convinced he's malfunctioning, and he isn't convinced by her
   story either — he watched his own kind get built to outlast "old."
3. **Val changes her argument.** Unable to win it on the facts, she reframes him as a problem: *"You
   are disrupting the war plans of the people you are meant to be guarding, and you must be
   stopped."* — his own instructions, turned around and used against him.
4. **She sets the Minotaur on him and leaves**, not staying to see the outcome and not convinced the
   Minotaur can win.
5. **Boss fight: the Minotaur.** The Gargoyle has no attack — this is
   [[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]'s stealth/stone kit, not a combat class. The fight is entirely
   dodge and defence, run in parallel with the Gargoyle trying to talk the Minotaur out of working
   for Val. He cannot hurt his opponent; he can only survive long enough to be heard.
6. **Resolution.** However the encounter ends mechanically, Val leaves believing she has not settled
   the matter — she resolves to destroy every remaining gargoyle rather than trust this one to be
   put down.

## Rewards

- No item. The reward is narrative: the Guardians of Night are now confirmed and personal, and the
  Gargoyle is left privately doubting himself — see Notes.

## Prerequisites

- [[the-waking-of-the-last-gargoyle]]

## Notes

- **He hides the doubt.** Val's claim doesn't convince him in the room, but it plants something he
  does not show her: after she leaves, he privately begins to doubt he is functioning properly. See
  [[gargoyle]].
- **The Minotaur is the same Minotaur** who helped smash the gargoyles in
  [[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]] — the soldier who did the hammer-work is the one being talked
  out of finishing the job.
- **Val's exit line is not a bluff.** She genuinely isn't sure the Minotaur wins, which is why the
  quest's real stakes — for her — are set up here rather than resolved: she leaves to go destroy the
  other gargoyles herself rather than risk this one surviving a second time.
- **This closes [[chapter-02]].** It is the chapter-scale revelation with a named antagonist that
  the chapter's open ending question was waiting on.
- Sets up [[gargoyle]]'s decision to leave [[realm-07]] with [[ninja]] — not persuasion by another
  protagonist, but his own doubt sending him looking for answers. See the [[gargoyle]] and
  [[ninja]] notes, and the handoff on what follows at the Celestials' gate.
