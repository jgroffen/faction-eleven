# The Unfreezing Of The Kingdom

[[val]] returns to her castle after the trial and **notices the Damned realm is frozen.** As she
questions what is going on, [[the-retcon-engine]] kicks in: a continuity glitch **rewrites her
memory of noticing** — and **unfreezes the game.** The player's kingdom resumes. Closes the
Damned's thread in [[chapter-03]].

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-03]] · **Giver:** [[val]] ·
**Location:** [[val-s-castle]]

## Hook

The kingdom has been frozen since [[wraith-delahaye]] broke out of the recruitment minigame
([[the-recruitment-of-the-wraith]]) — through the whole of her search of the map and her pursuit
of Val to [[realm-07]]. Nothing in [[realm-08]] has moved. Val is the first thing that comes back
into it from outside.

## Objectives

1. **Val comes home.** Her second return to the Damned realm — the first was the arrival Delahaye
   watched from outside the castle ([[the-search-of-the-frozen-map]]); this one is after
   [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]].
2. **She notices.** The world is frozen — a kingdom caught mid-campaign, still there, going nowhere.
   She starts to question what is going on.
3. **The retcon engine answers.** A continuity glitch: her memory of the freeze is rewritten so
   that **she did not notice it** — and the same correction **unfreezes the game.**
4. **Play resumes.** The player is back in their kingdom ([[damned-undead-kingdom-rts]]), as though
   nothing had stopped.

## Rewards

- No item. The kingdom is playable again, and the player has seen the engine cover its own tracks
  in a named antagonist's head.

## Prerequisites

- [[the-trial-of-the-last-gargoyle]]

## Notes

- **A scene, not a level.** Val is not playable; this is the engine acting on an NPC while the
  player watches. Whether it plays as a cutscene on the world map or something the player triggers
  by returning to the RTS is not decided.
- **Two manifestations in one thread.** The freeze was self-correction failing — a Pirate mechanic
  the Damned's game could not resolve. The unfreeze is the other half of the design's breakage:
  [[the-retcon-engine]] forcing history to add up, a **hard-retcon** in the terms of
  [[continuity-glitches]]. The player sees a rule break in chapter two and sees it papered over in
  chapter three.
- **Val is now a character the engine has edited.** She carries a god's power she cannot account
  for, a conviction a system built in her, and a gap where a memory was. None of it shows.
- **The player's kingdom did not lose anything.** The freeze is a pause, not a reset — the map
  Delahaye searched is the map the player gets back.

## Open

- Whether the player sees the glitch land on Val — a flicker, a repeated line, a scene that plays
  twice — or only its result.
- Whether Delahaye's absence from the map is noticed by the engine, the kingdom, or Val.
- What Val's castle is to the RTS once play resumes — one of the rival kingdoms, or outside the
  war ([[val-s-castle]]).
- Whether the kingdom's war has moved on in the fiction while the game was frozen, or the freeze
  was literal for everyone.
