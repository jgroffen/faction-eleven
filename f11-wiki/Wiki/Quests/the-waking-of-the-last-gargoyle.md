# The Waking Of The Last Gargoyle

The [[gargoyle]]'s opening. A bolt of power ends a torpor that should have lasted until the realm
needed him — and he comes to in a room full of his own kind with their heads broken open, unable to
move properly, not knowing why he is awake.

**Status:** design · **Type:** main · **Chapter:** [[chapter-02]] · **Giver:** [[gargoyle]] ·
**Location:** [[realm-09]]

## The Cutscene

Three movements, with the time jumps carrying the weight:

1. **The making.** Necromancers of [[realm-08]] and shamans of [[realm-09]] raise the dead as stone
   and imbue them: realm guardians, defenders only, unable to act except against invasion, unable to
   work against their own faction ([[the-gargoyle-guardians]]). They are dispatched, fight back the
   incursions of the [[celestials]], [[mystics]] and [[fey-folk]], **seal the gateways**
   ([[the-realm-barriers]]), return, and are instructed to rest until needed.
2. **The gargoyles enter stone form. Cut to black.** Fade in on the same room, and much time has
   passed. [[val]] enters hooded, with a minotaur and a giant. She says **"do it!"** and leaves. The
   hammers go along the row. On the last gargoyle **the hammer breaks**, and the head is only
   partly smashed. The two look at each other, shrug, and leave
   ([[the-smashing-of-the-gargoyles]]).
3. **Fade to black, fade in on the same room.** A bolt of power wakes him. Gameplay starts.

**The player is given the answer before the question.** Everything the Gargoyle spends the chapter
working out, the player already watched happen. His story is not a mystery for the audience; it is a
mystery for him, and the tension is watching a reasonable being reason his way toward something we
know.

## Objectives

1. **Move at all.** He can barely move. Nearby are **some of his own fragments**, which restore him
   somewhat ([[gargoyle-fragments]]).
2. **Inspect the others.** Among the smashed gargoyles he finds another fragment, granting the first
   gargoyle power: **stealth without moving**. His head is still visibly damaged.
3. **Get out without being seen.** He does not know why he is awake and concludes he **must not let
   anyone know he is active** until he understands what is happening.
4. **Reach a conclusion.** From the destruction around him he decides **the realm is under threat**
   — correctly, and for entirely the wrong reasons — and sets out to find out how.

## Why This Is A Good Tutorial

It teaches the whole game without a single instruction:

- The **starting kit is a restriction**, and the player earns it in the room rather than being given
  it. Stealth-while-stationary is discovered as a relief, not a limitation.
- **Stealth is motivated before it is used.** He hides because he is frightened and ignorant, not
  because a designer wanted a stealth section.
- **The upgrade fiction is established on the first pickup** — power comes from the dead.
- **The mood is set by the room.** The first thing the player does is search the bodies of the only
  others like him.

## Rewards

- [[gargoyle-fragments]] — mobility, then **stone-form** (stealth while stationary).

## Prerequisites

None — this is where the Gargoyle's story begins.

**Caused by** [[the-broken-barrier]], off-screen and elsewhere in the realm: the barrier's failure
returns the gargoyles' own imbued magic to them as the waking bolt. He does not know this at the
start and the player is only told the general shape of it.

## Notes

- **The bolt is not external.** It is gargoyle magic coming home, not somebody casting a spell on
  him — so there is no benefactor to look for, and the player should not be led to imagine one.
- **Failure here has to be gentle.** The fail state elsewhere is being thrown out of wherever he is
  ([[gargoyle-stone-metroidvania]]); in a room he cannot yet leave, that needs a different shape.
  Unresolved.
- The other smashed gargoyles in this room are the first instance of the pattern that continues
  across the realm — bodies in important places, all headless, some still holding fragments.
