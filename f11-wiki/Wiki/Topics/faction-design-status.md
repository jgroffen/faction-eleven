# Faction Design Status

How settled each of the ten factions is, and how deeply each should be reviewed. Agreed with the
author on 2026-08-08: **do not interview the thin factions into existence.** Design invented under
interview pressure is the weakest material in any wiki — the five unsettled factions are recorded
as unsettled and left alone until there is something real to record.

The three deep factions are exactly **chapter one** ([[chapter-based-release]]), and exactly the
contiguous arc 01–02–03 of [[the-wheel-of-realms]]. **All three are now settled** — chapter one's
design is specified end to end.

Chapter two adds the [[mystics]] ([[realm-04]]) as a major setting **and** the [[green-skins]]
([[realm-09]]) via [[the-second-signal]] — so the arc stops being contiguous. Realm 09 is reached
through an abandoned gate in [[realm-01]], not by walking round the wheel.

**Chapter two needs two more game styles** — [[mystics]] and [[green-skins]] — neither of which is
settled. That is the next design work after the story outline.

## What "pending" means

The author confirmed on 2026-08-08 that **the genre list has moved** since the prototype notes.
Every "pending" row above still carries its prototype game type in
[[faction-genre-mechanics]] — recorded as the last written state, **not as current design**. Do
not build against those rows.

## Concepts

- [[faction-unique-features]] — **none of the ten features are named yet.** This is the largest
  single content gap in the wiki: a system that defines the endgame, with no instances.
