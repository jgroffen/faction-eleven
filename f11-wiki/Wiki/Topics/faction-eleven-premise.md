# Faction Eleven — Premise

Faction Eleven is an open-world exploration computer game in which the player plays through **ten
different stories**, each as the main protagonist of one faction. The ten stories intertwine over
time into a single story, as the protagonists discover the other factions and interact with each
other. An **eleventh faction** is the game's mysterious antagonist, uncovered gradually across the
ten stories.

## Overview

Three structural commitments define the game:

- **Ten protagonists, ten stories.** Each faction has its own main character and its own story
  line. The player plays all of them, not one of them.
- **Convergence.** The stories are separate at first and intertwine over time into a single
  story, driven by the protagonists discovering and interacting with each other.
- **A hidden antagonist.** The eleventh faction is the antagonist. Its goals and motivations are
  not stated up front — the player uncovers them by playing through the factions' stories. See
  [[faction-eleven-antagonist]].

Moment to moment the game is a common exploration style shared by all factions
([[exploration-core-loop]]), with a distinct genre of mechanics layered on per faction
([[faction-genre-mechanics]]).

The stories converge through [[allied-faction-signals]] — devices the old alliances left in each
other's realms, silent since the sealing — rather than through the locked gates.

The world is a **multiverse**: each faction lives in its own realm, with its own mood, feel and
art-style ([[the-multiverse-of-realms]]). The game opens after [[the-long-disconnection]], and
the player braids the stories together by [[protagonist-swapping-and-story-gating]].
