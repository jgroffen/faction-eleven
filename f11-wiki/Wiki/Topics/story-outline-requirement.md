# Story Outline Before Development

**The gate on development:** the high-level outline of the entire story, its key story points, and
the role of each faction in it must exist **before any development starts**.

No code begins until the whole story exists at outline level. This is a project constraint, not a
preference — it sits alongside [[project-scope-and-constraints]] and it overrides the temptation to
start building chapter one just because chapter one is specified.

## Overview

Three deliverables are required, none of which exists in full:

1. **The high-level outline of the entire story** — beginning to end, across all eleven factions.
2. **The key story points** — the beats the whole thing hangs on.
3. **The role of each faction in it** — what all ten are *for*, narratively.

## Why This Is The Right Gate

The design already shows why. [[the-cracked-gate]] only works because the Ninja's failure and the
Robots' ability are authored to meet; [[the-calling-of-ancient-allies]] only works because the
Robots' comms were restored a chapter earlier. **The couplings run across factions and across
chapters**, so an outline built one faction at a time will not hold. And under
[[chapter-based-release]], early chapters ship and become unrevisable while later ones are still
being written — which is exactly the situation in which a missing outline becomes expensive.

## What Exists

| Deliverable | State |
|-------------|-------|
| Chapter one | **specified end to end** — [[chapter-01]] |
| Chapter two | outlined, unbalanced — [[chapter-02]] |
| Chapters three onward | nothing |
| The ending | nothing |
| The Ancients' motive | **nothing** — the answer the whole mystery resolves to is unwritten |
| Roles of the seven unbuilt factions | nothing — see [[faction-design-status]] |

## The Hardest Piece

[[faction-eleven-antagonist]]'s **motive**. It is recorded that the Ancients extended the cycle
"for unknown reasons", which is honest — but the outline cannot be finished without deciding what
those reasons are, because the role of every faction depends on what they are all unknowingly
part of.

## Concepts

- [[faction-design-status]] — which factions have any story at all.
